Brand guide

How X&Y3 looks and sounds. Use these rules on the website, proposals, social posts and trade-show materials.

01 · Logo

The mark

Three stacked layers make a soft-serve swirl. The three layers also stand for the "3" in X&Y3, and the green dot on top is the cherry.

  • 1Violet tile: the platform, solid and premium
  • 2Three white layers: the soft-serve swirl, and the "3"
  • 3Translucent cone: the product
  • 4Green cherry: growth and freshness
Primary · on light backgrounds
Reversed · on dark backgrounds
Mark only · favicon, app icon, social avatar

Clear space: keep empty space around the logo at least the width of the cherry dot × 2. Minimum size: mark 24px, full logo 110px wide.

05 · Voice & usage

How we sound

Confident, practical and partner-first. We sell the client's success, not our own name: the X&Y3 brand stays in the background and the client's brand is the star.

✓ Do

  • Lead with outcomes: revenue, no staff needed, open 24/7
  • Say "your brand" and "your client's brand" often
  • Use real machine photos on white or lavender backgrounds
  • Keep green for highlights and key actions

✗ Don't

  • Show supplier logos or model codes on public materials
  • Stretch, recolor or rotate the logo
  • Put the gradient behind long body text
  • Use more than one green element per small area
