How Do Ice Cream Vending Machines Ensure Food Safety?

Consumers care much about ice cream food safety in vending machines. To ensure it, three key aspects matter. The dual-zone temp control system keeps freezing chambers at -18℃ to -22℃ and pickup areas below 5℃, with 10-minute inspections and compensation for ±2℃ fluctuations. Hygienic design includes magnetic sealed doors, 304 stainless steel trays, 15° delivery channels, and 72-hour disinfection.
