Why Choose HUAXIN Ice Cream Sundae Vending Machine?
As a global leader with 13 years of industry experience and 70% domestic market share, HUAXIN’s automatic ice cream sundae vending machine is designed to solve core pain points for businesses and delight customers.
1. Conquer Space Anxiety
With a compact 0.91㎡ footprint, this machine fits perfectly on bar counters or small spaces in malls, airports, and schools. No assembly or renovation needed—just plug and play. Equipped with 4 adjustable casters, one person can easily move it, maximizing your usable area.
2. Free Up Labor Costs
Traditional ice cream service takes 2 minutes per cup and requires constant staff involvement (hand washing, cup fetching, cleaning). HUAXIN’s fully automatic system completes the entire process from selection to payment to dispensing in just 15-30 seconds, no manual intervention required. One person can manage 10 machines, slashing labor expenses.
3. Boost Impulse Purchases
Long lines make 30% of potential customers abandon their purchase. Our 15s fast dispense and transparent production window create visual appeal, driving spontaneous buys. 95% of users share their experience on social media, bringing 3-5 secondary purchases per post.
4. 59 Flavors for Maximum Profit
Mix 1 base milkshake, 3 toppings (e.g., mochi balls) and 3 sauces to create 59 unique flavors. Our exclusive cheese flavor (same quality as DQ but 1/3 cheaper) boosts repeat purchases by 30%, while milk tea-style toppings increase repurchase rate by 25%. With a cost per cup of $0.24-$0.29 and selling price of $0.96-$1.6, gross profit margin exceeds 65%. High-traffic locations generate $1,920 monthly net profit, leading to 3-4 month ROI.
5. Smart & Reliable Technology
Core Components: Embraco compressor (8+ year lifespan), German imported cooling fan, Omron sensors for precise operation.
Master OS Smart System: Remote monitoring via App (temperature, inventory, sales data), one-click cleaning, night mode for energy savings, and automatic profit sharing with partners.
Hygiene & Durability: UV + pasteurization, 2-6℃ cylinder antibacterial function, IP54 protection, and international certifications (CE, ETL, RoHS, CB). 98% of faults can be resolved remotely.
