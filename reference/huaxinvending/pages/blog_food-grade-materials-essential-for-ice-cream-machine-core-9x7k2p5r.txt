Why must the core of an ice cream vending machine be made of food-grade materials?
Why must the core of an ice cream vending machine be made of food-grade materials?
Date：2025-08-15 Author：Huaxin

The 304 stainless steel food-grade large-capacity material tank is a core component of ice cream vending machines, ensuring product quality and operational efficiency. Its corrosion resistance (compliant with GB 4806.9 standards) prevents contamination, while the 15-20L capacity reduces refilling frequency by 40%. Easy-to-clean properties lower maintenance costs and extend equipment lifespan, balancing hygiene and operational stability for long-term efficiency.

In the core components of ice cream vending machines, the material and capacity design of material tanks directly affect product quality and operational smoothness. Deterioration of milk slurry and improper cleaning can disrupt system operation; if material tanks have hidden material risks or insufficient capacity, they may cause raw material contamination, frequent replenishment, and other issues, thereby affecting the stable service of the equipment. 304 stainless steel food-grade large-capacity material tanks, through the integrated design of material safety, large-capacity adaptability, and durability, have become the key to balancing food hygiene and operational efficiency. Understanding their technical characteristics and practical value is of great significance for equipment selection and daily operations.


Food-Grade Properties of 304 Stainless Steel: Fundamental Guarantee for Avoiding "Material Risks"

As the preferred material for food contact, 304 stainless steel’s core advantages lie in compositional stability and safety compliance. This type of steel contains 18% chromium and 8% nickel, forming a dense oxide film that can effectively resist corrosion from milk fat, sugar, and acidic additives (such as fruit jam) in ice cream raw materials. It also complies with GB 4806.9-2016 National Food Safety Standard for Metal Materials and Products in Contact with Food.

Comparative tests show that after holding acidic ice cream raw materials (pH 4.0-5.0) for 72 consecutive hours, the heavy metal leaching amount (lead, chromium) from 304 stainless steel tanks is below 0.01mg/kg, far lower than the national standard limit. In contrast, under the same conditions, the leaching amount from ordinary 201 stainless steel tanks is 5-8 times that of 304 stainless steel, which may affect the flavor and safety of raw materials with long-term use. This material property fundamentally avoids "material risks" and provides a basic guarantee for the food hygiene of ice cream.

Operational Adaptability of Large-Capacity Design: Practical Value in Reducing "Replenishment Interruptions"


The "largeness" of 304 stainless steel food-grade large-capacity material tanks is not simply an increase in size, but capacity optimization based on operational scenarios. The capacity of mainstream equipment’s material tanks is mostly designed to be 15-20L, which can meet the production needs of 30-50 servings of ice cream with a single replenishment, reducing the replenishment frequency by about 40% compared to traditional 10L small-capacity tanks.

Operational data from a shopping mall location shows that equipment equipped with 20L large tanks extends the replenishment interval from 1 day to 1.5-2 days on weekdays, and can support continuous serving for 8 hours during peak weekends, reducing "service interruptions" caused by untimely replenishment. This design is particularly suitable for crowded scenarios, allowing operators to avoid frequent manpower deployment and indirectly reducing labor costs — calculated at a labor cost of 50 yuan per replenishment, a single device can save about 600 yuan per month.

Corrosion Resistance and Easy-to-Clean Performance: Long-Term Advantages in Reducing "


Maintenance Failures"The corrosion resistance of 304 stainless steel also manifests in simplifying maintenance processes and reducing the risk of "maintenance failures." Long-term adhesion of residual ice cream raw materials can easily breed bacteria or cause corrosion of the tank inner wall. However, 304 stainless steel has a smooth surface and strong chemical resistance; daily cleaning only requires wiping with neutral detergent without special maintenance. Even sticky residues such as jam can be easily removed with 80℃ hot water rinsing.

Data shows that the average maintenance cycle of 304 stainless steel tanks is 30% longer than that of ordinary stainless steel tanks, and the replacement frequency due to corrosion is reduced by more than 50%. According to feedback from Huaxin Technology, after one year of operation, 304 stainless steel tanks in the same batch of equipment still maintain smooth, rust-free inner walls, while 30% of ordinary tanks have developed local corrosion spots. This durability not only extends the equipment’s service life but also reduces "operational failures" caused by improper maintenance.

The design of 304 stainless steel food-grade large-capacity material tanks may seem like an optimization of a single component, but it is actually the key to connecting food safety and operational efficiency. From material safety complying with national standards, to scenario-adaptable large-capacity design, and to maintenance convenience brought by corrosion resistance, every feature revolves around the core goal of "reducing risks and improving stability." For operators, choosing equipment with such tanks is not only a guarantee of food hygiene but also a reasonable plan for long-term operating costs and service quality.
