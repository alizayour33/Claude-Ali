Turn Empty Commercial Space into a Soft Serve Revenue Point

Many European commercial spaces have small unused areas near entrances, corridors, waiting zones, family areas, and checkout points. These spaces may not be large enough for a full dessert shop, but they can become profitable vending points when equipped with a fully automatic ice cream vending machine.

Huaxin Max Series is designed for this kind of opportunity. It helps operators sell fresh soft serve ice cream through automatic ordering, payment, production, and dispensing, while remote monitoring allows the business owner to track sales, machine status, ingredients, cups, and alerts without staying on site all day.

Why This Business Model Works
Small Space, Clear Revenue Use

The machine uses a compact commercial footprint and can turn underused areas into self-service dessert points.

Impulse Purchase Product

Fresh soft serve ice cream has strong visual appeal, especially in family, tourism, shopping, and leisure environments.

Lower Daily Labor Pressure

Customers can order, pay, and receive ice cream automatically, reducing the need for dedicated serving staff.

Data-Driven Expansion

Operators can compare sales data across locations and expand only the points that prove profitable.

Four Business Models for European Partners
Business Model	How It Works	Best For
Self-Operation Model	The owner buys machines, places them in selected locations, manages refilling, and keeps sales revenue.	Vending operators, investors, and automated retail startups.
Revenue-Sharing Model	The machine operator cooperates with a mall, station, park, or property owner and shares revenue based on agreement.	Location partners with high traffic but no desire to operate foodservice directly.
Dealer Sales Model	A distributor sells machines locally and provides setup, spare parts, service, and operation guidance.	Equipment dealers, vending machine distributors, and local service companies.
Hybrid Operation Model	A partner sells machines while also operating several demonstration or revenue-generating machines.	Dealers who want both equipment profit and long-term operating income.

Operator Playbook: How to Start and Scale
1. Choose Test Sites

Start with different traffic types: mall, station, school, tourist area, supermarket, or entertainment venue.

2. Localize the Offer

Customize payment, language, screen content, pricing, machine wrap, and flavor combinations for local buyers.

3. Track Machine Data

Use remote monitoring to compare sales volume, refill timing, peak hours, and machine status across locations.

4. Expand Proven Locations

Keep high-performing locations, adjust weak points, and build a repeatable vending route or partner network.

Frequently Asked Questions
What makes an ice cream vending machine a good business opportunity?

It allows operators to sell fresh soft serve ice cream in high-traffic locations with automatic ordering, payment, production, and remote monitoring. This makes it easier to test, manage, and scale than a traditional dessert shop.

Why choose the Max Series for this business?

The Max Series offers 20L mix capacity, 160-cup storage, about 160–180 cup output after filling, 15–20 second serving, cashless payment support, remote monitoring, and custom branding, making it suitable for commercial locations.

Which locations are suitable for this business?

Shopping malls, airports, train stations, schools, supermarkets, tourist attractions, theme parks, cinemas, pedestrian streets, and family entertainment centers are suitable options.

Can this business be operated without staff at the machine?

Yes. The machine supports automatic customer ordering, payment, and production. Operators still need to arrange refilling, cleaning, basic checking, and maintenance planning.

Does the machine support cashless payment?

Yes. The Max Series can support credit card payment, QR payment, coin, bill, and customized payment systems according to local market needs.

Can I monitor several machines remotely?

Yes. Operators can remotely monitor sales data, machine status, ingredient level, cup status, revenue reports, temperature, alerts, and operation settings.

Can the machine be customized for my local brand?

Yes. Huaxin supports customization for machine appearance, logo, voltage, payment system, language, UI, screen content, and branding.

How should I calculate the ROI?

ROI should be calculated using local selling price, daily cup sales, ingredient cost, cup and topping cost, electricity, site fee, refill labor, maintenance, seasonality, and machine uptime. Huaxin can help build a customized estimate based on your target market.
