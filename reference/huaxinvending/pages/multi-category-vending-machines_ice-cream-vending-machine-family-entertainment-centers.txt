Product Description.

Add a Convenient Self-Service Treat to Your Entertainment Venue

Make ice cream part of the guest experience with Huaxin automatic ice cream vending machines. Designed for commercial self-service applications, the B86 Max, B85, and B84 can be considered for arcades, family entertainment centers, family parks, cinema areas, and other leisure venues.

With automated ice cream preparation, a large touchscreen, and configurable payment integration, Huaxin machines offer operators another way to serve guests during their visit.

A New Self-Service Option for Entertainment Venues

Family entertainment centers bring together games, activities, dining, and social experiences. An ice cream vending machine can complement these offerings by providing an additional food and beverage option in suitable customer areas.

Make Ice Cream More Visible

The machine's touchscreen and visible preparation area can help present ice cream as an accessible treat within your venue. Place it where guests can notice it naturally, such as a rest area, food zone, or waiting space.

Serve Guests at Their Own Pace

Self-service ordering and automated preparation provide an alternative to a conventional staffed serving counter. The actual operating workflow depends on the machine configuration, payment integration, and venue procedures.

Complement Family and Group Visits

Ice cream can be an additional purchase option for families, friends, and other groups visiting your entertainment venue. It can complement existing activities without requiring a dedicated ice cream counter.

Plan for Busy Periods

Weekends, school holidays, and special events may create different demand patterns. Machine capacity, ingredient replenishment, cup availability, cleaning, and routine inspection should be considered when planning for peak periods.

Adapt the Machine to Your Venue

Exterior branding and selected interface or payment options can be discussed according to project requirements. This helps operators plan equipment that fits their venue identity and customer purchasing process.

Choose a Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine for Your Venue

Huaxin B86 Max, B85, and B84 belong to the same B-series automatic ice cream vending platform. Their core functions and internal configuration are the same, while their exterior and door styling differ.

Choose the model appearance that best suits your venue layout and visual identity, then confirm the configuration required for your market and operating plan.

Huaxin B86 Max

A full-size automatic ice cream vending machine option for entertainment venues seeking a prominent self-service installation. Consider it for suitable food zones, leisure areas, or other locations with sufficient floor space and service access.

Huaxin B85

A B-series option with a different exterior design. It shares the platform's core functions, making it suitable for projects where the preferred machine appearance is an important part of the equipment selection.

Huaxin B84

Another exterior design within the same B-series platform. It can be evaluated for entertainment venues that need to coordinate vending equipment with their existing interior design and customer areas.
