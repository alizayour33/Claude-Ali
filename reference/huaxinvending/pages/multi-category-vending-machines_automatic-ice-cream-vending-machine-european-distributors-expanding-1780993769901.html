Frequently Asked Questions

Why should vending machine distributors add ice cream vending machines?

Ice cream vending helps distributors offer a more visual and higher-value automated retail category beyond snacks, drinks, and coffee. It is suitable for operators and locations looking for fresh dessert sales with lower labor pressure.

What customers can European distributors target?

Distributors can target vending operators, shopping malls, airports, train stations, supermarkets, retail chains, campuses, cinemas, theme parks, tourist attractions, and automated retail investors.

What is the capacity of the Max Series?

The Max Series has a 20L mix cylinder, 160-cup storage capacity, and about 160–180 cup output after filling, depending on product settings and serving size.

Does the machine support cashless payment?

Yes. The Max Series can support credit card payment, QR payment, coin, bill, and customized payment systems according to local market requirements.

Can the machine be customized for a local distributor brand?

Yes. Huaxin supports OEM/ODM customization for machine appearance, logo, screen content, language, voltage, payment system, UI, and machine wrap.

Can operators manage several machines remotely?

Yes. Operators can remotely monitor sales data, machine status, ingredient level, cup status, revenue reports, temperature, alerts, and operation settings.

What support does Huaxin provide to vending machine distributors?

Huaxin provides customization support, remote technical guidance, operation training, spare parts availability, payment configuration support, and one-year machine warranty.

How can distributors sell this product more effectively?

Distributors should sell the Max Series as a complete automated dessert vending solution. The strongest sales points are high capacity, remote monitoring, cashless payment, low labor operation, custom branding, spare parts support, and scalable multi-location deployment.
