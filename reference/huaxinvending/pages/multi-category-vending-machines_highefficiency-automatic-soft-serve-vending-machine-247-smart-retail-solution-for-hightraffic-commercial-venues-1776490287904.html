Overcoming Traditional Challenges

Traditional ice cream businesses face rising costs and operational hurdles. Our solution solves:

  • Zero Labor Dependency: Eliminates the need for staffing, hand-washing, and manual serving, saving at least 2 minutes per transaction.
  • Space Efficiency: The ultra-compact footprint (less than 1m²) allows placement in "gold-standard" high-traffic areas where traditional stores cannot fit.
  • Automated Hygiene: Features a built-in UV + Pasteurization system and one-click self-cleaning, removing the "cleaning disaster" of daily manual disassembly.
