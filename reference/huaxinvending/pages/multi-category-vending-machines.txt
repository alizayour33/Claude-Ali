Ice Cream Vending Machine UAE for Malls, Attractions and Premium Retail Locations

Huaxin’s B86 Max, B84, B85 and B86 offer UAE operators several ways to build a self-service frozen dessert point. The machines suit projects in Dubai and Abu Dhabi shopping malls, family entertainment centres, indoor areas at attractions and other managed retail locations. Customers order on a touchscreen, pay through the configured payment system and collect a freshly prepared dessert. Operators can review machine information remotely, while exterior graphics and customer-facing content can be adapted to the project. Model specifications, local payment compatibility and installation conditions should be confirmed before ordering. For semi-outdoor sites, Huaxin must review the exact environment and supplied configuration; standard outdoor, all-weather operation should not be assumed.
