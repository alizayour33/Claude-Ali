The Fully Automatic Ice Cream Machine model Easy 7 is designed for commercial use, offering high efficiency and ease of operation. With a compact design (660mm x 334mm x 622mm) and a weight of 80.5kg, it produces 60g servings in just 20 seconds. Featuring an 11.6-inch 4K touchscreen, intelligent robotic arm, and rapid cooling technology, it ensures consistent, high-quality ice cream with minimal maintenance. Ideal for cafes, restaurants, and retail spaces, it supports remote management and multiple dessert options, including ice cream, smoothies, and milkshakes. This machine combines advanced technology with user-friendly operation to maximize productivity and profitability.
