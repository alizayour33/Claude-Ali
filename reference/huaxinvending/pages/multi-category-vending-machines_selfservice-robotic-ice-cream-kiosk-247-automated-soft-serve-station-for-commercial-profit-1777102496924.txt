Overcoming Retail Bottlenecks with Automation
The High Labor Crisis

Traditional ice cream service requires constant staffing for scooping, cleaning, and transactions. Huaxin kiosks automate the entire lifecycle—selection, payment, production, and self-cleaning—effectively reducing labor costs to zero.

The Sanitation Challenge

Manual cleaning is time-consuming and prone to human error. Our integrated UV + Pasteurization system keeps ingredients fresh in refrigerated hoppers (2-6°C), allowing for long-interval maintenance and superior food safety compliance.

Q: What is the maximum daily cup capacity for the B83max?

A: The B83max features a 20L liquid hopper and a 160-cup dispenser. In high-demand scenarios, it can produce 160-180 cups before requiring an ingredient refill.

Q: Does the system integrate with international cashless payments?

A: Yes. We support full integration with Nayax, Ingenico, and regional payment gateways, supporting Credit Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and localized QR systems across 33+ countries.

Q: How does the machine perform in high-humidity tropical climates?
