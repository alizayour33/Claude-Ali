FAQ
Common Questions from Dealers and Commercial Buyers
What type of customer is this machine best for?

It is suitable for vending machine dealers, regional distributors, commercial operators, shopping mall businesses, entertainment centers, cinemas, airports, campuses, tourist attractions, and retail stores.

Can the machine run without staff?

Yes. The machine is designed for self-service ordering, payment, automatic production, and customer pickup. Operators still need to refill ingredients, check hygiene, and perform routine maintenance.

Does the machine support remote monitoring?

Yes. Operators can remotely check sales data, machine status, material levels, operating conditions, and alerts. This is especially useful for multi-location operators.

What payment systems are supported?

The machine can support QR code payment, card payment, coin, bill, and customized payment systems depending on local market requirements.

Can the machine be customized for my market?

Yes. Huaxin supports customization for appearance, voltage, language, payment system, branding, and certain project requirements. OEM/ODM cooperation is also available.

Do you support dealers?

Yes. Huaxin supports dealer cooperation with product information, technical guidance, customization options, remote support, spare parts supply, and commercial project advice.
