A smart ice cream vending machine changes this model by turning the full purchasing process into an automated workflow.

Self-Service Ordering

Integrated Payment

Automatic Production

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is this machine suitable for distributors?

A: Yes. The machine is suitable for distributors who want to sell automatic ice cream vending machines, support local operators, or develop a multi-location vending business model.

Q: Can I customize the machine for my country?

A: Yes. Voltage, payment system, language, exterior appearance, logo, advertising screen content, and operating system settings can be customized according to market requirements.

Q: What payment methods can the machine support?

A: The machine can support cash, coins, credit cards, QR code payments, and customized payment systems depending on the target country and distributor requirements.

Q: How long does it take to make one ice cream?

A: The machine can usually produce one cup in about 15 to 20 seconds, depending on model configuration, recipe, and operating settings.

Q: Does the machine need staff to operate?

A: The machine is designed for self-service vending. Staff are mainly needed for refilling ingredients, cleaning, inspection, and basic maintenance.

Q: Can operators manage several machines remotely?

A: Yes. The remote management system helps operators check sales data, machine status, temperature, ingredient status, revenue reports, and fault alerts.

Q: What locations are suitable for this vending machine?

A: Suitable locations include shopping malls, amusement parks, campuses, airports, railway stations, cinemas, tourist attractions, parks, commercial streets, and other high-traffic areas.

Q: What is the minimum order quantity?

A: One unit can be ordered. For distributors, bulk buyers, and regional partners, machine configuration and pricing can be discussed according to order quantity and customization requirements.

Q: Do you provide after-sales service?

A: Yes. We provide online remote guidance, video technical support, spare parts availability, operation training, and a one-year whole-machine warranty.
