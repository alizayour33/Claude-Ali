Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How many flavors can the machine produce?
A: By combining 1 liquid base with 3 types of jams and 3 types of toppings, you can offer up to 59 unique flavor combinations.

Q: How long does it take to dispense one cup?
A: The entire process from payment to dispensing takes approximately 15 to 20 seconds.

Q: Is the machine easy to clean?
A: Yes. The machine includes a one-click automated self-cleaning function. We recommend a full cleaning cycle once or twice a week.

Q: Can the machine be customized for my country?
A: Absolutely. We offer professional customization for appearance (stickers/colors), local voltage, payment systems, and language support (English, German, French, etc.).

Q: Do I need a constant internet connection?
A: The machine can operate offline for sales. However, an internet connection is required to utilize remote monitoring and real-time data features.
