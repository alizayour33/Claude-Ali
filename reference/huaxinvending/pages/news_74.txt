Keys to Profitable Ice Cream Vending Machine Operations
First of all, operators choose ice cream vending machines with the features of high quality and strong durability. Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machines can fulfill this condition because the company has been committed to adopting the parts of international famous brands and selecting the best machine parts after hundreds of tests. Huaxin ice cream vending machine has a long-lasting and stable freezing power technology. The marathon freezing technology guarantees that the machine will continuously and stably make ice cream without freezing cylinder. This 24-hour ice cream vending machine can be switched to night mode at regular intervals, which also saves electricity. This is undoubtedly a great joy for every businessman. The high puffing technology developed by the company makes the Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine use less ingredients than the traditional ice cream vending machine. Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine focuses on operators' interest and greatly reduces their investment cost. At the same time, the design of Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine aims to attract more customers. The 32-inch touchscreen and visible window of the product can get more customers.
 
Secondly, operators need to make small changes to the traditional business model. The profit logic of ice cream vending machines is not just “buy low fee, sell high price”, but a multi-layer value system. Operators can take advantage of the product price difference and efficiency of making ice cream. This is the most basic profit idea of Huaxin ice cream vending machine. In China, the price difference between the cost of a single cup of ice cream and the ordinary selling price reaches 6 to 8 yuan, and the gross profit margin has reached 70%. The unmanned vending model saves labor costs. Traditional stores need 2 or 3 clerks. The monthly labor cost is a considerable amount. Huaxin ice cream vending Machine only needs to refill ice cream materials from 1 to 2 times per week, the labor cost is negligible. Huaxin self service ice cream vending Machine can remotely check the ice cream sales through your mobile phone. Operators can analyze the hot selling time and flavors of ice cream according to the sales data to help brands optimize their marketing strategies. Huaxin ice cream vending machine can provide authorized customized packaging, and the popular stickers can bring their own social communication attributes.
 
Finally, the screen and stickers of the ice cream vending machine can be implanted with brand advertisements. This convenient way of publicizing can better attract customers. At the same time, operators can also set up marketing activities through the remote control software. Through the “scan to collect coupons”, operators can guide users to join the brand Mini program and accumulate customers who have willingness of repurchasing. Ice cream vending machines support the settings of promotion activities, liking “buy two get one free”, “second cup half price”
