The Venditaria Vending Exhibition was held in Milan, Italy, attracting a large number of technology enthusiasts and industry experts eager to witness the automation field. The event was held at the Milan International Exhibition Centre, and Huaxin Technology Intelligent Vending Company (formerly Liujia Technology) participated in the exhibition as an industry representative to discuss the progress and development trends in the field of vending.


 
Known for its cutting-edge approach to automation, Huaxin Technology highlighted the company's most market-competitive products at the show, including the ice cream vending machine and the newly launched chocolate bean vending machine, which combines artificial intelligence, machine learning, and sustainable development technologies. These two products demonstrate both the company's technological R&D strength and its unique insights in the vending machine field.
 
The show provided a platform for Huaxin to exchange technologies, where we held discussions with potential partners, customers and other stakeholders to gain insights into its emerging trends and technologies in the vending space. Meanwhile, visitors of Huaxin's booth were able to experience the process of making ice cream and chocolate beans, as well as learn about the operation model of the ice cream vending machine and Huaxin's brand story.
 
We are proud to be at the forefront of this dynamic industry, and the feedback and connections we make here will shape our strategy and product offerings in the coming months, as Huaxin looks forward to connecting with other industries and driving the market forward.


References / Sources

Author's Introduction: Huaxin Co., Ltd. has manufactured commercial automated ice cream vending machines in Dezhou, China, since 2013, with machines shipped to more than 50 countries and regions. Each unit completes a 72-hour stress test before shipment, and certifications such as CE, IECEE CB, NSF/ANSI 6 and ETL vary by model.
