Product Overview

The High Expansion Rate Ice Cream Automatic Selling Machine by Huaxin Technology is the ultimate solution for modern, unmanned ice cream retail. Designed for efficiency, hygiene, and profitability, this fully automated machine combines cutting-edge technology with user-friendly operation to deliver a seamless ice cream vending experience.

With a 43% expansion rate, our machine produces creamy, high-quality ice cream with zero ice crystals — rivaling top brands like KFC and DQ. It supports up to 59 flavor combinations using one base milk syrup, three jams, and three toppings, offering endless customization to meet diverse consumer preferences.

Ideal for high-traffic locations such as shopping malls, amusement parks, transit hubs, and office lobbies, this machine operates 24/7 with minimal human intervention.

Frequently Asked Questions
Q: What is the expansion rate of this ice cream machine?

A: The machine features a 43% high expansion rate, ensuring smooth, creamy texture without ice crystals — comparable to premium commercial soft-serve standards.

Q: How many flavors can it make?

A: It can create 59 different flavor combinations using one base milk syrup, three fruit jams, and three dry toppings.

Q: How long does it take to make one cup of ice cream?

A: From order to dispense, it takes only 15 seconds.

Q: What payment methods are supported?

A: It supports coins, bills, credit cards, QR code payments, and is compatible with MDB protocol devices.

Q: Is the machine suitable for outdoor or high-humidity environments?

A: Yes. It has an IP54-rated cooling fan and is built to withstand dust and humid conditions.

Q: Can I monitor the machine remotely?

A: Yes. The machine connects to a cloud platform where you can track sales, inventory, machine status, and even adjust settings remotely.

Q: What certifications does the machine have?

A: It is certified with CE, ETL, RoHS, and ETL, ensuring compliance with international safety and quality standards.

Q: How often does it need cleaning?

A: The self-cleaning function is recommended 1–2 times per week, each cycle taking about 15 minutes.

Q: What is the power consumption?

A: It consumes approximately 10–15 kWh per day, with an energy-saving night mode to reduce costs.

Q: Do you offer customization?

A: Yes. We support customizations in appearance, voltage, payment systems, language options, and more.

Q: What is the warranty and after-sales support?

A: The machine comes with a 1-year warranty, 24/7 remote video support, and modular spare parts for easy replacement.

Q: Can it operate without an internet connection?

A: Yes. The machine can function normally offline, though internet is required for remote features.
