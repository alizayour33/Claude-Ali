During May Day, the temperature gradually rises, and the demand for ice cream and other cold beverages increases dramatically. With the arrival of the May Day holiday, the weather turns warm, eating ice cream has become the first choice of people to quench their thirst and enjoy the delicious flavor of ice cream, the ice cream market ushered in the peak season of sales. As a convenient access to ice cream channels, the ice cream Maker for sale will inevitably be warmly sought after by consumers.

The soft serve ice cream vending machine placed quality is the first step of “traffic harvesting”.From the consumption scene, people’s willingness to travel during the May Day holiday is strong. These places have more people’s flow, where are tourist attractions, commercial centers, transportation hubs. In popular tourist attractions, tourists are prone to thirst and fatigue during the tour, and the emergence of ice cream vending machines allows them to quickly buy ice cream anytime, anywhere to satisfy their appetite. In commercial centers, people are also attracted by the sweetness of ice cream when shopping and stop to buy it at any time. Transportation hubs such as high-speed rail stations, bus stations and other places, passing through a large number of travelers, waiting for the time to take the bus, ice cream vending machine has become a good choice to pass the time and enjoy the food. According to relevant statistics, during the May Day holiday last year, the average daily sales of vending ice cream machines in a scenic spot reached 300 cups, with very substantial sales; the daily sales of vending machines in commercial centers can also be stable at about 200 cups, fully demonstrating the strong money-sucking ability of ice cream vending machines in these scenarios during the May Day holiday.
