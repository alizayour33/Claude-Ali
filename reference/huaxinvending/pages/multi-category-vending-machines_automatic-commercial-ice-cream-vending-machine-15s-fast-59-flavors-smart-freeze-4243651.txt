Product Description.


Product Overview

This smart automatic commercial ice cream vending machine is designed for unmanned 24-hour retail, with 13 years of R&D experience, 11 iterations, and multiple international certifications. It solves traditional machine pain points such as large space occupation, high labor cost, low output and difficult cleaning, providing a profitable automatic ice cream solution for commercial scenes.

Core Advantages

1. Ultra Fast Cooling & Output: Adopts Embraco variable frequency compressor, 5 seconds fast cooling, 15 seconds fast cup output, 12 hours continuous output 1373 cups, no frozen cylinder.

2. Space Saving: Only 0.91㎡ floor area, 320KG with movable casters, plug-and-play without installation and transformation.

3. Multiple Flavors: 1 base milk slurry with 3 jams and 3 toppings, combining 59 flavors, expansion rate 43% without ice residue.

4. Intelligent System: Master OS system supports APP remote monitoring, price setting, data statistics, one-key self-cleaning, fault self-detection, 95% problems solved remotely.

5. Sanitary & Safe: UV + pasteurization, 2-6℃ low temperature antibacterial, closed discharge, no contact production.

Pain Points Solution

- Space Anxiety: Mini body fits bar and limited space.

- Labor Cost: Unmanned operation, no manual service needed.

- Low Conversion: 15s fast output improves impulse consumption.

- Low Efficiency: Monthly output much higher than traditional machines.

- Cleaning Trouble: One-key self-cleaning, no daily disassembly washing.
