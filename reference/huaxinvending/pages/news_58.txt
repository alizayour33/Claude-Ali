Automatic Retail Expansion: Huaxin Tech's Unmanned Machines Gain Attention in European and American Markets

——Chinese "Smart Manufacturing" Leads Global Unmanned Retail Trends

As the global automatic retail market continues to grow, Huaxin Tech is speeding up its expansion into European and American markets with its self-developed ice cream vending machines, catching the eye of the industry. As a leader in China’s automatic retail sector, Huaxin Tech is injecting new energy into smart retail through innovative tech, a diverse product lineup, and a global strategy.

Product Lineup: From Ice Cream to Entertainment, Covering Multiple Scenarios

 

In recent years, Huaxin Tech has showcased its core products at international exhibitions like the 2024 Singapore International Food Equipment Expo and the Asia Amusement & Attractions Expo. Highlights include:

 

Little Snowflake Ice Cream Vending Machine: This star product has already been exported to 32 countries and regions, covering over 4,000 locations. Its quick-freeze system, multiple payment options (cash, mobile payments, etc.), and smart inventory management make it a hit in supermarkets and amusement parks across Europe and America.

The company also launched new products like the tabletop ice cream machine and the 9th-gen smart ice cream machine (featuring remote control and one-touch cleaning), further solidifying its tech edge in the food retail sector.

 

Tech Breakthroughs: Smart + Sustainable

Huaxin Tech’s strength lies in its in-house R&D:

Its ice cream machines allow remote management via smartphones for real-time monitoring, inventory checks, and promotions, slashing maintenance costs.

Energy-efficient cooling tech and low-power components align with Europe and America’s sustainability demands.

 

At events like the 2024 Singapore Food Equipment Expo and the GTI Guangzhou Expo, Huaxin Tech not only attracted over 23,000 professional buyers but also secured partnerships with European and American distributors. Company executives noted that the “unmanned + entertainment” retail model is gaining traction in high-traffic areas like transportation hubs and theme parks.

 

Future Plans:

Tailor products to Western preferences (e.g., healthier low-sugar options, local payment integrations).

Boost IoT R&D for better user behavior analysis.

Keep pushing tech-driven innovation to deliver smarter, more convenient retail experiences globally.

Huaxin Tech aims to win global trust through quality and creativity, proving that “Made in China” can lead the future of smart retail.

References / Sources

Author's Introduction: Huaxin Co., Ltd. has manufactured commercial automated ice cream vending machines in Dezhou, China, since 2013, with machines shipped to more than 50 countries and regions. Each unit completes a 72-hour stress test before shipment, and certifications such as CE, IECEE CB, NSF/ANSI 6 and ETL vary by model.
