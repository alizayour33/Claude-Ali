Product Description.

Self-Service Ice Cream for Shopping Mall Retail Points

A shopping mall ice cream vending machine gives shoppers a place to order and collect freshly prepared soft serve without staff preparing each purchase. Huaxin’s B84, B85, B86 Max and B83 Max combine touchscreen ordering, automated production and a viewing window in a floor-standing commercial format.

For mall operators, dessert project investors, location operators and distributors, these models offer a way to establish an automated dessert point within an approved retail area. Positions near escalators, cinema foyers, children’s areas and food courts can be evaluated against customer visibility, stopping space, utility access and daily servicing requirements.

Choose a Position Where Shoppers Can Stop and Buy

The total number of visitors entering a mall does not establish demand at an individual machine. Evaluate the exact position: who passes it, whether they can see the menu and price, and whether they have room and time to complete a purchase.

Make the Purchase Easy to Understand

Customers select a dessert on the touchscreen, complete payment through the configured system and collect the prepared cup from the pickup opening. The production viewing window lets them watch the automated preparation process.

The 32-inch touchscreen can display the ordering interface and configured branded content. Exterior graphics can carry your logo, colors and dessert concept. For a mall installation, prioritize a readable menu, clear prices and collection instructions that customers can understand from the approaching walkway.

Submit the exterior artwork and screen content for mall approval. Keep the production viewing window distinct from the pickup opening in both graphics and operating instructions.

Configure Payment for the Destination Market

For projects in the United States, Europe, Southeast Asia and the Middle East, payment integration must be reviewed for the specific country and operating arrangement.

Card, QR and mobile payment options depend on the local PSP or payment provider, terminal, interface compatibility and machine configuration. Provide the preferred provider and terminal details before ordering so the required methods can be assessed.

Multilingual interface adaptation can also be discussed for the target market. Confirm the language, menu wording and display layout during project configuration.

Build the Menu Around One Base Mix

The B Series uses one main base mix at a time. Compatible sauces and toppings can create different menu combinations around that base; the machine should not be presented as running several independent ice cream base flavors simultaneously.

For a mall dessert concept, confirm the recipe, portion size, compatible cups and any sauce or topping configuration before preparing the final menu. A focused menu can make touchscreen selection easier and simplify replenishment planning.

Calculate ingredient and packaging cost using the actual serving recipe. Include base mix, sauces, toppings, cups and other consumables, then assess payment fees, rent, any revenue share and service labor separately when reviewing the business plan.

Use Remote Management to Plan On-Site Work

Network-enabled B Series machines support remote monitoring of operating status and selected business data. Depending on the Master OS configuration, connectivity and account permissions, operators can review sales, receive status alerts and manage selected settings or basic operations.

For operators managing several malls, these functions can help identify which locations need attention and compare sales patterns. Remote alerts still require a response plan. Staff must visit the machine to replenish supplies, clean components, inspect the customer area and perform physical service.

Agree on service access hours and the replenishment route with the mall before installation. Assign responsibility for routine visits and for responding when the machine cannot complete a purchase.

Frequently Asked Questions
Where should the machine be placed in a shopping mall?

Compare approved positions near escalators, cinema foyers, children’s areas and food courts. Favor clear visibility, space to stop and access for servicing. Keep the machine and any queue clear of entrances, escalator landings and main circulation routes. Review nearby dessert competition and customer dwell time alongside passing traffic.

How much floor space does it need?

Allow for the machine body, door opening, ventilation, customer standing space and maintenance access. Request the current installation drawing for the selected model before approving the floor plan. A machine footprint alone does not define the full area needed for practical operation.

What are the electricity requirements?

The standard B Series specification is 220 V, 50 Hz, with a rated power of 1,800 W. Optional 110 V and 60 Hz configurations require confirmation before ordering. Have the mall’s facilities team check the proposed electrical supply against the selected configuration, including circuit capacity, connection and grounding. Rated power is not a measure of daily electricity consumption.

Does an employee need to stay beside the machine?

Normal self-service operation does not require an employee to prepare and hand over every order. An assigned operator is still needed for replenishment, cleaning, inspections and physical service. Plan staffing around sales patterns, travel time and the mall’s access rules.

How are ingredients and cups replenished?

An operator refills compatible mix and matching cups according to the machine’s operating instructions. The listed models provide approximately 20 L of mix capacity and storage for approximately 160 cups, subject to cup specification and configuration. Set the replenishment schedule using actual consumption, portion size and ingredient-handling requirements. Cup storage does not represent a guaranteed number of sales between visits.

How can daily sales be estimated?

Assess traffic at the proposed shopping mall ice cream vending machine position rather than using total mall attendance.

An initial planning formula is:

Estimated cups per day = visitors passing the proposed position × assumed purchase conversion rate × average cups per order.

Conversion and cups per order remain assumptions until measured. Validate them through a trial covering weekdays and weekends, and record completed sales, peak periods, stockouts, downtime and payment problems. Review operating costs alongside sales before expanding to additional locations.
