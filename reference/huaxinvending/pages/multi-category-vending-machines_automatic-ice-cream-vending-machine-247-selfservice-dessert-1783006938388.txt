Traditional ice cream sales usually depend on staff. A customer places an order, an employee takes a cup, operates the soft serve machine, adds toppings, cleans the area, and handles payment. During peak hours, this process can create waiting time. During low-traffic hours, keeping staff on site may not be cost-effective.

Huaxin automatic ice cream vending machine changes this workflow.

The customer completes the order on the machine. The machine handles payment, cup delivery, ice cream making, and finished cup pickup through an automated process. The operator only needs to refill mix, cups, jams, toppings, and perform regular cleaning and maintenance.

This makes the machine suitable for businesses that want to sell soft serve, frozen yogurt, sorbet, or Açaí-style dessert products without building a full dessert counter.

How the Automatic Ice Cream Vending Machine Works

The machine is designed around a simple self-service customer journey. First-time users can understand the process quickly, while operators can reduce the need for on-site service staff.

1. Select Dessert on the Touchscreen

Customers start by using the large touchscreen ordering interface. The screen can display product images, prices, flavor options, order instructions, brand content, and promotional information.

The 32-inch touchscreen is especially useful in commercial locations because it works not only as an ordering screen, but also as a visible advertising display. Operators can use it to show menu images, brand videos, seasonal offers, or location-specific promotions.

2. Complete Payment

After confirming the order, the customer completes payment through the configured payment system.

The machine can support multiple payment options, including:

Payment Option	Suitable Use Case
Credit card / debit card	Shopping malls, airports, cinemas, tourist attractions
NFC / contactless payment	Markets with Apple Pay, Google Pay, and tap-to-pay habits
QR code payment	Southeast Asia, Middle East, Latin America, and mobile-payment markets
Coin / bill payment	Schools, factories, amusement centers, and cash-based locations
Customized payment integration	Dealers, operators, and local payment service providers

Payment configuration can be adjusted according to the target country, local payment habits, and project requirements.

3. Automatic Making and Serving

Once payment is completed, the machine starts the automatic production process.

The system can automatically deliver the cup, dispense ice cream, control the serving process, and add configured ingredients such as jam or toppings. The closed production process helps keep the serving area cleaner and reduces direct human contact with the product.

A cup can be prepared in about 15–20 seconds, depending on product settings and configuration. This short serving time is important for commercial locations where customers often make quick purchase decisions.

4. Pick Up the Finished Cup

After the dessert is ready, the customer takes the finished cup from the pickup area. The process is clear, simple, and suitable for self-service use.

For operators, this means the machine can continue selling during lunch breaks, evening traffic, weekends, holidays, and other high-demand periods without requiring a dedicated ice cream server.

Flexible Payment System for Different Countries

Payment is one of the most important parts of a vending business. A good machine must match the payment habits of the local market.

Some locations rely heavily on card payment. Some markets prefer QR code payment. Some schools, factories, or amusement centers may still need coin or bill payment. For international dealers and operators, payment flexibility is often a deciding factor before purchase.

This machine can be configured with different payment systems according to the project requirements. It can support card payment, contactless payment, QR code payment, coin payment, bill payment, or customized payment integration.

For B2B buyers, this makes the machine easier to adapt to different countries, venues, and customer groups.

Fast Production and Stable Output

Speed matters in self-service dessert vending.

If a machine takes too long to make one cup, customers may walk away, especially in high-traffic areas. Huaxin automatic ice cream vending machine is designed to complete one cup in about 15–20 seconds.

This helps reduce waiting time and supports higher order turnover during busy periods.

The Max models use a 20L mix tank and can produce about 160–180 cups after one full refill, depending on cup size, serving weight, and recipe settings. The cup storage can hold about 160 cups, helping operators reduce refill frequency during daily operation.

This capacity is suitable for commercial points where the machine may need to serve customers continuously during peak traffic.

Flavor Combination with One Base Mix, Jams, and Toppings

This machine uses a single main mix system. Operators can work with soft serve mix, frozen yogurt mix, sorbet mix, or Açaí-style base according to their menu plan.

To create more product choices, the machine can combine the base mix with jams and toppings. This allows operators to offer different flavor combinations without changing the main product base frequently.

For example, one base mix can be paired with fruit jam, chocolate sauce, nut toppings, granola-style toppings, or other dry ingredients depending on the machine configuration and local food supply.

This setup is practical for vending operators because it keeps the ingredient system easier to manage while still giving customers more menu options.

Hygiene, Cleaning, and Food Safety Design

Food safety is a major concern for dessert vending equipment. Customers need to trust the product, and operators need a machine that is not difficult to maintain.

The machine uses a closed serving design to reduce unnecessary exposure during the production process. It also supports one-touch cleaning, helping operators simplify daily or regular cleaning work.

The low-temperature storage system helps maintain the condition of the mix inside the machine. Operators should still follow local food safety rules, ingredient shelf-life requirements, and cleaning schedules based on their market.

Compared with a traditional staffed soft serve counter, the automated process reduces repeated hand contact and helps standardize serving quality.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is an automatic ice cream vending machine?

An automatic ice cream vending machine is a self-service dessert machine that can accept customer orders, process payment, make ice cream, and deliver the finished cup through an automated process.

The customer ordering, payment, making, and pickup process can be completed without staff assistance. However, operators still need to refill mix, cups, jams, toppings, and perform regular cleaning and maintenance.

How long does it take to make one cup?

The machine can usually make one cup in about 15–20 seconds, depending on product settings, serving size, and configuration.

What payment systems can the machine support?

The machine can support coin, bill, credit card, QR code payment, and customized payment systems. Payment configuration can be adjusted according to the target market.

Can the machine support contactless payment?

Yes. The machine can be configured with contactless payment options depending on the local payment provider and project requirements.

Can I monitor the machine remotely?

What products can the machine make?
