Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many flavors can the machine offer?

A: By combining 1 liquid base with 3 types of jams and 3 types of dry toppings, customers can DIY up to 59 different flavor combinations.

Q: How often does the machine need cleaning?

A: Thanks to the built-in pasteurization system, a full cleaning is typically only required once or twice per week using the one-click auto-wash feature.

Q: What payment methods are supported?

A: The machine supports global payment standards including banknotes, coins, credit cards (Visa/Mastercard), and mobile QR payments like Apple Pay or Google Pay.
