Solving B2B Operational Challenges with Robotics

1. Is Labor Eating Your Profit Margins?

Traditional soft-serve shops lose up to 40% of their net profit to labor costs and employee turnover. The Huaxin B83 is a 100% Unattended Robotic Station. It manages payments, inventory, and hygiene cycles autonomously, allowing you to manage 20+ units via a mobile App through Cloud Telemetry.

2. How to Achieve 43% Higher Yield Per Cup?

Texture drives repeat business, but air drives profit. Our proprietary Air-Pump Overrun Tech achieves a 43% overrun, infusing air to create a silky, "DQ-style" mouthfeel. This also increases your product volume by 43%, meaning you sell more cups from the same amount of liquid mix.

3. Can You Guarantee 24/7 Food Safety?

Manual cleaning is prone to human error. Huaxin uses an Integrated UV + Pasteurization Matrix. The machine keeps the mix at a safe 2-6°C and self-sanitizes, reducing the manual deep-cleaning frequency from daily to just once per week, ensuring total hygiene compliance in malls and transit hubs.
