Frequently Asked Questions
What payment methods are supported?

Our vending machines accept coins, banknotes, credit cards (Visa/Mastercard), and QR code payments (Apple Pay/Google Pay). Custom payment solutions available for specific regional requirements.

How often does it require maintenance?

With the self-cleaning function, only weekly sanitation is needed. Major components like compressors (8+ years lifespan) and sensors require no routine maintenance. Remote diagnostics cover 95% of issues.

Where to get ice cream ingredients?

We provide starter ingredient packages with your order. Continuously available through:
• Our global supply network (pre-mixed formulations)
• Local dairy suppliers (cost-saving option)
• Automatic low-ingredient alerts via mobile app

Is professional installation required?
