Solving High-Traffic Retail Pain Points with Automation

1. Eliminate Rising Labor Costs

Staffing is the single biggest expense in traditional retail. The Huaxin B84max is a 100% Unattended Station that manages payments, orders, and hygiene cycles autonomously. One person can remotely manage 20+ units via our App, effectively reducing labor overhead by 90%.

2. Premium Texture Equals Repeat Customers

Consistency is key to franchising. Our 43% Overrun Air-Pump Tech achieves a silky, "DQ-grade" creaminess by precisely infusing air. Commercially, this also means you get 43% more volume from the same liquid mix, directly increasing your profit per serving.

3. Zero-Risk Hygiene Management

Manual cleaning human error can lead to safety risks. Huaxin kiosks feature an Integrated UV + Pasteurization Matrix. The system keeps ingredients at a safe 2-6°C and self-sanitizes, reducing the manual deep-cleaning cycle to once or twice a week.
