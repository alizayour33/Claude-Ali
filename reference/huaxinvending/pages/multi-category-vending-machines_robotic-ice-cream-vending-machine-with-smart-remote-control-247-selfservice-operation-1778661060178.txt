Frequently Asked Questions
How many flavor combinations are supported?

The machine supports up to 59 flavor combinations using one milk base with multiple syrup and topping combinations.

How quickly can the machine produce one serving?

The vending system can produce one cup of soft serve ice cream within approximately 15 to 20 seconds.

Does the machine require frequent cleaning?

No. The machine includes an automatic self-cleaning system, and routine cleaning is generally recommended once or twice per week.

Can operators monitor the machine remotely?

Yes. Operators can remotely view sales data, ingredient levels, temperature status, and machine performance through the cloud management system.

Can the machine be customized for different countries?

Yes. Custom language systems, payment methods, voltage configurations, and branding designs are available.

What locations are suitable for installation?

The machine is ideal for shopping malls, tourist attractions, airports, schools, entertainment venues, commercial streets, and other high-traffic areas.
