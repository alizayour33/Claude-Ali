Date：2025-06-25 Author：Huaxin

Huaxin Co., Ltd. sets new industry record! Our vending ice cream machine delivered 1,373 cups non-stop in 12 hours with zero jams and perfect temperature control - the world's first real-world endurance test. Say goodbye to "melting ice cream" and "stuck cups" in traditional machines Trust the solution that keeps your business running smoothly.

Huaxin Technology as a pioneer in the field of vending ice cream  machines, redefining reliability: An Industry-First Endurance Test! In an unprecedented 12-Hour Non-Stop Marathon on June 23, 2025 in Shandong’ Inzone Shopping Mall, we put our vending ice cream machines to the ultimate public test: 1,373 cups flawlessly served, zero mechanical failures, zero temperature fluctuations, zero quality compromise. This is not just a demonstration-it's our ironclad promise to global B2B partners: True reliability is the foundation of profitable operations.
 
Initial Stage: Stable Operation, Demonstrating Strength
  The vending machine ice cream  ran very stably, and the cup - dispensing speed and quality met the expected results. The technical team closely monitored the equipment's parameters through the real - time monitoring system to ensure that the equipment was operating within the normal range.


Middle Stage:Facing Challenges, Upholding Quality

  The real - time monitoring data showed that the temperature inside the vending  ice cream machine was always controlled within the set range, ensuring the quality and taste of the ice cream.


Final Stage:Sprinting Moment, Achieving New Success

  At 9 p.m., after 12 hours of continuous operation, our ice cream vending machine successfully dispensed 1373 cups, setting a new record in the industry! Throughout the event, the equipment achieved zero - fault operation.

  Within 12 hours, the equipment produced an average of about 1.91 cups per minute, demonstrating a very fast production rate. Each cup of ice cream was of exceptional quality, with a smooth texture and a fresh, delicious flavor, earning unanimous praise from the audience. This demonstrates that our products not only meet consumers' demand for speed but also ensure the quality and taste of the product.
