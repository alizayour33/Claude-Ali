How many servings can be made after fully refilling the ingredients and cups? How many servings can a machine sell per day?
The cup dispensers in some B Series models can hold approximately 160 cups. The actual number of servings depends on portion weight, overrun, recipe, and wastage. Daily sales volume is not a fixed machine specification; it also depends on foot traffic, operating hours, location conversion rate, refill frequency, and the machine's heat dissipation conditions.
How many servings can the machine produce per hour, and how long does one serving take?
Under normal ingredient conditions, ambient temperatures, and machine settings, commonly used models typically take approximately 15–20 seconds to prepare one serving. Actual hourly output also depends on the interval between customer orders, payment processing speed, serving size settings, topping combinations, continuous cooling load, and ambient temperature.
What are the dimensions, weight, and footprint of each machine model?
Dimensions and weight vary by model. You can check the specifications on the relevant product detail page or contact our account managers for guidance.
How do I choose the right machine model for my project?
We recommend first confirming the location type, available space, expected daily sales volume, intended product category, local payment methods, indoor or outdoor conditions, and initial purchase quantity. The floor-standing B Series is generally more suitable for busy public locations, while Easy7 or Power7 may be considered for compact spaces, staffed locations, or sites near foodservice counters. Huaxin can recommend suitable models and configurations based on this information.
What are the differences between the various ice cream vending machine models?
The models mainly differ in exterior design, dimensions, installation space requirements, product pickup structure, display design, and optional configurations.
Which ice cream vending machine models does Huaxin currently offer?
Huaxin currently offers the B Series of floor-standing ice cream vending machines, as well as compact models such as Easy7 and Power7. The B Series includes B83 Max, B84, B85, B86, and B86 Max, primarily designed for public commercial locations such as shopping malls, tourist attractions, family entertainment centers, and campuses. Easy7 and Power7 are better suited to retail, foodservice, and indoor commercial settings where space is limited.
