B84 FAQ
Is B84 intended for unattended self-service retail?

Yes. B84 is designed around touchscreen ordering, payment integration, automatic production, cup delivery, and remote management for self-service commercial operation.

Can B84 integrate local cashless payment methods?

Payment integration depends on the target country and payment provider. Card, NFC, QR code, and other supported solutions can be evaluated for the local market.

What should I confirm before choosing a B84 location?

Confirm the installation space, power supply, network connection, payment setup, ingredient supply, replenishment access, and operating environment before deployment.

Can the internal mechanical structure of B84 be customized?

No. Huaxin does not customize the internal mechanical structure or electronic layout and does not offer ODM. Customization is limited to exterior branding, interface, payment integration, and software functions.

Can multiple B84 machines be managed remotely?

Yes. Master OS can provide machine status, sales information, inventory reminders, and operational alerts across multiple deployed units.

Which frozen dessert concepts can be evaluated for B84?

B84 can be configured for soft serve, frozen yogurt, sorbet, açaí-style frozen desserts, and other suitable frozen dessert mixes. Ingredient and recipe compatibility should be confirmed before deployment.
