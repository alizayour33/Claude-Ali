Frequently Asked Questions
How long does it take to make one cup of ice cream?

Our ice cream vending machine delivers a complete serving in just 15 seconds from order to dispensing, making it the fastest solution in the market for high-traffic locations.

What is the daily energy consumption?

The machine is designed for maximum efficiency, consuming only 6-8kWh per day. The smart night mode and inverter compressor technology significantly reduce energy costs compared to traditional models.

How many flavors can the machine create?

With our innovative combination system, you can create 59 different flavor variations using 1 base milk syrup, 3 fruit jams, and 3 dry toppings, offering exceptional variety to customers.

Can I monitor the machine remotely?

Yes, our Master OS includes comprehensive remote management capabilities. You can monitor sales data, inventory levels, machine status, adjust pricing, and receive maintenance alerts through our mobile app from anywhere.

What is the machine's footprint?

The compact design requires only 0.85㎡ of floor space (1080mm × 890mm × 1965mm), making it ideal for locations where space is limited but customer traffic is high.

How many cups can the machine hold?

The standard configuration holds 80 cups, with an optional extended capacity of 160 cups available for high-volume locations. The smart inventory system alerts you when replenishment is needed.

What payment methods are supported?

Our machines support all major payment options including cash (notes and coins), credit/debit cards, mobile payments, and QR code scanning, ensuring convenience for all customers.

How often does the machine require cleaning?

The automated self-cleaning function makes maintenance simple. We recommend cleaning 1-2 times per week, with the entire process taking just 15 minutes using the built-in cleaning system.

What is the expected return on investment?

With proper placement in high-traffic areas, most operators achieve ROI within 3-4 months. The combination of low operating costs, 24/7 operation, and high-profit margins creates exceptional earning potential.

Is technical support available?

We provide comprehensive support including 24/7 remote assistance, video troubleshooting guides, and a network of technical partners worldwide. Most issues can be resolved remotely within minutes.
