What Makes This Machine Exceptional

At the core of this machine lies our proprietary rapid cooling system, powered by the premium Embraco compressor (1.5HP, imported from Brazil). Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does it take to make one serving?

A: From order placement to cup delivery, the complete process takes 15-20 seconds, with the actual freezing and dispensing occurring within 5 seconds thanks to our rapid cooling technology.

Q: How many flavor combinations are possible?

A: With 1 base mix, 3 jam flavors, and 3 toppings, customers can create up to 59 different flavor combinations, allowing for extensive personalization and repeat purchase incentives.

Q: Does the machine require an internet connection to operate?

A: The machine can operate offline for basic vending functions. However, an internet connection (5G/4G/WiFi) enables advanced features like remote monitoring, payment processing, and cloud-based management.

Q: How often does cleaning and maintenance need to be performed?

A: The built-in one-click cleaning system automates most maintenance tasks. We recommend a full cleaning cycle 1-2 times per week, with routine daily checks taking less than 5 minutes.

Q: What is the warranty coverage?

A: We provide a comprehensive 1-year warranty on all core components including the compressor, control systems, and dispensing mechanisms. Extended warranty options are available.

Q: Can the machine be customized for specific markets?

A: Yes, we offer extensive customization options including voltage configurations, payment systems, language interfaces, exterior branding, and even flavor-specific formulations to match local preferences.

Q: What is the typical return on investment?

A: Based on average sales data from our global customer base, most locations achieve payback within 3-4 months. High-traffic premium locations can recover investment in as little as 2 months.

Q: How many machines can one person manage?

A: Thanks to the Master.OS remote management system, a single operator can efficiently monitor and manage 20-30 machines, with scalability support for larger networks.

Q: Are spare parts readily available?

A: Yes, with annual production capacity exceeding 10,000 units and a global distribution network, we maintain comprehensive spare parts inventory for all models and ensure rapid delivery worldwide.
