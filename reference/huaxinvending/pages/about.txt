Our Manufacturing Base

Founded in 2013 and based in Dezhou, Shandong, China, Huaxin specializes in developing and manufacturing fully automatic commercial ice cream equipment, including floor-standing ice cream vending machines and countertop models.

Our 15,000 m² factory integrates machining, sheet metal fabrication, powder coating, refrigeration system production, and final assembly. In-house manufacturing of key mechanical components supports quality control, batch production, and spare parts supply. Quality checks include incoming material inspection, 48-hour refrigeration system pressure testing, and final functional testing before shipment.

Huaxin provides equipment and customization support for operators, distributors, and brands, including exterior branding, multilingual interfaces, and project-specific payment configurations.
