What Can the HUAXIN B85Max Do?
The HUAXIN B85Max is a complete, self-contained ice cream retail solution. It performs every step automatically: * **Instant Order Fulfillment:** From customer selection on the 32-inch BOE touchscreen to payment (coin, note, card, QR) and delivery, the entire process takes 15-20 seconds, capturing impulse buys. * **Diverse Flavor Creation:** Utilizing a single 20L hopper system, it intelligently dispenses a base mix and combines it with up to 3 toppings and 3 sauce options, allowing customers to create up to 59 unique flavor combinations. * **Autonomous Operation & Maintenance:** The machine runs 24/7. It executes scheduled self-cleaning cycles and features a UV + Pasteurization sterilization system, eliminating daily manual cleaning and ensuring consistent hygiene.

What Makes It Exceptional? (The Engineering & Intelligence Edge)
This machine dominates through superior engineering and smart management:

Core Performance Technology: Features 5-second rapid freezing, “Marathon Freezing Power” for high-volume continuous output (e.g., 1373 cups in 12 hours), and “Precise Cold Control” to prevent freeze-ups (0% freeze-block), ensuring reliability during peak times.

Premium Component Reliability: Built with industry-leading parts including an Embraco compressor (8+ year lifespan), German imported condenser fans, and OMRON photoelectric sensors for precise movement control, guaranteeing long-term durability and low maintenance.

Master OS Intelligent System: A proprietary cloud-based platform enables remote real-time monitoring of sales, inventory, and machine status. Operators can adjust prices, run promotions, diagnose over 95% of faults remotely, and enable energy-saving Night Mode—all from a smartphone app.

Space & Operation Optimized Design: Its compact footprint (0.91㎡) allows placement in high-rent locations. It is plug-and-play, requires no assembly, and its modular design with plug-and-play terminals simplifies any needed maintenance.

What Do You Gain? (Tangible Business Traetlormation)
Deploying the B85Max translates into direct and measurable commercial advantages:

Dramatic Labor & Cost Savings: Eliminates the need for dedicated staff to serve ice cream. One person can manage up to 10 machines. Combined with low energy consumption (10-15 kWh/day), this significantly reduces operational overhead.

Enhanced Customer Engagement & Sales: The visual production window creates a “theater effect,” boosting social media sharing which drives secondary sales. Flavor variety and premium options (like cheesecake base) increase repeat purchase rates by 25-30%.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How many flavors can it actually serve?
A: Through combinations of 1 base mix, 3 sauces, and 3 dry toppings, the system can create up to 59 different flavor experiences for customers.

Q: How often does it need cleaning?
A: The machine has an automatic cleaning function. We recommend running the cleaning cycle 1-2 times per week, which can be initiated remotely via the app.

Q: What happens if the machine has a fault?
A: Over 95% of issues can be diagnosed and resolved remotely through our Master OS app via video guidance and modular part replacement. We offer 24/7 remote technical support.

Q: Can it operate without an internet connection?
A: Yes. The machine can perform all vending functions offline. An internet connection is only required for remote monitoring, data syncing, and remote management features.

Q: What is the power consumption?
A: The rated power is 1500W-1800W. With its efficient inverter compressor and Night Mode, actual daily consumption is typically between 10-15 kWh.
