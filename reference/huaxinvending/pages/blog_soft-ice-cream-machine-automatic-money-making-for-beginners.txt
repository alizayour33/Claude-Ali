Soft Ice Cream Machine Entrepreneurship: A Fully Automatic Money-Making Solution Even for Beginners
Date：2025-11-22 Author：Huaxin

This smart ice cream vending machine is an ideal entrepreneurial project for beginners, requiring no experience. It’s easy to operate—with one-button cleaning and a user-friendly app—and offers proven high-traffic locations (malls, communities, schools). Beyond ice cream sales, it generates extra income via screen/cup ads and boosts other businesses. Backed by predictive alerts and modular repairs, it lowers risks, letting entrepreneurs start easily without quitting jobs or heavy investment.


Many people dream of starting a business, especially in the ice cream industry, but they are held back by the fear of failure due to lack of experience. Well, this automatic ice cream vending machine is your perfect solution. Last year, a client from Singapore—who had never worked with automatic ice cream vending machines before—bought two of our machines and placed them in an office building. Guess what? He recovered 60% of his investment in the first month, and now he has ten machines operating under his name.
In fact, there's no need to overcomplicate it. Today's smart ice cream machines are far from the old-fashioned ones that required professional technicians to adjust. Just like using a smartphone, you turn it on, tap the screen a few times, and the machine runs on its own.
How to Choose a Location for the Machine? Here Are Proven Options
The biggest headache for new entrepreneurs is choosing a location. Here are several verified solutions for you:
Next to escalators in shopping malls, especially on the floor with cinemas. One of our clients placed a machine opposite a cinema ticket office—after movies ended, the line for ice cream was even longer than the line for tickets.
In front of community convenience stores is also a great choice. A client from Zhengzhou installed a machine outside a convenience store; parents would buy ice cream on a whim when picking up their kids from school. He said this is much more hassle-free than running a traditional ice cream shop—no need to hire staff or pay high rent.
Near schools is another solid option. Placing the machine in school teaching buildings, cafeterias, or at the school gate allows sales during breaks and after school hours, with surprisingly good sales volume.
Simple Operation, Time-Saving
Many people worry about not knowing how to operate the machine, but this concern is unnecessary. We sum up the operation for clients in three sentences: Add ingredients when they run out, press one button to clean when it's dirty, and you don't need to do anything else.
The management app on your phone is designed as simply as a game. Just open it once a day to adjust settings or restock based on the machine's status. We have a 65-year-old aunt client who could barely use a smartphone before—now she manages three machines and is more skilled than many young people.
Cleaning deserves a special mention. Cleaning old-fashioned ice cream machines was a nightmare—you had to take apart every part. Now, you just press a button, and the machine sterilizes and cleans itself automatically, taking only 15 minutes from start to finish.
More Ways to Make Money Than You Think
Beyond selling ice cream, the large screen on the machine is a goldmine. We help clients display rotating ads for nearby businesses on the screen, which adds several hundred dollars in monthly income. A client in a tourist area came up with an even smarter idea: he put ads for local homestays on the screen and earns a commission for every booking generated.
The cups are another moneymaker. We customize exclusive cup designs for machines of chain brands—this alone brings in tens of thousands of dollars in additional annual income. Even individual operators can partner with local businesses to print ads on cups and earn extra cash.
Most importantly, this machine helps drive traffic to your other businesses. One client owns a milk tea shop; after placing an ice cream machine at the shop entrance, his milk tea sales increased by 30%. As he put it: “Customers who buy ice cream often pick up a cup of milk tea too—this traffic comes so naturally.”
Professional After-Sales Service
When I first entered this industry, my biggest fear was getting midnight calls from clients saying their machines were broken. Now, things are different—our backend system issues an alert before a machine breaks down.
Even if repairs are needed, there's no need to panic. The core components are modularly designed, just like Lego bricks—you only need to replace the broken part. We provide detailed tutorial videos, and following them allows you to fix the machine in just ten minutes.
In fact, the hardest part of starting a business is taking the first step. The original intention of our solution is to help people with zero experience start their entrepreneurial journey easily.
I remember last year at a Guangzhou exhibition, a young man lingered around our machine for three days. Finally, he said to us: “After seeing this, starting a business doesn't seem that scary anymore.” Now, his machines have been operating near university towns for half a year, and just a few days ago, he said he wants to order two more.
If you're looking for a reliable entrepreneurial project, start with an ice cream machine. It doesn't require you to quit your job, have a professional background, or invest all your savings. Just plug it in and restock—and leave the rest to the machine.
