  • The cup dispensers in some B Series models can hold approximately 160 cups. The actual number of servings depends on portion weight, overrun, recipe, and wastage. Daily sales volume is not a fixed machine specification; it also depends on foot traffic, operating hours, location conversion rate, refill frequency, and the machine's heat dissipation conditions.
    Under normal ingredient conditions, ambient temperatures, and machine settings, commonly used models typically take approximately 15–20 seconds to prepare one serving. Actual hourly output also depends on the interval between customer orders, payment processing speed, serving size settings, topping combinations, continuous cooling load, and ambient temperature.
    Dimensions and weight vary by model. You can check the specifications on the relevant product detail page or contact our account managers for guidance.
    We recommend first confirming the location type, available space, expected daily sales volume, intended product category, local payment methods, indoor or outdoor conditions, and initial purchase quantity. The floor-standing B Series is generally more suitable for busy public locations, while Easy7 or Power7 may be considered for compact spaces, staffed locations, or sites near foodservice counters. Huaxin can recommend suitable models and configurations based on this information.
    The models mainly differ in exterior design, dimensions, installation space requirements, product pickup structure, display design, and optional configurations.
    Huaxin currently offers the B Series of floor-standing ice cream vending machines, as well as compact models such as Easy7 and Power7. The B Series includes B83 Max, B84, B85, B86, and B86 Max, primarily designed for public commercial locations such as shopping malls, tourist attractions, family entertainment centers, and campuses. Easy7 and Power7 are better suited to retail, foodservice, and indoor commercial settings where space is limited.
  • The cost per serving depends on the ingredient purchase price, portion weight, cups, spoons, sauces, toppings, payment processing fees, cleaning losses, and local energy costs. There is no single figure that applies worldwide. Contact us, and our account managers can help estimate costs for your local market.
    Customers are generally not required to purchase ingredients or paper cups from Huaxin. They may source ingredients and consumables locally, provided they meet food regulations, machine recipe requirements, and size specifications. To reduce risks during initial operation, we recommend providing ingredient specifications, cup dimensions, and samples to Huaxin for testing and confirmation before purchasing.
    Common optional toppings include suitably sized dry ingredients such as nuts, grains, and dried fruit, as well as fruit jams and other sauces with suitable flow properties.
    Huaxin's commonly used B Series vending configurations use one base mix at a time, with sauces and toppings creating different menu combinations. The machine can be switched between frozen yogurt, soft serve ice cream, sorbet, and Açaí-style products, but two different base mixes cannot be processed simultaneously in the same standard mix hopper.
    The number of menu options depends on the combinations of base mix, sauces, and dry toppings. By combining different toppings and sauces, the machine can offer up to 54 flavor combinations.
    With compatible recipes and completed testing, Huaxin machines can be used to prepare soft serve ice cream, frozen yogurt, sorbet, and Açaí-style frozen desserts.
  • The machine uses refrigeration, temperature monitoring, cleaning procedures, and optional pasteurization to help maintain safe operation. However, operators must still manage ingredient expiration dates, refill times, cleaning records, and environmental hygiene.
    Cleaning frequency depends on the ingredient recipe, operating hours, local food safety regulations, ambient temperature, and whether pasteurization is enabled. Operators should routinely clean the dispensing outlet, drip collection area, and any visible food residue, and follow the operating manual for cleaning the ingredient flow path and periodic disassembly and cleaning.
    The machine offers an automatic cleaning function. The operator manually adds clean water, and the machine performs an automatic circulating cleaning cycle.
    Pasteurization is available as an optional feature on all Huaxin models. Please contact an account manager for details.
  • The machine can be used for extended commercial operation. High ambient temperatures increase the refrigeration load, so sufficient ventilation space must be left around the machine, and direct sunlight or enclosed installations should be avoided.
    Operators add ice cream mix through the designated refill area and replenish paper cups, dry toppings, and sauces according to the machine specifications. When operating parameters reach preset conditions, the machine alerts the operator.
    Once the machine arrives, customers can contact our after-sales specialists for guidance on startup, ingredient refilling, menu setup, cleaning, and basic troubleshooting. For more complex projects, a more detailed technical handover can be arranged before shipment.
    After a customer selects a product on the touchscreen and completes payment, the machine automatically dispenses a cup, prepares the frozen dessert, adds the selected toppings or sauces, and delivers the finished product to the pickup area. Sensors, refrigeration, control systems, and a remote management platform monitor key operating conditions, reducing the need for manual ordering and preparation.
  • Local mobile payments, bill acceptors, and coin acceptors must be confirmed individually for each country. Customers should provide the country, currency, banknote or coin denominations, preferred payment brand, and payment service provider information.
    Nayax or other mainstream third-party card readers can be installed. Actual availability depends on whether the provider operates in the customer's country, the terminal model, MDB compatibility, SIM or network solution, merchant account eligibility, and local certifications.
    Models can be equipped with an MDB payment interface, and integration with a customer-specified payment module can be evaluated.
    Depending on the configuration, the machine can support cashless payments such as credit cards, debit cards, NFC, QR codes, and Apple Pay. Cash modules such as bill acceptors and coin acceptors can also be evaluated. Available methods depend on the destination country, payment service provider, communication protocol, network conditions, and local regulations.
  • The machine interface can support multiple languages and branding customization, including logos, menu images, and advertising videos.
    Huaxin machines can be managed remotely through a cloud platform. Common functions include viewing sales orders, operating data, machine online status, certain consumable statuses, and fault information, as well as centralized management of multiple machines.
  • Huaxin can discuss distributor pricing, territorial support, and brand cooperation with partners who have local sales, service, and market development capabilities. Distribution terms typically depend on the purchase plan, annual sales volume, after-sales capabilities, and market investment. Territorial protection requires clear sales commitments and a cooperation agreement.
    Pilot projects can usually begin with smaller quantities. Tiered pricing for bulk purchases can be evaluated based on model, configuration, customization requirements, and order volume. Exclusive exterior designs, extensive software customization, or special hardware customization may require a higher minimum order quantity.
    Machine pricing depends on the model, payment system, refrigeration and food safety configuration, topping system, language, branding customization, and purchase quantity. Customers can receive a formal quotation after providing their country, required quantity, and configuration preferences.
  • Machine language, voltage, frequency, and plug configurations can be customized for the target market.
  • Machines are packed with protective materials suitable for international transport, with securing, moisture protection, and shock protection measures arranged according to the model.
    Ice cream vending machines are usually shipped by sea. Less-than-container-load (LCL) shipping is available, while full-container-load (FCL) shipping can be arranged for suitable order quantities. Transit times depend on the route, transshipment, vessel schedules, destination port congestion, and customs clearance.
    Huaxin can evaluate shipping arrangements such as CIF and DDP based on the destination and logistics conditions.
    Shipping costs depend on quantity, destination port or full delivery address, shipping method, and trade terms. For a reasonably accurate estimate, we need at least the country, city or port, postal code, quantity, and preferred terms, such as CIF, CFR, or DDP.
    Huaxin can arrange exports of ice cream vending machines to most markets with established international logistics and normal import conditions. Customers should provide their country, city or port, and postal code during the quotation stage.
  • Customers do not necessarily need to attend factory training. Huaxin can provide operating manuals, video tutorials, and remote training covering startup, refilling, menu configuration, cleaning, routine maintenance, and basic troubleshooting. More structured factory training can be arranged as needed for bulk projects, distributors, or local service teams.
    Overseas after-sales support normally begins with remote diagnosis using photos, videos, fault codes, and backend data. Huaxin can provide online technical guidance, repair videos, parameter checks, and spare parts replacement support.
  • Profit margins and payback periods depend on selling price, daily sales volume, ingredient and consumable costs, rent, payment processing fees, shipping and import costs, maintenance, and seasonal fluctuations.
    Common locations include shopping malls, tourist attractions, family entertainment centers, cinemas, campuses, transport hubs, and other high-footfall commercial spaces.
    Huaxin can provide relevant machine photos, videos, or case study materials according to the customer's country, project type, and applicable permissions. On-site visits depend on the availability of suitable local projects and the operating customer's consent. To protect customer privacy and business information, Huaxin does not disclose customer contact details, sales figures, or profit data without authorization.
