According to relevant statistics, during the May Day holiday last year, the average daily sales of vending ice cream machines in a scenic spot reached 300 cups, with very substantial sales; the daily sales of vending machines in commercial centers can also be stable at about 200 cups, fully demonstrating the strong money-sucking ability of ice cream vending machines in these scenarios during the May Day holiday.
