The FAQ of Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine
  Q1. What is the capacity of milk syrup? How many cups of ice cream can it makes when filling in the tank?
  A1. The capacity of milk syrup is 20L, raw material tank is 50cm long, 30cm wide, and 20cm high. If you fill the tank once(according to the capacity of single cup), you can make 150 to 180 cups of ice cream.
  Q2.What is the voltage of the ice cream vending machine？How much power does it consume?
  A2. The voltage is 220V. The peak voltage is 1800W, The average daily electricity consumption is  about 10 to 15 kWh
  Q3. Can the soft serve ice cream vending machine be placed outdoor? Is it rainproof?
  A3. The rainproof machine can be placed outdoor, but we also suggest you make a cover for the machine to prevent the machine from aging too quickly after a long-term exposure to the sun.
  Q4. What is the operating principle of an ice cream vending machine?
  A4. It is the liquid milk syrup become a delicious ice cream via extreme ice refrigeration technology and puffing system. The core chip plays a important role in this process. We have self-developed process combining Taylor’s Principle and Japanese technology.
  Q5. Does the ice cream machine need to be cleaned?
  A5. In order to ensure the taste of ice cream, we recommend regular cleaning. The machine has one-click cleaning function, which is very easy to operate.
These are specific operational steps as follows:
First, you need to click Defrost, the milk syrup will be defrosted.
Second, you need to click cleaning button again and pour water into the material tank. It is better to pour warm water because the milk syrup is the oil substance. The machine start to clean automatically after click the button.
Third, you need to click Output Open, removing waste water.
Please repeat the above steps twice or more. If the discharging water is clear, you could stop cleaning.
