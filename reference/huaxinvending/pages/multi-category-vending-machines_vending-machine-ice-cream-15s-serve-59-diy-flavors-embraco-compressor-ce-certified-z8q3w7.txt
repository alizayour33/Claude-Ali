FAQ About Vending Machine Ice Cream
Q1: How long does it really take to make one cup of ice cream?

A: Exactly 15 seconds from order completion to cup dispensing—3x faster than traditional manual shops, which solves the problem of customer loss due to long waits.

Q2: How do you achieve 59 DIY flavors?

A: The machine is equipped with 1 milk syrup tank, 3 jam containers (e.g., strawberry, chocolate, passion fruit), and 3 topping boxes (e.g., popping boba, crisp flakes). Through free combination, it creates 59 unique flavors, including popular milk tea-inspired combinations.

Q3: Why is the Embraco compressor a key advantage?

A: Embraco is a world-leading compressor brand imported from Brazil. It reduces energy consumption by 40% compared to ordinary compressors, with a service life of over 8 years. It also ensures stable refrigeration even in high-temperature environments (up to 40°C), avoiding shutdowns in summer.

Q4: Can I manage the machine remotely when I'm not on site?

A: Yes! Our cloud control system allows you to check real-time sales data (daily/weekly/monthly), monitor raw material and cup levels, adjust prices, and start self-cleaning—all via a mobile app. You’ll receive instant alerts for low supplies or faults.

Q5: How often does the machine need cleaning?

A: It’s recommended to use the one-click self-cleaning function 1-2 times a week, which takes only 15 minutes each time. No need to disassemble parts—saving a lot of manual labor compared to traditional machines that require 2+ hours of disassembly cleaning.

Q6: What payment methods does the machine support?

A: It supports multiple payment methods, including coins, banknotes, credit cards, QR codes (WeChat/Alipay), and NFC. We can also customize the payment system according to your local market needs.

Q7: Is the machine compliant with European standards for export?

A: Yes! The machine has obtained CE certification (mandatory for EU market access) and ETL certification (food safety standard). It also meets ROHS environmental requirements, so it can be smoothly deployed in Europe, North America, and Southeast Asia.

Q8: How much space does the machine require?

A: The machine dimensions are 1080mm*890mm*1965mm, with a floor area of only 0.85㎡—it can fit in narrow spaces like mall escalator sides and office tea rooms, solving the problem of limited space.

Q9: What if the machine breaks down suddenly?

A: First, you’ll receive a fault alert on the app. Our 24/7 after-sales team provides remote video guidance—98% of faults (e.g., sensor errors) can be solved online. For hardware issues, we offer modular parts that can be replaced in 5 minutes with video instructions.

Q10: What is the minimum order quantity, and can I get a sample?

A: The minimum order quantity is 1 unit—you can purchase a sample to test the market. We also provide customized services (appearance, voltage, language) for bulk orders (10+ units).
