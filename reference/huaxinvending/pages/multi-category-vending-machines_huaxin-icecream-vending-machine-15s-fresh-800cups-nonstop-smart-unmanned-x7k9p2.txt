Frequently Asked Questions
How does the 15-second serving time compare to traditional ice cream machines?

Our ice cream vending machine utilizes patented 5-second flash freeze technology and optimized mechanical processes to achieve 3x faster serving times than conventional machines, significantly reducing customer wait times during peak hours.

What makes your machine capable of 800-cup non-stop production?

Through advanced marathon freezing technology and precision temperature control, our system prevents cylinder freezing even during extended operation. The 20L large-capacity hopper and efficient cooling system maintain consistent performance.

How does the remote management system work?

Our proprietary cloud platform enables real-time monitoring of sales data, inventory levels, machine status, and remote parameter adjustments. Receive automatic alerts for maintenance needs or low ingredients via mobile app.

What payment methods are supported?

The ice cream vending machine supports comprehensive payment options including credit/debit cards, mobile QR codes (Alipay, WeChat Pay), cash (notes and coins), and contactless payments for maximum customer convenience.

What certifications does the machine have for international markets?

Our ice cream vending machines are fully certified with ETL (North America), CE (Europe), RoHS, and ETL certifications, ensuring compliance with international food safety and electrical standards for global deployment.

How does the visual window enhance customer experience?

The full transparency design allows customers to watch the entire ice cream creation process - from cup selection to dispensing and topping application. This visual engagement increases trust in food safety and creates Instagrammable moments that boost social sharing.
