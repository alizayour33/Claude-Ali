Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How many flavors can the machine produce?
A: By combining 1 liquid base with 3 jams and 3 dry toppings, the machine can generate up to 59 unique DIY flavor combinations.

Q: Is the machine difficult to install?
A: No. It is a "plug-and-play" solution. You only need a stable power supply and a small space. We provide full video guidance for setup.

Q: Can I customize the machine for my country?
A: Yes. We support full OEM/ODM customization including local voltage (110V/220V), local payment systems (Credit Card, QR, Coins), and multiple language interfaces.

Q: How often does it need cleaning?
A: Thanks to the low-temperature preservation and pasteurization system, manual cleaning is only recommended 1-2 times per week using the one-click cleaning function.
