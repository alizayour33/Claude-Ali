The commercial ice cream vending machine is an intelligent unattended retail solution designed for high-traffic environments such as shopping malls, tourist attractions, airports, and campuses. Equipped with a powerful compressor refrigeration system and a 32-inch interactive touchscreen, the machine can automatically produce fresh soft-serve ice cream in just 15 seconds.
