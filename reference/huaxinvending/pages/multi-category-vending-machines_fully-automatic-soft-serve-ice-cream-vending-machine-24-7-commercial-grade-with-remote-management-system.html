What Makes This Machine Exceptional

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does it take to make one serving?

A: With 1 base mix, 3 jam flavors, and 3 toppings, customers can create up to 59 different flavor combinations, allowing for extensive personalization and repeat purchase incentives.

Q: Does the machine require an internet connection to operate?

A: The machine can operate offline for basic vending functions. However, an internet connection (5G/4G/WiFi) enables advanced features like remote monitoring, payment processing, and cloud-based management.

Q: How often does cleaning and maintenance need to be performed?

A: The built-in one-click cleaning system automates most maintenance tasks. We recommend a full cleaning cycle 1-2 times per week, with routine daily checks taking less than 5 minutes.

Q: What is the warranty coverage?

Q: Can the machine be customized for specific markets?

Q: What is the typical return on investment?

Q: How many machines can one person manage?
