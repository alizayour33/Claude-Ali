Does an Ice Cream Vending Machine Use Electricity?

Ice cream vending machines rely entirely on electricity to operate core components like refrigeration (-18°C to -20°C maintenance), industrial control systems, payment modules, and ordering screens. Power failure causes rapid temperature rise, melting ice cream, and disabling user interactions. Manufacturers adopt energy-saving measures, such as intelligent temperature control and night energy-saving modes, to balance power demand and environmental protection, ensuring efficient and sustainable operation while maintaining ice cream quality and user experience.
