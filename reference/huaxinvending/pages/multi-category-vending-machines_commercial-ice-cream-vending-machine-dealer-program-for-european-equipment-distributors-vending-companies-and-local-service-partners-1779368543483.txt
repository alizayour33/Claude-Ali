Frequently Asked Questions
Who can become a commercial ice cream vending machine dealer?

Vending equipment distributors, foodservice equipment dealers, local service companies, retail project developers, and operators with commercial customer resources can become suitable dealer partners.

What makes the Max Series suitable for dealers?

The Max Series has strong commercial selling points, including 20L capacity, 160-cup storage, 15–20 second serving, cashless payment support, remote monitoring, self-cleaning, and custom branding.

What customers can dealers target?

Dealers can target vending operators, shopping malls, airports, train stations, tourist attractions, theme parks, supermarkets, schools, retail chains, and local investors.

What is the capacity of the Max Series?

The Max Series has a 20L mix cylinder, 160-cup storage capacity, and can produce about 160–180 cups after filling, depending on product settings and serving size.

Does the machine support cashless payment?

Yes. The machine can support credit card payment, QR payment, coin, bill, and customized payment systems according to local market requirements.

Can dealers customize the machine?

Yes. Huaxin supports OEM/ODM customization for appearance, logo, machine wrap, voltage, payment system, language, UI, screen content, and local brand presentation.

Can operators manage the machine remotely?

Yes. Operators can remotely monitor sales data, temperature, ingredient status, cup status, revenue reports, machine alerts, and operation settings.

What support does Huaxin provide to dealers?

Huaxin provides customization support, operation training, remote video technical support, spare parts availability, troubleshooting guidance, software assistance, and one-year machine warranty.
