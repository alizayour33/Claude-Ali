Move Soft Serve Sales Beyond the Traditional Counter

Traditional soft serve equipment normally depends on an employee to take the order, prepare the cup, operate the dispensing handle, complete payment, and clean the serving area. This model works inside staffed restaurants, but it can limit operating hours and increase labor requirements when businesses want to expand into shopping malls, cinemas, campuses, tourist attractions, or family entertainment centers.

The Huaxin B85 changes this process by combining soft serve production with vending automation.

Customers order directly from the touchscreen, complete payment through the configured payment terminal, and receive a prepared soft serve cup through the pickup area. The machine handles cup dispensing, soft serve production, sauce or topping addition, and cup transfer as part of one automated workflow.

This makes the B85 more than a traditional soft serve freezer. It is a complete soft serve ice cream vending machine developed for commercial self-service retail.

Automatic Soft Serve Production from Order to Pickup

The B85 is designed to complete the main customer-facing steps automatically:

The customer selects a soft serve product on the touchscreen.
The configured payment system confirms the transaction.
The machine automatically releases a cup.
The cup is positioned beneath the dispensing system.
Soft serve is produced according to the programmed serving settings.
Selected sauces or toppings are added when included in the configuration.
The finished cup is transferred to the pickup area.

A typical cup can be completed in approximately 15–20 seconds, depending on the recipe, serving weight, ingredient condition, and topping configuration.

The transparent preparation window allows customers to watch the cup being made. This visible process helps turn the machine into part of the customer experience, especially in entertainment, leisure, and family-oriented locations.

Stable Cup Production for Commercial Locations

Soft serve consistency matters in unattended retail. A machine must do more than dispense quickly—it must repeatedly position the cup, control the serving amount, and complete the preparation process without requiring an employee to correct every order.

The B85 uses an automated cup dispensing and positioning system supported by industrial control components and photoelectric sensors. The cup dispenser uses a multi-spiral separation structure to help release one cup at a time, while sensors monitor cup availability and movement.

The mechanical transmission system controls the cup position during production, helping maintain a stable presentation from one order to the next.

This automated process is particularly valuable for locations where the operator cannot assign a dedicated member of staff to the machine throughout the day.

One Soft Serve Base with Multiple Menu Options

The B85 uses a single main ingredient cylinder. It does not store several independent soft serve base flavors at the same time.

Operators normally select one suitable soft serve mix—such as vanilla, chocolate, cheese-flavored soft serve, or another compatible recipe—as the main product base. Different menu options can then be created by adding configured sauces and toppings.

Depending on the machine configuration, the menu may include combinations such as:

Vanilla soft serve with strawberry sauce
Vanilla soft serve with chocolate sauce
Soft serve with fruit-flavored sauce
Soft serve with granola or dry toppings
Soft serve with nuts or biscuit pieces
Seasonal branded soft serve combinations

This approach gives operators more menu variety without requiring several separate freezing cylinders.

To change the main soft serve flavor, the existing mix must be removed, the ingredient system must be cleaned, and the new mix must be prepared according to the supplier’s operating instructions.

Suitable Soft Serve Ingredients

The machine should use a commercial soft serve liquid mix or properly prepared powder-based mix that is suitable for automatic dispensing equipment.

The ingredient should have:

Stable viscosity
Consistent sugar and fat content
Good freezing performance
No large fruit pieces or solid particles
Reliable storage characteristics
Compatibility with the machine’s food-contact system

Before commercial operation, the selected recipe should be tested to confirm freezing performance, dispensing shape, serving speed, and cleaning requirements.

Operators may source ingredients locally, provided the recipe meets the machine’s operating requirements. Huaxin can also provide guidance for mix testing and machine parameter adjustment.

Remote visibility does not remove the need for refilling and cleaning, but it helps operators plan these tasks more efficiently and respond faster when a machine requires attention.

Cleaning and Routine Operation

Although the B85 reduces customer-facing labor, the machine still requires regular ingredient replenishment, surface cleaning, food-contact maintenance, and scheduled internal cleaning.

The machine includes a one-touch cleaning function to simplify the flushing process. Operators should also clean removable food-contact parts, sauce lines, topping components, the dispensing area, pickup area, and ingredient tank according to the operating manual and local food safety requirements.

A practical operating routine normally includes:

Checking the ingredient level
Replenishing cups
Inspecting sauces and toppings
Wiping the touchscreen and pickup area
Running the required cleaning process
Checking the remote management dashboard
Recording maintenance and sanitation activities

Cleaning frequency depends on the ingredient used, operating environment, daily sales volume, local regulations, and the operator’s food safety plan.

FAQ
1. What type of soft serve mix can be used?

The machine can use compatible commercial liquid soft serve mix or properly prepared powder-based mix. The ingredient should have stable viscosity, consistent freezing performance, and no large solid particles. Testing the selected local mix before commercial deployment is recommended.

2. How fast can the B85 prepare one cup?

A standard soft serve cup normally takes approximately 15–20 seconds from production start to completion. Actual time may vary according to serving weight, recipe, ingredient temperature, sauce selection, and machine settings.

3. What cup size does the machine support?

The cup dispensing system is configured around the selected paper cup dimensions before delivery. The machine can store up to approximately 160 cups. Exact cup volume, diameter, height, material, and serving weight should be confirmed during order configuration.

4. Can the machine offer different soft serve flavors?

The B85 has one main ingredient cylinder, so it uses one soft serve base flavor at a time. Operators can create multiple menu choices by combining the base with different sauces and toppings. Changing the main flavor requires replacing the mix and cleaning the ingredient system.

5. How is the machine cleaned?

The machine includes a one-touch cleaning function to assist with internal flushing. Operators must also clean removable food-contact parts, the dispensing area, sauce or topping components, the ingredient tank, and the pickup area according to the manual and local food safety rules.

6. Is the machine suitable for high-traffic locations?

Yes. Its approximately 15–20-second production cycle, 20L ingredient capacity, cup storage, cashless payment support, and remote monitoring make it suitable for malls, cinemas, attractions, campuses, and family entertainment centers. Operators should plan timely cup and ingredient replenishment during busy periods.
