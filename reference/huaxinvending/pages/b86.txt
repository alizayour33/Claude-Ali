FAQ
What payment options can the B86 ice cream vending machine support?

B86 can work with card, NFC, QR code and other payment solutions depending on the target country and payment provider. Huaxin can assist with payment integration based on local market requirements.

How much daily operator attention does the B86 require?

Service frequency depends on daily sales and menu configuration. Operators mainly monitor ice cream mix, cups, toppings and other consumables, while remote management can help track machine status and inventory reminders.

What routine cleaning and maintenance does B86 require?

B86 is designed for routine commercial cleaning and maintenance. Operators should follow the provided procedures for food-contact parts, the dispensing area, ingredient containers and other components requiring regular sanitation.

Can multiple B86 vending machines be managed remotely?

Yes. Master OS supports multi-machine management, allowing operators to check machine status, sales information, inventory reminders and operational alerts across different locations.

What should I confirm before installing a B86 commercial ice cream vending machine?

Confirm installation space, electrical requirements, network connectivity, payment setup, ingredient supply and the operating environment. Huaxin can provide installation and operation guidance before deployment.

What after-sales support is available for B86 operators and distributors?

Huaxin provides operation guidance, remote technical support, troubleshooting assistance and spare parts support to help operators maintain B86 machines after installation.
