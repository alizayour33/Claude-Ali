Product Description.

HUAXIN Smart Ice Cream Vending Machine - Redefine Commercial Ice Cream Sales

As a global leader in smart ice cream vending machines with 13 years of experience, HUAXIN has served 33 countries and holds 70% of China’s market share. Our smart ice cream vending machine is engineered to solve core business pain points while delivering exceptional user experiences.
Why Choose HUAXIN Smart Ice Cream Vending Machine?
1. Space-Saving Design for Maximum Flexibility
Traditional vending machines occupy valuable space, limiting placement options. Our machine features a compact 0.91㎡ footprint, weighing only 320KG with adjustable casters for easy movement. No assembly or store modification needed—just plug and play, perfect for bars, malls, scenic spots, and other high-traffic areas.
2. Full Automation to Cut Labor Costs
Say goodbye to manual operations. From flavor selection and payment to production, the entire process takes just 15-20 seconds. No more staff required for cup handling, ice cream making, or cleaning—one person can manage 10 machines, saving significant labor expenses. The closed discharge port ensures contactless production, maintaining hygiene and safety.
3. Smart Technology for Effortless Management
Equipped with the Master OS intelligent system, the machine supports remote control via a mobile app. Monitor real-time data (temperature, ingredient levels, sales reports), adjust prices, and perform one-click troubleshooting—95% of issues can be resolved remotely. Switch between working and night modes to minimize energy consumption, and use the one-click self-cleaning function for hassle-free maintenance (recommended 1-2 times a week).
4. Flavor Diversity to Boost Repeat Purchases
With 1 base milk paste, 3 toppings, and 3 jams, you can create 59 unique flavors. Our exclusive cheese ice cream (same quality as DQ at 1/3 the price) increases repeat purchases by 30%, while milk tea-inspired toppings (e.g., taro balls) boost repurchases by 25%. The 43% expansion rate ensures smooth, ice crystal-free ice cream.
5. High Durability and Global Certification
Core components are sourced from top brands: Embraco compressor (8+ year lifespan), Omron sensors, and German imported cooling fans. The machine has passed CE, ETL, RoHS, and CB certifications, with key parts (solenoid valves, stirring motors) tested for millions of operations. The 5.4㎡ condenser and insulation cotton reduce energy loss, resulting in a daily power consumption of only 10-15 kWh.
6. High Profit Margin and Quick ROI
With a per-cup cost of $0.22-$0.27 and a selling price of $0.90-$1.50, the profit margin exceeds 65%. High-traffic locations can sell up to 200 cups per day, generating a monthly net profit of $1,800—recovering investment in just 3-4 months. Additional revenue streams include screen advertising and cup branding.
