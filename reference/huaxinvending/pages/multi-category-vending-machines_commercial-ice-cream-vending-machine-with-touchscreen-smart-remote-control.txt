Product Description.

Commercial Ice Cream Vending Machine

For Modern Retail Spaces | Smart Remote Control | Touchscreen Operation

This commercial ice cream vending machine is engineered for shopping malls, airports, scenic spots, and other high-footfall locations. With a fully automated workflow, customers can complete selection, payment, and ice cream dispensing in as fast as 15 seconds, maximizing impulse purchases and operational efficiency.

Smart Automation & Labor-Free Operation

The machine eliminates manual labor through a fully automatic process, reducing staffing costs and human error. Integrated sensors, intelligent motors, and a sealed production chamber ensure hygienic, stable, and consistent ice cream output.

Advanced Refrigeration & Long-Term Stability

Powered by a high-performance imported compressor and a large-area condenser system, this ice cream vending machine delivers fast cooling, low energy consumption, and continuous operation. It supports marathon-style production, making it suitable for peak hours without freezing cylinder issues.

  
Remote Monitoring & Intelligent Management

Through mobile and web-based dashboards, operators can monitor sales data, ingredient levels, machine status, and fault alerts in real time. Features such as one-click self-cleaning, night energy-saving mode, and automatic revenue reports significantly reduce operational workload.

Flexible Flavor Combination & High Profit Potential

Using a single base mix combined with multiple toppings and jams, the machine can create up to 59 flavor combinations. This flexibility boosts repeat purchases and allows operators to adapt menus to local preferences with minimal cost.
