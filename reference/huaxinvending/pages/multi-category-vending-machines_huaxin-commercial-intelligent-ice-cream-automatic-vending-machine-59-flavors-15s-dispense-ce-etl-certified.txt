Product Description.

Revolutionize Your Retail with the HUAXIN Smart Ice Cream Vending Machine

In today’s high-cost retail environment, traditional ice cream service faces significant challenges: large footprints, high labor costs, and slow service speeds that lead to lost sales. HUAXIN’s latest B83-B86max series solves these pain points by integrating world-class refrigeration technology with a fully automated, contactless dispensing system.

Key Performance Advantages

Ultra-Fast Production: Utilizing full-frequency conversion technology, the machine achieves initial cooling in just 5 seconds and dispenses a finished cup in as little as 15 seconds.

Space-Efficiency: Occupying only 0.91 square meters, this machine fits perfectly in high-traffic malls, airports, and schools without the burden of high rent.

Industrial-Grade Components: Equipped with a genuine 1.5HP Embraco compressor and OMRON sensors, the hardware is built for an 8-year lifespan with 24/7 stable operation.

Superior Hygiene: Built-in UV and Pasteurization systems, combined with a 2-6°C low-temperature hopper, ensure the highest food safety standards without daily manual deep-cleaning.

Smart Management and Remote Control

The proprietary Master OS allows operators to manage an entire fleet from their smartphone. You can monitor ingredient levels, track real-time sales data, and perform 95% of troubleshooting remotely. The one-touch self-cleaning feature reduces maintenance time by 80% compared to traditional machines.

Maximizing Your ROI

High Profit Margins: With a cost per cup of 1.5-1.8 RMB and a retail price of 6-10 RMB, owners enjoy a gross profit margin exceeding 65%.

Flavor Versatility: Support for 1 base mix with 3 jams and 3 toppings allows for 59 unique flavor combinations to keep customers returning.

Impulse Purchase Magnet: The 32-inch BOE touchscreen and panoramic glass window turn the production process into a visual attraction, encouraging social media sharing and repeat visits.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How many flavors can the machine produce?
By combining one liquid base with three types of jams and three toppings, you can offer up to 59 different flavor combinations.

How long does it take to serve one customer?
The entire process from payment to dispensing takes approximately 15 to 20 seconds.

What is the capacity of the machine?
The Max models feature a 20L hopper and can hold 160-180 cups, ensuring you don't run out during peak hours.

Is it difficult to clean the equipment?
No. The machine features an automated one-touch cleaning system. We recommend a full cleaning cycle 1-2 times per week.

What payment methods are supported?
The system is highly flexible, supporting coins, banknotes, credit cards, and QR codes. We can also customize the payment system for specific regional requirements.

What kind of warranty and support is provided?
We offer a 1-year warranty on the entire machine, 24/7 remote video technical support, and modular parts for easy replacement.
