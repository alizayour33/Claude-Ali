Frequently Asked Questions

 

How often does the machine need to be cleaned and refilled?

The machine features an automatic internal cleaning cycle. The mix hopper needs to be refilled based on usage; the real-time inventory tracking system will alert you when levels are low. External surfaces require routine wiping as per standard food service hygiene practices.

 

 

Can I customize the flavors and branding on the screen?

Yes, absolutely. The touchscreen interface is fully customizable. You can upload your own graphics, logos, and flavor names to match your brand identity.

 

 

What kind of ice cream mix does it use?

The B83 Series is designed to work with standard commercial liquid soft-serve ice cream mix, available from most food service distributors.

 

 

Is the machine suitable for outdoor use?

The standard model is designed for indoor or covered outdoor use. For fully exposed outdoor locations, please inquire about our specific outdoor-rated enclosures and kits.
