Why Açaí Flavor Theme? ★★★★★

Açaí-flavor soft serve helps vending operators create a more modern and attractive dessert concept. Its berry-inspired taste, visual appeal, and trendy positioning make it suitable for young consumer locations such as malls, gyms, universities, airports, and lifestyle venues. With a themed soft serve vending machine, operators can build a branded self-service dessert point, offer toppings and cashless ordering, and create a new sales point without the labor cost and pressure of a full dessert shop.

A Complete Flow from Touchscreen Order to Cup Pickup

The machine supports touchscreen ordering, cashless payment, automatic soft serve production, sauce and topping selection, and self-service cup pickup. Operators can also customize machine wrap, screen menu, product images, pricing, and payment options according to their brand and local market.
With these functions, an Açaí-flavor soft serve vending machine can work as a branded self-service dessert point, not only as a simple ice cream machine.

Simple Customer Journey

Açaí-flavor ice cream vending should be simple, fast, and visually clear for first-time customers. The whole flow can be completed by the customer without staff assistance.

Choose Açaí Flavor Menu

Customers select the Açaí-flavor soft serve, cup size, topping combination, and promotional set directly on the touchscreen.

Pay and Wait for Automatic Production

After payment, the vending machine starts production automatically. The cup moves into position, soft serve is dispensed, and the selected toppings or sauces are added according to the configured process. The whole process is visual and predictable, helping customers feel confident even on their first purchase.

Pick Up the Finished Cup

When production is finished, the customer picks up the Açaí-flavor ice cream cup from the pickup window. No staff operation is required in normal daily sales. For operators, this means a compact dessert point that can sell for long hours with lower labor involvement.

Açaí Vending Machine in a German Mall

  • 110cups sold by day
  • €3.90base price per cup
  • +€0.50per extra topping
  • B83 Maxselected machine model

An operator in Germany runs a Huaxin B83 Max as a dedicated açaí point inside a shopping mall. The machine carries the operator&#39;s own açaí wrap, a German-language pickup label and a card reader, so shoppers order, pay and collect their cup on their own. The menu is kept short: açaí soft serve at €3.90, with toppings at €0.50 each.

On a Saturday we asked the operator how the machine was doing. By 4 p.m. it had sold 110 cups, and the operator expected around 180 by the end of the day. This is one weekend day at one site, not a forecast for every location. What it does show is that mall shoppers will buy açaí soft serve from a self-service machine at €3.90 a cup.
