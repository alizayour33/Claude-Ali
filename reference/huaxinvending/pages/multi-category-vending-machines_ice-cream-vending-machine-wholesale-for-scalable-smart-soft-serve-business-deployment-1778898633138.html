Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do you support ice cream vending machine wholesale orders?

A: Yes. HUAXIN supports wholesale orders for distributors, importers, operators, investors, and regional partners. Machine configuration, price, customization, and delivery plan can be discussed according to order quantity and project needs.

Q: What is the minimum order quantity?

A: One unit can be ordered. For wholesale buyers and bulk projects, we can provide suitable configuration suggestions based on your market, location plan, and customization requirements.

Q: Can the machine be customized for my country?

A: Yes. We support customization for voltage, language system, payment method, machine appearance, logo, screen content, and OEM/ODM requirements.

Q: Which payment methods are available?

A: The machine can support bills, coins, credit cards, QR code payment, and customized payment systems according to the target market.

Q: How fast can the machine make one cup?

A: One cup can usually be produced in about 15 to 20 seconds, depending on model configuration, recipe, and operation settings.

Q: Can operators manage many machines at the same time?

A: Yes. The remote system helps operators check sales data, revenue reports, order status, temperature, ingredient level, machine status, and fault alerts across multiple machines.

Q: What locations are suitable for wholesale deployment?

A: Suitable locations include shopping malls, amusement parks, schools, campuses, airports, railway stations, cinemas, tourist attractions, parks, commercial streets, and other high-traffic areas.

Q: Is installation complicated?

A: Installation is simple. The machine requires suitable placement space, compatible power supply, and network setup if remote functions are needed. User manuals and video guidance are available.

Q: What after-sales service do you provide for wholesale buyers?

A: We provide video technical support, online remote guidance, spare parts availability, operation training, and one-year whole-machine warranty support.
