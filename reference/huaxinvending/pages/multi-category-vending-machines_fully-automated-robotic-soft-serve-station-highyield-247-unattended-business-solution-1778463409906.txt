Overcoming Scaling Barriers with Unattended Robotics
1. Eliminate the High Cost of Rising Labor

Human staff are the single biggest expense and risk in traditional retail. The Huaxin B83 is a 100% Unattended Station. It manages payments, inventory, and hygiene cycles autonomously, allowing you to manage 20+ units via a mobile App through Cloud Telemetry, effectively transforming labor costs into pure profit.

2. Superior Texture via 43% Overrun Mastery

Texture is what drives consumer loyalty. Our proprietary Air-Pump Tech achieves a 43% overrun, infusing air to create a silky, "premium-brand" mouthfeel. Commercially, this also means you get 43% more product volume from the same liquid mix, directly increasing your yield and bottom line per cup.

3. Solving the Hygiene & Sanitation Nightmare

Hygiene is non-negotiable in public spaces. Huaxin kiosks feature an Integrated UV + Pasteurization Matrix. The machine keeps the mix at a safe 2-6°C and self-sanitizes, reducing manual deep-cleaning to once per week and ensuring total food safety compliance in high-traffic airports and malls.

Technical Infrastructure for Global Reliability
Industrial-Grade Cooling

Equipped with Embraco Inverter compressors and a 5.4m² oversized condenser. This system allows the machine to maintain "Marathon output" (continuous peak-hour dispensing) without cylinder freeze-ups, even in high ambient temperatures.

Precision Motion Control

Our RK Industrial Controller integrates Omron photoelectric sensors to coordinate cup placement and dispensing arms with 0.1mm accuracy, effectively eliminating mechanical waste and maximizing uptime.
