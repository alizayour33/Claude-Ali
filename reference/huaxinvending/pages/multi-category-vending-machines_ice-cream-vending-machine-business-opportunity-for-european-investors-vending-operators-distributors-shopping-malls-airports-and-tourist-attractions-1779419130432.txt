Frequently Asked Questions
Is an ice cream vending machine a good business opportunity?

Yes. It can be a strong automated retail opportunity for operators who have access to high-traffic locations, suitable pricing, reliable refill planning, and remote machine management.

Why is the Max Series suitable for commercial operation?

The Max Series has a 20L mix cylinder, 160-cup storage, about 160–180 cup output after filling, 15–20 second serving, cashless payment support, and remote monitoring, making it suitable for higher-traffic commercial locations.

Who can start this business?

Vending operators, investors, distributors, dealers, shopping mall partners, tourism site operators, and retail businesses can start this business if they have suitable locations and operation resources.

What locations are suitable for ice cream vending?

Shopping malls, airports, train stations, schools, supermarkets, cinemas, theme parks, tourist attractions, pedestrian streets, and family entertainment centers are suitable locations.

Does the machine support cashless payment?

Yes. The Max Series can support credit card payment, QR payment, coin, bill, and customized payment systems according to local market requirements.

Can I monitor the machine remotely?

Yes. Operators can remotely monitor sales data, machine status, temperature, ingredient level, cup status, alerts, revenue reports, and operation settings.

Can the machine be customized for my brand?

Yes. Huaxin supports OEM/ODM customization, including appearance, logo, machine wrap, screen content, language, payment system, voltage, UI, and local brand presentation.

How can I calculate ROI?

ROI depends on daily cup sales, selling price, ingredient cost, cups, toppings, electricity, site fee, refill cost, maintenance, and machine uptime. Huaxin can help you build a customized estimate based on your country and target locations.
