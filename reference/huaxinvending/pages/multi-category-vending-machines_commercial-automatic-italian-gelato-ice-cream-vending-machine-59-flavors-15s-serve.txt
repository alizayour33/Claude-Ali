Product Description.

What Can It Do?
The Ultimate Automated Gelato Station** This intelligent vending machine is a complete, self-contained gelato shop. It automates the entire process: from customer selecting their preferred combination of gelato and toppings on the 32-inch touchscreen, to payment processing, cup dispensing, precision filling, and final topping application—all within a remarkable 15 seconds. It operates tirelessly 24/7, traetlorming high-footfall locations into profitable, unmanned dessert points. Whether it's a classic creamy gelato or an innovative creation with gourmet sauces and crunchy toppings, this machine delivers consistent, high-quality servings every time, capturing instant sales that traditional counters often miss.
What Makes It Exceptional? Engineering Excellence Meets Smart Innovation
Space-Saving Powerhouse
Boasting a minimal footprint of just 0.91m², the machine can be installed anywhere without the need for renovation work. Its integrated wheels enable easy repositioning, allowing operators to capitalize on high foot traffic areas flexibly.
Unmatched Core Performance
Equipped with a Brazilian-made Embraco variable-speed compressor, it delivers ultra-fast freezing in 5 seconds and maintains stable "marathon freeze" for non-stop operation. The 43% overrun rate ensures a creamy, ice-crystal-free texture that rivals offerings from premium ice cream brands.
Hygiene & Durability Built-In
A combined UV and pasteurization system, paired with low-temperature bacteriostasis in the hopper, provides comprehensive food safety guarantees. Key components including OMRON sensors and German-imported condenser fans are selected for their ability to withstand millions of reliable cycles.
Master OS Intelligent System
Beyond basic vending functionality, the system supports remote monitoring of real-time sales data, ingredient levels, and machine health status via a mobile app. Operators can implement dynamic pricing strategies, launch promotional coupons, enable automatic revenue sharing with location partners, and trigger self-cleaning cycles—all directly from their smartphones.
Flavor Variety Engine
With one base mix, three sauce options, and three dry topping choices, the machine unlocks up to 59 possible flavor combinations. This versatility caters to diverse consumer tastes, drives repeat purchases, and encourages social sharing among users.

Investing in this machine means investing in a proven, high-yield revenue generator, with the following key benefits:
Significant Cost SavingsEliminate labor costs associated with dedicated service staff. The energy-efficient design, featuring an eco-mode, reduces daily power consumption to as low as 10–15 kWh.
