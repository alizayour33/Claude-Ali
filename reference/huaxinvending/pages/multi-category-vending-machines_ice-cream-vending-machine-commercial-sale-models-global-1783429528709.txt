If you are searching for an ice cream vending machine for sale, you are probably not only looking for a machine picture or a low price. Most serious buyers want to know which model fits their location, what payment systems can be used, how the machine is shipped, whether customization is available, and how after-sales support works after import.

Huaxin has built commercial ice cream vending machines in our own factory in Dezhou, China, since 2013, and we sell directly to operators, dealers, importers, dessert brands, retail chains and location owners. The range covers B Series floor-standing machines for public commercial spaces and two countertop models, Power7 and Easy7, for counters and smaller stores. This page covers what you need to know to buy one: available models, quotation, order size, payment, production, delivery, certificates and after-sales support. B Series Floor-Standing Machines

For a vending route, shopping mall placement or distributor business, the B Series is usually the main choice. The customer orders and pays on a 32-inch touchscreen. The machine drops a cup, makes the soft serve, adds the selected sauce or topping and moves the cup to the pickup port. On the B83 Max to B86 Max, a serving takes approximately 15–20 seconds under standard operating conditions. To see each step in more detail, read how our robot ice cream vending machine makes a cup.

The machine runs one base mix at a time, and sauces and dry toppings build the menu. With compatible formulations tested on the machine, it serves soft serve, frozen yogurt, sorbet and açaí-style frozen desserts. Operators check sales, machine status, supply levels and fault alerts across sites through Master OS remote management.

Power7 and Easy7 Countertop Models

Power7 is a countertop vending machine. Customers order and pay on the machine, which makes the ice cream and hands the cup out through an enclosed pickup area. It suits shops that want self-service ice cream but have no room for a floor-standing cabinet.

Easy7 is a countertop automatic ice cream machine for staffed counters. It starts making ice cream as soon as a compatible cup or cone is placed, and payment goes through the shop’s own till or an added payment module. Cafés and dessert shops use it to add ice cream to the menu without building a dessert station.

The price of a commercial ice cream vending machine is built from the model, payment system, hygiene options such as pasteurization on compatible B Series configurations, topping system, interface languages, exterior branding and quantity. Freight and trade terms are quoted separately. Send us the following, and we will recommend a model and configuration and then quote:

· Target country and city

· Preferred machine type or usage location

· Estimated order quantity

· Required payment methods

· Voltage and plug requirements

· Whether branding or language customization is needed

What We Confirm with You Before Production

· Electrical: 220 V / 50 Hz is standard and 110 V / 60 Hz is optional. Voltage, frequency and plug type are set before the order; the machine cannot switch voltage on site.

· Payment: card, contactless, QR code and mobile wallet payments can be configured, and banknote and coin modules are set up for your currency. The machines provide an MDB interface, and a reader you choose is confirmed through integration testing. The merchant account for a card reader is opened in your name with your local provider. More detail: ice cream vending machine payment systems.

· Language and screen content: interface languages are confirmed against the current software version, and you approve the translated text.

· Mix and cups: outside China we do not supply mix, so you buy mix and cups locally. Send us the mix specification, a sample and your cup dimensions, and we will test them on the machine before you buy in volume.

· Site: the machines are designed for indoor commercial locations. Outdoor or semi-outdoor sites are assessed separately for shelter, temperature, rain, sun, dust and power.

· Certification: we check the requirements of your market against the certificates listed under Certifications by Model.

Trial Order or Multi-Unit Rollout

Orders can start with one machine. A single unit is a practical way to test a location: you learn how often it needs refilling, how your local payment setup performs and what customers buy before you commit more capital.

For formal operation across several sites, we recommend planning around a 10-unit order. Configuration, exterior design, menus and spare parts are settled once for the whole batch, and larger quantities can ship as a full container. Our guide Single-Unit Pilot or 10-Unit Rollout? walks through both routes.

Two buyers show how this often goes. A German operator watched our machines run at a trade show for several days and met our technical team, then ordered one B83 Max as a sample. After testing it in Germany for more than three months, the operator ordered a full container, and we went through several rounds of payment hardware changes together. That customer later became a Huaxin distributor. In Spain, a buyer bought two Huaxin machines and two machines from another manufacturer at the same time to compare them. After testing, the buyer reordered from us several times, moved from running their own machines to selling them, and later became our agent.

Distributor pricing depends on purchase plans, annual volume, after-sales capability and marketing investment. Regional protection may be discussed based on sales commitments and a signed cooperation agreement. For what a bulk order includes, see wholesale ice cream vending machines.

Payment, Production and Delivery
Payment Terms

Regular export orders use a deposit, with the balance paid before shipment. The deposit amount and accepted payment methods are stated in the proforma invoice and contract.

Production Lead Time

Every machine is built to order. Production typically takes about 30 days from order confirmation, and the contract sets the actual date. Exterior artwork, payment hardware and interface languages need to be approved before production starts, so sending these early keeps the schedule.

Before leaving our factory, every machine completes a 72-hour stress test and an item-by-item inspection across five areas: electrical cabinet, refrigeration system, front door, cleaning and labelling. Any issue is fixed by the responsible team and re-inspected.

Export Packing

Machines are packed in protective export packaging, with fixing, moisture protection and shock protection arranged according to the model. They must stay upright throughout transport and handling.

Shipping and Trade Terms

Most orders ship by sea, either LCL or as a full container when the quantity allows. Air or land transport has been arranged for individual projects. We can quote CIF, DDP and other Incoterms, and the quotation and contract state what the price includes. Transit time depends on route, transshipment, sailing schedule, port congestion and customs clearance.

We provide the commercial invoice, packing list and bill of lading for customs clearance. Some forwarders have their own rules for machines that contain refrigerant, so we check with the forwarder before booking. For a freight estimate, send your country, city or port, postal code, quantity and preferred trade terms. See also our guide to ice cream vending machine shipping cost.

When the Machine Arrives

A B Series machine weighs roughly 350–400 kg. Arrange unloading equipment and a clear route to the site before it lands; on CIF orders, transport from the port or warehouse to your location is arranged locally. Let the machine stand upright at its location for 24 hours before powering it on, check that the socket is earthed, and leave about 50 cm between the back of the machine and the wall.

After-Sales Support

Start-up and training. When the machine arrives, contact your Huaxin after-sales specialist, who will guide you through start-up, refilling, menu setup, cleaning and basic troubleshooting. We provide operating manuals, video tutorials and remote training. Factory training can be arranged for volume projects, distributors and local service teams.

Remote diagnosis. If a fault occurs, send us photos, videos and the fault code. On network-enabled machines we can also review the Master OS back-office data.

Video guidance. Our technicians follow up with online guidance, repair videos and parameter checks, so most routine issues are handled by the operator on site.

Spare parts. Common spare and wear parts can be ordered with the machines and shipped in the same consignment, so they are on hand when the machine reaches its location. Later orders are placed by model and part number.

On-site service. Overseas support is delivered mainly through remote diagnosis, video guidance and spare parts. In some markets, local partners or distributors provide on-site service; availability is confirmed during purchasing.

One operator in Trinidad and Tobago received a B84 after customs clearance and then worked through several start-up questions with our team. About three weeks later, the operator told us they were satisfied with the machine and the issues had been resolved.

FAQ
1. Do you have machines in stock?

We build to order rather than hold finished stock, because voltage, payment hardware, interface language and exterior are set for each market. Lead time is covered under Production Lead Time above.

2. Do I pay a franchise fee or royalty?

No. Huaxin is an equipment manufacturer. You buy the machines and run them under your own brand, and we charge no franchise fee and no royalty on your sales.

3. Can I see the machine working or visit the factory?

Yes. We can share machine photos, videos or case materials based on your country and project type; anything involving a customer is shared with that customer’s permission. You are welcome to visit our factory in Dezhou: we pick buyers up at Dezhou East high-speed rail station or Jinan Yaoqiang International Airport. If you cannot travel, we can arrange a live video factory tour.

4. Can I place the machine outdoors?

The machines are designed for indoor commercial locations. Covered outdoor or semi-outdoor sites need a separate assessment of shade, temperature, rain, dust and power supply before ordering. Hot weather raises the cooling load, so keep ventilation clearance around the machine and avoid direct sunlight.

5. Is exclusive distribution available?

Regional protection or exclusivity may be discussed based on sales commitments and a market development plan, and it requires a signed cooperation agreement.
