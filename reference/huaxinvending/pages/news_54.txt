Global First! Empowering Smart Entertainment—Huaxin Tech Debuts at 41st CAAPA China

March 19th marked the grand opening of the 41st CAAPA China (Beijing) International Amusement Equipment Expo (Beijing International Tourism & Leisure Industry Expo) at the Beijing National Convention Center. As the world’s first creator of smart ice cream machines, Huaxin Tech stole the spotlight with its cutting-edge innovations, injecting fresh tech-driven energy into the entertainment and leisure industry.

 This year’s expo, themed “Tech Powers Entertainment, Innovation Leads the Future,” brought together over 500 global industry leaders across a 100,000-square-meter exhibition space. Huaxin Tech’s star product—the desktop fully automated ice cream machine—became the talk of the show. Its “15-second instant serving, one-touch self-cleaning, and app remote control” features wowed theme park operators, resort managers, and industry experts alike.
 



 

Designed for high-traffic venues like amusement parks and tourist hotspots, Huaxin’s machines not only deliver fresh, on-the-spot treats but also slash labor and time costs for operators.

 
 
  
  

The first day of the expo was a smash hit for Huaxin. Their “Free Ice Cream Experience Zone” had visitors lining up for 100 meters. All 12 demo machines sold out by noon—even the display units were snapped up. Deals were signed on the spot, including a trial order for 5 machines from a major North China amusement park. “The speed and cleaning efficiency blew us away,” said the park’s manager. By day one, over 300 clients had visited the booth, and some models are now backordered for three months due to surging demand.

 
  

The sold-out frenzy, while unexpected, proved customers’ trust in Huaxin’s tech. Many had followed the product online but were convinced after seeing the 15-second magic in person. The team also connected with 100+ international buyers from Europe, Southeast Asia, and beyond—hinting at even bigger orders ahead.
 From “world-first” to “world-leading,” Huaxin Tech is doubling down on innovation. The sold-out success is just the beginning. The company is now racing to meet delivery deadlines, helping clients worldwide tap into the smart tech revolution. Next up? Expanding into more scenarios and delivering faster, smarter experiences for everyone.

Author's Introduction: Huaxin Co., Ltd. has manufactured commercial automated ice cream vending machines in Dezhou, China, since 2013, with machines shipped to more than 50 countries and regions. Each unit completes a 72-hour stress test before shipment, and certifications such as CE, IECEE CB, NSF/ANSI 6 and ETL vary by model.
