How do ice cream vending machines stay cold?
Date：2025-11-07 Author：Huaxin

Ice cream vending machines rely on the refrigeration cycle—compression, condensation, expansion, and evaporation of refrigerants—to keep ice cream frozen. Advanced technologies like Huaxin’s 5-second rapid cooling and marathon freezing power boost efficiency and stability. Regular cleaning of key components and inspections of refrigerants and mechanical parts ensure continuous, reliable operation, delivering convenient, high-quality ice cream.


Ice cream vending machines provide great convenience for us to get ice cream. But how do these machines work to keep the ice cream in a perfectly frozen state? Let's delve into the relevant technologies and principles.
Refrigeration cycle: the core of refrigeration
The refrigeration cycle is the cornerstone for ice cream vending machines to maintain low temperatures. The refrigerant absorbs heat when evaporating and releases heat when condensing, forming a continuous cooling cycle.
Compression stage: The compressor is the "heart" of the cycle. It sucks in low-temperature and low-pressure refrigerant vapor and compresses it into high-temperature and high-pressure vapor. This process increases both temperature and pressure, preparing the refrigerant to release heat.
Condensation stage: The condenser is usually a network of coils with a cooling fan. The high-temperature and high-pressure vapor flows into the condenser, releases heat to the surrounding air, and condenses into high-pressure liquid.
Expansion stage: The high-pressure liquid then passes through the expansion valve, where its pressure and temperature drop significantly, turning into low-temperature and low-pressure liquid.
 Evaporation stage: The evaporator is the component that directly contacts the ice cream storage area. The low-temperature and low-pressure liquid enters the evaporator, absorbs heat from the ice cream mix inside the evaporator, turning the mix into a semi-solid state, and the refrigerant evaporates into low-temperature and low-pressure vapor. These vapors then return to the compressor, and the cycle repeats indefinitely.
Self-developed core refrigeration technologies adopted by vending machines
To improve refrigeration performance, efficiency, and reliability, some machines adopt advanced self-developed technologies. Take Huaxin's intelligent ice cream vending machine as an example:
5-second rapid refrigeration technology: This technology shortens the time from startup to the optimal temperature for ice cream dispensing to only 5 seconds, while the industry average is more than 15 seconds. It can reduce the waiting time for ice cream production, cut down customer queuing time by 40% during peak hours, and improve operational efficiency and user experience.
Marathon freezing power technology: This technology is designed to ensure stable operation in high-temperature environments. It has undergone rigorous endurance testing - continuously producing 1373 cups within 12 hours (an industry first). It can eliminate machine shutdowns during sales surges, thereby avoiding revenue losses and customer dissatisfaction.
Cold quantity micro-control refrigeration technology: By precisely adjusting the flow of refrigerant, it can prevent the machine from freezing the cylinder.
Ice cream core technology: Independently developed by Huaxin Technology, this technology combines the Taylor principle and Japanese engineering technology. It can ensure the taste and stable output of ice cream, meeting high standards for flavor and production consistency.
Maintenance measures to ensure continuous refrigeration
Clean the condenser and evaporator regularly. The accumulation of dust and dirt on these components will hinder heat exchange. Cleaning them monthly, or more frequently in dusty environments, can ensure efficient heat release and absorption.
Daily inspections:
Refrigerant stock: Low refrigerant levels will reduce refrigeration capacity. Check the refrigerant stock quarterly, and repair leaks or replenish refrigerant if necessary.
Mechanical components: Compressors, condenser fans, and valves are key components for the cycle operation. Check monthly for abnormal noise, vibration, or overheating, and promptly repair or replace damaged parts to avoid malfunctions.
 In conclusion, ice cream vending machines maintain low temperatures through a combination of basic refrigeration cycles, innovative self-developed technologies, and careful maintenance, ensuring that every serving of ice cream has an excellent taste.
