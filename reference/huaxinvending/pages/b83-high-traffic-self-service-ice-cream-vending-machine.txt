FAQ
What payment methods can be integrated with the ice cream vending machine?

The machine can support card, NFC, QR code, and other payment solutions depending on the target country and payment provider. Huaxin can assist with payment integration based on your local market requirements.

How often does the machine need to be refilled?

Refill frequency depends on daily sales volume and menu configuration. Operators mainly need to monitor ice cream mix, cups, toppings, and other consumables. The remote management system can help track machine status and inventory reminders.

How is the ice cream vending machine cleaned?

The machine is designed for routine commercial cleaning and maintenance. Operators can follow the provided cleaning procedures for the food-contact system, dispensing area, ingredient containers, and other components that require regular sanitation.

Can I manage multiple ice cream vending machines remotely?

Yes. Master OS supports multi-machine management, allowing operators to check machine status, sales information, inventory reminders, and operational alerts across different locations.

What do I need to prepare before installing the machine?

You should confirm installation space, electrical requirements, network connectivity, payment setup, ingredient supply, and the operating environment. Huaxin can provide installation and operation guidance before deployment.

Does Huaxin provide after-sales and technical support?

Yes. Huaxin provides operation guidance, remote technical support, troubleshooting assistance, and spare parts support to help operators maintain their machines after installation.
