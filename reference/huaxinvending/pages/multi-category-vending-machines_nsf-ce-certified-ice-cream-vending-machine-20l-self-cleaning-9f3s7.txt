Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
What do ETL and CE certifications mean for this ice cream vending machine? +

ETL certification ensures all parts in contact with food (like the 304 stainless steel hopper and food-grade hoses) meet global food safety standards, so customers can eat with confidence. CE certification is mandatory for EU market access, proving the machine complies with EU safety, health, and environmental regulations—ideal for cross-border business.

How many cups can the 20L capacity make, and how often do I need to refill it? +

A full 20L hopper can make 160-180 standard-sized ice cream cups. For high-traffic spots like shopping malls (50-200 cups/day), you only need to refill 1-4 times a week. The machine’s cloud platform sends real-time low-stock alerts to your mobile, so you’ll never miss a refill.

How does the self-cleaning function work, and how often is it needed? +

The machine features one-click automatic cleaning: just add cleaning solution, press "clean" on the touchscreen or app, and it completes the entire process in 15 minutes—no manual disassembly required. We recommend cleaning 1-2 times a week, which saves over 8 hours of labor monthly compared to traditional machines.

Can the machine really run 24/7 without supervision? What about energy consumption? +

Yes! It’s fully automated: customers order via touchscreen, pay, and get ice cream in 15 seconds—no staff needed. The Embraco变频 compressor reduces energy consumption by 40% vs traditional models, with an average daily power use of 10-15 kWh. It also has a "night mode" that lowers screen brightness and adjusts cooling to save more electricity.

What if the machine breaks down? Is there after-sales support? +

The machine comes with a 1-year full warranty. 95% of faults (like freezing cylinder or sensor issues) can be solved remotely via app/ video guidance—our engineers provide 24/7 support. For hardware issues, we have spare parts in 26+ exported countries, and local technicians can arrive within 24-48 hours. We also offer lifetime free software updates.

Can I customize the machine’s voltage, language, or payment methods? +

Yes! We offer OEM/ODM customization: voltage (220V/110V/others), 20+ languages (English, German, French, etc.), and payment systems (adapt to local methods like Alipay, PayPal, or local cards). You can also customize the machine’s appearance with your brand logo or IP stickers.
