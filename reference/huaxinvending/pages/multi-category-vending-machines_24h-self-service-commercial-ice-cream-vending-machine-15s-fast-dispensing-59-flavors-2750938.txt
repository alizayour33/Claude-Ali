Product Description.

Product Introduction

This 24H self-service commercial ice cream vending machine is developed by HUAXIN with 13 years of industry experience and 11 generations of upgrades. It holds multiple international certifications and dominates 70% of the domestic market, exported to more than 30 countries. It fully solves the pain points of traditional ice cream equipment and creates a stable unmanned retail solution.

Core Features

1. Fast Cooling & Production: Adopts Embraco variable frequency compressor with 5-second ultra-fast cooling and 15-second cup output, supporting 12 hours continuous high-volume output.

2. Compact & Flexible: Occupies only 0.91㎡, with movable casters, plug-and-play without installation or modification.

3. Rich Flavors: Combines 1 milk base, 3 jams and 3 toppings to make 59 flavors, with 43% expansion rate and no ice residue.

4. Intelligent System: Equipped with Master OS, supporting APP remote monitoring, one-key cleaning, fault self-detection and automatic sub-accounting.

5. Sanitary Security: Built-in UV and pasteurization, 2-6℃ low-temperature antibacterial, closed and contact-free production.

Pain Points Solved

- Reduces space occupation and placement limits.

- Eliminates manual labor and cuts operating costs.

- Improves impulse purchase conversion with fast output.

- Lowers cleaning frequency and maintenance difficulty.

Application Scenarios & Business Model

Suitable for shopping malls, scenic spots, schools, airports, railway stations and commercial streets. Single cup cost 1.5-1.8 CNY, selling price 6-10 CNY, gross profit rate over 65%. High-quality locations can achieve 200 cups per day, with monthly net profit of 12,000 CNY and payback within 3-4 months.

Comparison with Traditional Machines

Traditional machines require manual operation, 2 minutes per cup, daily disassembly cleaning, large size and low efficiency. This machine is fully automatic, 15 seconds per cup, weekly cleaning, energy saving 40%, small size and high output.
