Frequently Asked Questions
How many flavors can the machine provide?

The machine supports up to 59 flavor combinations using one milk base with multiple syrups and toppings.

How long does it take to produce one ice cream?

The average production time is approximately 15 to 20 seconds per serving.

Does the machine require daily cleaning?

No. The system includes automatic self-cleaning functionality. Weekly cleaning 1-2 times is generally sufficient.

Can the machine operate without internet?

Yes. The vending machine can sell products offline normally. Internet access enables remote monitoring and management functions.

Can the machine be customized?

Yes. Custom branding, language systems, payment systems, voltage specifications, and appearance customization are supported.

Where can this machine be installed?

It is suitable for shopping malls, airports, schools, commercial streets, cinemas, amusement parks, tourist attractions, and other high-footfall locations.
