Product Description.

Commercial Ice Cream Machine Built for Modern Vending Business

Designed specifically for commercial operators, this ice cream machine combines automation, hygiene, and profitability. It replaces traditional labor-intensive ice cream production with a fully automated vending process, enabling operators to maximize sales in high-footfall locations.

Compact Design, High Commercial Value

With a footprint of less than 1㎡, this commercial ice cream machine fits easily into shopping malls, cinemas, airports, and supermarkets. Plug-and-play installation allows rapid deployment without renovation.

Fully Automatic Operation, Zero Labor Dependency

From order selection to payment and dispensing, the entire process is automated. A single cup is produced in as fast as 15 seconds, reducing wait times and converting impulse demand into actual sales.

Visual Production, Higher Conversion

A transparent production window allows customers to watch the ice cream being made, increasing trust, engagement, and social sharing, which directly drives secondary consumption.

Food Safety & Hygiene for Commercial Standards

The machine adopts a closed dispensing structure combined with UV sterilization and low-temperature material storage. Automatic cleaning functions reduce daily maintenance and ensure compliance with international food safety standards.

 
Intelligent Control & Remote Management

Operators can remotely monitor sales data, ingredient levels, machine status, and revenue reports via mobile devices. Fault self-detection enables rapid troubleshooting, with most issues resolved remotely.

Flexible Flavor Expansion for Higher Profits

Using a single base mix combined with multiple toppings and sauces, this commercial ice cream machine supports dozens of flavor combinations, increasing repeat purchases and adapting to local market preferences.

  
Global Certifications & Commercial Reliability

Certified with CE, ETL, ETL, and RoHS standards, this machine is trusted by operators worldwide. Industrial-grade components ensure long service life, stable output, and low operational noise.

Customization & Branding Support

Support OEM/ODM customization including appearance, payment systems, voltage, language interface, and branding graphics. Perfect for franchise chains, brand collaborations, and large-scale commercial deployment.

This commercial ice cream machine is not just equipment—it is a scalable vending business solution designed to deliver stable revenue, reduced costs, and long-term growth.
