Profit from the Bar Corner: How Can a Fully Automatic Ice Cream Machine Increase Customer Unit Price by 30%?
Profit from the Bar Corner: How Can a Fully Automatic Ice Cream Machine Increase Customer Unit Price by 30%?
Date：2025-11-24 Author：Huaxin

This article addresses the pain point of low store space utilization under high rental costs, and analyzes how fully automatic ice cream machines, with their compact size, top exhaust design, and multi-functionality, become the ultimate tool to improve store efficiency (sales per unit area). Through real scenarios, we will demonstrate how, without increasing staff or adding complex operations, these machines can effectively utilize idle corners to generate additional revenue and significantly boost the customer unit price.


For friends running stores, especially those in popular business districts, there's a clear calculation in mind: every square meter of the store is consuming real money in rent. That 0.5-square-meter corner at the bar—will you place a decorative item there, or a "silent employee" that can continuously make money?
This choice may be quietly affecting your profit statement. Today, we won't talk about grand theories; instead, we'll discuss how to turn that unremarkable little space into the most dynamic "profit corner" in your store.
Calculate the "Space Account": Your Corner Is More Valuable Than You Think
Let's start with a simple calculation. Suppose the monthly rent for your store is 5,000 Euros, and the total area is 50 square meters. Then the monthly cost per square meter is 100 Euros, and the monthly cost for a 0.5-square-meter corner is 50 Euros.
If you place a fully automatic ice cream machine here, selling 30 cups per day at 3 Euros per cup, it can generate an additional monthly turnover of 2,700 Euros. Using a space cost of 50 Euros to drive a profit of 2,700 Euros—this "space return on investment" is surprisingly high.
Top Exhaust Design: The Invisible Contributor to Spatial Freedom
Why do many traditional devices dare not be placed at the front desk? Because poor air circulation may cause the machine to overheat, and even worse, hot air blowing directly at customers would ruin their experience.
The top exhaust design of the "Huaxin EASY 7" is tailor-made for front desk scenarios. This means:
It works perfectly against the wall: Hot air rises upward, so it doesn't rely on backside heat dissipation and won't damage the wall.
It fits seamlessly into the bar counter: It can be integrated directly with your operation counter, becoming part of your workflow.
You can place it anywhere with peace of mind: There's no need to re-plan the entire store layout just to find a spot for it. This freedom of placement is a lifesaver for store owners with limited space.
Multi-Functional: Filling the Gaps in Business Hours
If a machine can only do one thing, its value is limited. However, a smart device knows how to "arrange its working hours".
10 AM: It can make smooth milkshakes to pair with breakfast sets.
2 PM: It can produce refreshing smoothies, the perfect pick-me-up to beat the afternoon slump.
7 PM: It steadily serves high-quality ice cream, the ideal dessert after dinner.
By adjusting the "master-level freezing curve", it can make a variety of frozen treats. This means that with the cost of one device and the space of one corner, you can launch three potential new products at the same time. It effectively extends the product sales period, keeping this "corner employee" busy from morning to night.
Truly "Zero-Addition" Operation: No Extra Staff Needed
You might be wondering: If I add a new product line, do I have to hire more people? Absolutely not.
Its "one-click production" and "intelligent robotic arm" design allow existing staff to make a cup of ice cream in just 10 seconds. It doesn't increase operational complexity; instead, it simplifies the production process.
Not to mention its remote control function. The store manager can check sales volume, set parameters, and even turn the machine on or off remotely via a mobile phone. Managing it is far more hassle-free than managing an employee.
In the end, in modern retail and catering industries, competition has become so refined that it extends to the meticulous operation of every inch of space. That corner you've overlooked is precisely a profit unit waiting to be activated.
When you introduce a fully automatic ice cream machine, you're not just getting a new product. What you're getting is:
A space magician: Using the smallest floor area to create a new growth driver for revenue.
A time filler: Covering multiple sales periods throughout the day to improve equipment utilization and store efficiency.
An efficiency booster: No extra staff needed, simplified operations, and smoother business operations.
After all, when running a store, many times you don't lose to competitors—you lose to your own rigid mindset. You always think, "My store is too small to fit it" or "The operation is too complicated to handle". But the bosses who really know how to make money are best at finding infinite business opportunities within limited space.
