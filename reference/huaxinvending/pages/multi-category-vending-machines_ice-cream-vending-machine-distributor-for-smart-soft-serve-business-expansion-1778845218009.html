Key Features That Help Distributors Sell More Confidently

Fast Automatic Production

Customers can complete selection, payment, and production through a self-service process. The machine can produce one cup in about 15 to 20 seconds, helping reduce waiting time and improve conversion during peak traffic.

Remote Management System

Operators can remotely check temperature, ingredient status, order data, sales reports, machine status, and revenue performance. This is especially useful for distributors supporting multi-location customers.

Multiple Flavor Combinations

One milk base can be combined with toppings and jam options to create multiple flavor choices, improving repeat purchases and helping operators build a differentiated dessert menu.

Visual Production Window

The visible production process creates a stronger sense of freshness, technology, and entertainment. It helps attract attention in commercial locations and encourages social sharing.

Food Safety Design

The machine uses a closed production process, low-temperature ingredient preservation, ultraviolet and pasteurization support, and food-grade contact materials to help operators maintain reliable hygiene standards.

Customizable for Local Markets

Voltage, payment method, language, exterior design, logo, screen content, and system configuration can be customized to support different countries, operators, and distributor sales models.
