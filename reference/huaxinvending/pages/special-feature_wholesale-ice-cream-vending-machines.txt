Unmanned: Suitable for long-term operation needs of amusement parks, without the need for additional personnel.

Fast cup dispensing: 15 seconds to dispense cups, avoiding tourists queuing up and losing out.

Visual appeal: The panoramic window displays the production process, attracting children and young tourists to take photos and spread the message.

Remote management: Administrators can view sales and device status in real-time through their mobile phones, without the need for frequent inspections.

Compact footprint: Only 0.91 square meters, does not damage the park landscape, and can be flexibly placed.

Energy saving and silent: The noise level is less than 45 decibels and does not affect the park environment.

Health and safety: Closed production+UV sterilization, in compliance with outdoor hygiene standards.

Support for account splitting: It can automatically split accounts with park management for smoother cooperation.

University campus - high-frequency consumption engine for young customers
