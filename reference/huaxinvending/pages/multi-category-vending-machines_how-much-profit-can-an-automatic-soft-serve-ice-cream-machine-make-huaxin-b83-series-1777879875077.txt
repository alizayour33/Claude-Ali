How Does an Automatic Ice Cream Machine Solve Your Business Pain Points?
1. Is Labor Eating Your Profits?

Traditional shops require 2+ staff. Huaxin B83 is a fully robotic kiosk that handles payments, dispensing, and self-cleaning. One person can manage 10 machines via a smartphone App, effectively cutting labor costs by 100%.

2. Worried About Food Safety?

Cleaning is usually a nightmare. Our built-in UV + Pasteurization system ensures your mix stays fresh in the 2-6°C refrigerated hopper. The closed, touchless window prevents any human contamination, meeting CE/ETL hygiene standards.

3. Need Consistent Quality (Overrun)?

Customers hate icy textures. Our 43% overrun air-pump technology achieves a silky, "DQ-style" creaminess. By infusing the perfect amount of air, you also get more volume from less raw material, increasing your profit per cup.
