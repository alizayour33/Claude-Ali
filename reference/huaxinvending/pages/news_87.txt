Shocking Debut! Industry First!Ice Cream Vending Machine Smashes Extreme Challenge-Making 1,000 Cups in Just 8H 20M
Date：2025-06-24 Author：Huaxin

This competition is about ice cream machine 12-hour endurance challenge, an industrial challenge launched by Huaxin. Huaxin Co., Ltd is the leader of intelligent ice cream machines. In the period of 8 hours 20 minutes, the soft ice cream vending machine successfully exceeded the 1,000-cup mark! The total number of cups produced reached a staggering 1,373 cups!

This competition is about ice cream machine 12-hour endurance challenge, an industrial challenge launched by Huaxin. Huaxin Co., Ltd is the leader of intelligent ice cream machines. As a core manufacturer, Huaxin Co., Ltd has established a comprehensive production and R&D base covering 15,000 square meter. The company can produce up to 6,000 units annually, and its ice cream machines have been exported to nearly 26 countries and 3,000 regions all over the world. That’s why we have confidence and strength to face this extreme challenge.

 
Breakthrough Debut

Our company dares to be the first in the industry! Huaxin Technology launched the industry's first ice cream vending machine 12-hour extreme dispense challenge on June 23, 2025 in Shandong’ Ginza Shopping Mall! This is not a simulation, not laboratory data, but a real shopping mall environment of hardcore combat!

 

Challenge Result: Shocking the whole scene

Goal: Our ice cream vending machine aims to complete the production of 1,373 cups of ice cream in 12 hours.

Results: In the period of 8 hours 20 minutes, the soft ice cream vending machine successfully exceeded the 1,000-cup mark! The efficiency far exceeded expectations!

End of the event: the total number of cups produced reached a staggering 1,373 cups!

Core indicator: zero failure throughout the whole process! Our soft ice cream vending machine is stable and reliable, and withstood the test of ultra-high intensity!
 

Site Direct: Popularity Explosion

The challenge site was swarming with people, and consumers were enthusiastic, clamoring to experience the ice cold brought about by this “extreme speed”! The long queue is the greatest recognition of our products.
 

Milestones

This is not only a successful marketing campaign, but also an important breakthrough in the field of soft ice cream vending machine! We used real operational data to prove that Huaxin soft ice cream vending machines have the excellent ability to cope with peak traffic and continue to serve food stably and efficiently, setting a new benchmark for the industry!

Looking into the future

The success of the extreme challenge is just the beginning! Huaxin Co., Ltd. will continue to innovate and bring this high efficiency and reliability to more consumers, making it a reality to enjoy high quality ice cream anytime, anywhere!

References / Sources

Author's Introduction: Huaxin Co., Ltd. has manufactured commercial automated ice cream vending machines in Dezhou, China, since 2013, with machines shipped to more than 50 countries and regions. Each unit completes a 72-hour stress test before shipment, and certifications such as CE, IECEE CB, NSF/ANSI 6 and ETL vary by model.
