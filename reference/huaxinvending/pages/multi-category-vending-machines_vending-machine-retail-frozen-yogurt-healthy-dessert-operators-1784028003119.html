Frozen yogurt customers are often looking for more than a traditional ice cream purchase. They may be attracted by yogurt-based flavors, customizable toppings, portion-controlled servings, modern branding, and a convenient self-service experience.

The Huaxin B86 frozen yogurt vending machine is designed around this type of retail environment. It combines automatic preparation, cashless payment, a large touchscreen, optional topping systems, and remote machine management in one commercial vending platform.

Instead of requiring a staffed yogurt counter, the machine allows customers to select a product, complete payment, watch the preparation process, and collect the finished cup independently. This makes it suitable for locations that want to introduce a frozen yogurt menu without building a full dessert shop or keeping employees beside the equipment throughout the day.

A Frozen Yogurt Concept, Not Just Another Soft Serve Machine

Although frozen yogurt and soft serve can use similar freezing and dispensing systems, the way they are presented to customers is different.

A frozen yogurt project usually places more emphasis on:

  • Yogurt-based product formulas;
  • Fruit-inspired menu combinations;
  • Granola, nuts, dried fruit, and sauce options;
  • Customizable dessert cups;
  • Clean, modern visual branding;
  • Younger and wellness-oriented consumer groups.

The nutritional profile of the finished product depends on the selected frozen yogurt mix, serving size, sugar content, and toppings. Operators therefore remain in control of the actual product formula and can select ingredients that match their market positioning.

The machine provides the preparation and retail platform. The operator determines whether the menu is positioned as a classic frozen yogurt dessert, a fruit-and-granola yogurt cup, a premium indulgent product, or a lighter snack option.

B86 as the Primary Frozen Yogurt Vending Model

The B86 is recommended as the main model for this page because its full-height cabinet, 32-inch touchscreen, visible preparation area, and customizable exterior give operators enough space to build a recognizable frozen yogurt concept.

Its large front panel can display:

  • Frozen yogurt product photos;
  • Ingredient and topping information;
  • Promotional videos;
  • Seasonal menus;
  • Brand stories;
  • Ordering instructions;
  • Cross-promotions with gyms, campuses, or shopping centers.

The visible preparation window also lets customers watch the automatic process. This adds interaction to the purchase and makes the machine more noticeable in open commercial areas.

B83 Max, B84, B85, and B86 Max can be supplied as alternative exterior designs based on the same vending platform. Operators can choose the cabinet appearance that best fits their brand, location, and rollout plan without changing the basic frozen yogurt business concept.

One Frozen Yogurt Base, Multiple Menu Combinations

The machine uses a single base-mix cylinder. It cannot hold several different frozen yogurt base formulas at the same time.

However, one well-selected frozen yogurt base can support a much broader menu when combined with toppings and sauces.

Possible menu concepts include:

  • Original frozen yogurt with granola;
  • Strawberry sauce frozen yogurt;
  • Mango sauce frozen yogurt;
  • Frozen yogurt with chopped nuts;
  • Yogurt with dried fruit and granola;
  • Chocolate sauce and nut combinations;
  • Seasonal fruit-inspired menus;
  • Kids’ yogurt cups with colorful dry toppings.

Depending on the selected configuration, the machine can be equipped with dry-topping and sauce modules. Suitable ingredients may include free-flowing granola pieces, chopped nuts, dried fruit pieces, cereal-style toppings, and fruit-based or dessert sauces.

Ingredient size, moisture, stickiness, and flow characteristics must be tested before commercial use. Very large, fresh, wet, or easily clumping ingredients may not be suitable for automatic dispensing.

This menu structure gives operators more product variety without requiring several frozen yogurt cylinders or a large back-of-house preparation area.

Simple Self-Service Customer Journey

The B86 provides a clear automated ordering process.

1. Select the Frozen Yogurt Product

Customers use the 32-inch touchscreen to view the menu, product images, prices, and available topping combinations.

Operators can organize products by size, topping combination, seasonal promotion, or target customer group. Multiple languages can also be configured for international locations.

2. Complete Payment

The machine can be configured for local payment requirements, including:

  • Credit and debit cards;
  • NFC and contactless payment;
  • QR-code payment;
  • Mobile wallet solutions;
  • Coins or banknotes where required.

The final payment configuration depends on the destination country and the selected payment provider.

3. Automatic Preparation

After payment is confirmed, the machine automatically releases a cup, dispenses the frozen yogurt, adds the selected sauce or topping configuration, and transfers the finished cup to the pickup area.

A standard serving can be completed in approximately 15–20 seconds, helping reduce queues in busy commercial locations.

4. Collect the Finished Cup

The customer collects the frozen yogurt from the protected pickup area without requiring assistance from an employee.

The full process is designed to be easy for first-time users and suitable for unattended or lightly supervised retail environments.

Suitable Locations for Frozen Yogurt Retail

Gyms and Fitness Centers

Frozen yogurt can be positioned as a convenient post-workout dessert or refreshment. Operators can select their own yogurt formula and build menus using granola, nuts, or fruit-based sauces.

The machine can be placed in reception areas, member lounges, leisure zones, or shared commercial spaces without requiring a full food counter.

Schools and University Campuses

Students are familiar with touchscreen ordering and cashless payment. A self-service frozen yogurt machine can be installed in cafeterias, student centers, libraries, dormitory common areas, or recreation facilities.

Menu pricing, portion size, operating periods, and product availability can be adjusted remotely.

Shopping Malls

Malls provide strong impulse-purchase opportunities and a broad customer mix. The B86 can operate near cinemas, family areas, food courts, escalator exits, leisure spaces, or fashion retail zones.

Its screen and exterior can be customized to create a standalone frozen yogurt brand rather than appearing as a generic vending machine.

Lifestyle and Family Entertainment Venues

Bowling centers, trampoline parks, indoor playgrounds, cinemas, gaming venues, tourist attractions, and family entertainment centers can use the machine to add a dessert category without opening another staffed counter.

Premium Commercial Locations

Hotels, office campuses, airports, transportation hubs, private clubs, and mixed-use developments can use customized branding and cashless payment to create a more polished self-service dessert experience.

Cashless Payment for International Markets

Frozen yogurt vending is especially suitable for locations where customers already use cards, phones, or QR codes for small purchases.

Huaxin can configure the payment hardware and communication interfaces according to the target market. Payment selection should be confirmed before production because acquiring systems, terminals, certifications, and communication protocols vary by country.

For operators deploying multiple machines, a consistent payment setup also makes transaction management, reconciliation, and location expansion easier.

Available functions can include:

  • Daily and historical sales data;

  • Machine operating status;
  • Ingredient-level information;
  • Cup availability;
  • Temperature monitoring;

This is particularly useful for operators working with several gyms, campuses, malls, or franchise locations.

Instead of checking every machine manually, the operations team can identify which location needs refilling, cleaning, or technical attention and organize service routes more efficiently.

Cleaning and Routine Maintenance

Frozen yogurt equipment requires a clear cleaning routine because the base mixture passes through food-contact components.

The B86 includes a one-touch cleaning function that simplifies internal rinsing and routine cleaning. Removable food-contact parts and sauce pathways should also be cleaned according to the operator’s schedule, the ingredient supplier’s instructions, and local food-service requirements.

A typical operating routine should include:

  • Checking the base-mix condition;
  • Cleaning the dispensing area;
  • Wiping the touchscreen and pickup area;
  • Inspecting topping containers;
  • Running the cleaning program;
  • Cleaning removable food-contact components;
  • Checking seals, tubes, and dispensing nozzles;
  • Recording cleaning and refilling activity.

When changing to a different frozen yogurt base, the remaining material must be removed and the system cleaned before the new formula is added. This helps avoid flavor mixing and keeps the product output consistent.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. What type of frozen yogurt mix can be used?

Operators can use a commercial frozen yogurt mix that is suitable for automatic freezing and dispensing equipment. Both ready-to-use liquid mix and correctly prepared powder mix may be considered, but the formula should be tested for viscosity, freezing performance, sweetness, acidity, and finished texture before commercial operation.

2. Can the machine automatically add toppings?

Yes. Depending on the selected configuration, the machine can dispense dry toppings and sauces. Suitable options may include granola, chopped nuts, dried fruit pieces, cereal-style toppings, chocolate sauce, and fruit-based sauces. The ingredients must have suitable particle size and flow characteristics.

3. Can it dispense fresh fruit?

Fresh fruit with high moisture content is generally more difficult to store and dispense through a standard automatic topping module. Dried fruit, fruit pieces specifically prepared for dispensing, or fruit-based sauces are more practical options. Fresh fruit can also be added separately by staff in a supervised retail concept.

4. Can several frozen yogurt flavors be loaded at the same time?

No. The machine has one base-mix cylinder and supports one frozen yogurt base at a time. Different menu products are created by combining that base with different sauces and dry toppings.

5. How do operators change the frozen yogurt flavor?

The remaining base mix should first be removed. The food-contact system must then be rinsed and cleaned before the new frozen yogurt formula is added. A test serving should be prepared to confirm texture and dispensing performance before sales resume.

6. How often does the machine need cleaning?

The machine includes a one-touch cleaning function to simplify routine work. The actual cleaning schedule depends on the frozen yogurt formula, operating hours, ambient conditions, and local food-service requirements. Dispensing nozzles, topping containers, the pickup area, and removable food-contact parts should be inspected and cleaned regularly.

7. Which locations are most suitable?

Recommended locations include shopping malls, fitness centers, universities, schools, family entertainment venues, cinemas, tourist attractions, office campuses, airports, hotels, and other modern commercial environments with young or convenience-oriented customers.

8. Does it support cashless payment?

Yes. The machine can support credit cards, debit cards, NFC, QR-code payment, and compatible mobile payment systems. Coins and banknotes can also be configured where required.

9. Can I monitor several machines remotely?

Yes. Operators can remotely review sales, machine status, ingredient information, alerts, and other operational data. This is useful for managing machines across several gyms, campuses, malls, or cities.

10. Can the exterior and menu be customized?

Yes. Huaxin supports customization of the machine graphics, brand colors, logo, touchscreen interface, languages, payment system, voltage, product images, and promotional content.
