Why Açaí Flavor Theme?
★★★★★

Açaí-flavor soft serve helps vending operators create a more modern and attractive dessert concept. Its berry-inspired taste, visual appeal, and trendy positioning make it suitable for young consumer locations such as malls, gyms, universities, airports, and lifestyle venues.
With a themed soft serve vending machine, operators can build a branded self-service dessert point, offer toppings and cashless ordering, and create a new sales point without the labor cost and pressure of a full dessert shop.

A Complete Flow from Touchscreen Order to Cup Pickup

The machine supports touchscreen ordering, cashless payment, automatic soft serve production, sauce and topping selection, and self-service cup pickup. Operators can also customize machine wrap, screen menu, product images, pricing, and payment options according to their brand and local market.
With these functions, an Açaí-flavor soft serve vending machine can work as a branded self-service dessert point, not only as a simple ice cream machine.

Simple Customer Journey

Açaí-flavor ice cream vending should be simple, fast, and visually clear for first-time customers. The whole flow can be completed by the customer without staff assistance.

Choose Açaí Flavor Menu

Customers select the Açaí-flavor soft serve, cup size, topping combination, and promotional set directly on the touchscreen.

Pay and Wait for Automatic Production

After payment, the vending machine starts production automatically. The cup moves into position, soft serve is dispensed, and the selected toppings or sauces are added according to the configured process. The whole process is visual and predictable, helping customers feel confident even on their first purchase.

Pick Up the Finished Cup

When production is finished, the customer picks up the Açaí-flavor ice cream cup from the pickup window. No staff operation is required in normal daily sales. For operators, this means a compact dessert point that can sell for long hours with lower labor involvement.

FAQS
Can the machine make real açaí ice cream?
Yes. The machine makes açaí soft serve from an açaí mix: ice cream powder mixed with water, a ready-to-use liquid mix, or açaí jam combined with a soft serve base. The taste depends on the mix you choose. In Germany, an operator already sells açaí soft serve from a B83 Max in a shopping mall.
Do you supply açaí mix?
Not outside China. Most operators buy açaí mix from a local supplier or use their own recipe. If you already have a mix, we can test it on the machine for you before you order.
Can one machine sell açaí and regular ice cream at the same time?
No. Each machine runs one base mix at a time. You can switch from açaí to another base by changing the mix and running the cleaning procedure, and toppings and sauces add variety to whichever base you use. If you want açaí and vanilla side by side, plan two machines.
How many cups of açaí can one machine sell in a day?
It depends on footfall, price and placement. In a shopping mall in Germany, a B83 Max sold 110 cups of açaí soft serve by 4 p.m. on a Saturday at €3.90 a cup, and the operator expected around 180 for the day. B-series machines are rated at up to 120 cups per hour.
Can I sell açaí soft serve from a countertop machine?
Yes. Power7 and Easy7 are countertop models for açaí shops, cafés and dessert counters. Power7 can be configured with two toppings and two sauces. Easy7 is the more compact option: place a cup under the outlet and it serves automatically.
Can I use my own brand on the machine?
Yes. The exterior graphics, screen menu, product images, logo, and launch materials can be customized for your brand.
What locations are suitable?
Shopping malls, gyms, universities, office buildings, transport hubs, hotels, amusement venues, and other high-traffic locations are suitable for this type of themed dessert vending.
Can the machine support cashless payments?
Yes. Payment options can be configured according to your target market, including QR code payment, card payment, and other local payment systems where available.
Is the machine easy to operate?
The machine is designed for self-service operation. Customers order on the screen, pay, and pick up the cup automatically. Operators mainly handle refilling, cleaning, and remote operation checks.
Can I launch several machines in different locations?
Yes. Remote management and standardized branding make it suitable for multi-location operation, distribution projects, and regional franchise or agency models.
Can the screen menu show multiple products?
Yes. The screen can show different flavors, toppings, product images, prices, promotional banners, and language options based on your market plan.
How do I get a solution plan?
Leave your contact information and target country. Our sales team will recommend a suitable machine model, configuration, customization plan, and quotation based on your project.
