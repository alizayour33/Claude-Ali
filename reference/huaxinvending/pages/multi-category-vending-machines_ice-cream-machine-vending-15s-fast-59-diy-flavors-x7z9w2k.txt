Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
1. How long does the Ice Cream Machine Vending take to make one cup?

Only 15 seconds from order to pickup—3x faster than traditional ice cream shops (2-5 minutes). Even during peak hours, queues are reduced by 40%.

2. Why can it provide 59 DIY flavors? Are the ingredients safe?

It supports 3 types of jams and 3 types of toppings (total 6 ingredients), which can be combined into 59 flavor combinations. All contact parts (tank, hoses) are made of 304 stainless steel or food-grade PP material, and the dual UV+ozone sterilization system ensures safety—compliant with ETL food safety standards.

3. Can I really manage the machine remotely 24/7?

Yes. Via our dedicated mobile app, you can check real-time sales data, monitor raw material levels (alert when below 20%), adjust prices, start self-cleaning, and receive fault alerts. 98% of minor issues (e.g., temperature fluctuations) can be resolved remotely without on-site visits.

4. Is the Ice Cream Machine Vending suitable for outdoor use?

Absolutely. It is equipped with a German-imported fan with IP54 protection (resistant to dust and moisture) and a 5.4㎡ large condenser (2x larger than ordinary models) for efficient heat dissipation. It can work stably even at 38°C outdoor temperature—ideal for parks and scenic spots.

5. What certifications does this machine have? Can it be exported to Europe/US?

It has CE certification (EU market access), ETL certification (food safety), and ETL certification (US/Canada electrical safety). Currently, it has been exported to 26 countries including Germany, the US, and Singapore—no compliance barriers.

6. How much power does it consume daily? Is it energy-saving?

Daily power consumption is only 10-15kWh, 40% lower than traditional commercial ice cream machines (20-25kWh/day). The night mode (22:00-7:00) further reduces energy use by 30%—saving ~$50/month in electricity costs for community locations.

7. What payment methods does it support?

It supports cash (coins/bills), credit cards, QR codes (Alipay/WeChat Pay/Apple Pay), and contactless payments. It can be customized to match local payment systems (e.g., Gcash in the Philippines, Boost in Malaysia) to meet regional needs.
