Why Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine can seize the Southeast Asian market?
Why Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine can seize the Southeast Asian market?
Date：2025-05-16 Author：Huaxin

Huaxin ice cream vending machines will give customers and operators an excellent experience in terms of quality, audience, safety and convenience. Lower noise，innovative design, simple operation, large touchscreen to keep ice cream vending machine popular in the southeast Asian market.

In the era of intelligent technology, the unmanned vending industry has developed rapidly. Self-service vending machines have become the best choice for business in the new era. Due to the location and climate factors in Southeast Asia, there is a great demand for ice cream and cold drinks in the Southeast Asian market. Discovering this business opportunity, Huaxin Co., Ltd. focuses on independent research, development and production of ice cream machines suitable for the local market. Currently, we have been engaged in the ice cream vending machine industry for 13 years. During this time, the company has conducted thousands of researching tests just to ensure the production of high quality ice cream vending machines suitable for sales. After market research, Huaxin Soft Serve Ice Cream Vending Machine has already grabbed the Southeast Asian market, and 7 out of every 10 ice cream vending machines for sale are shipped to Malaysia. Why our machines are so popular in Malaysia? This is because our ice cream vending machines will give customers and operators an excellent experience in terms of quality, audience, safety and convenience. 

 
Reasons why Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine leads the Southeast Asian market
In terms of quality, the company cooperates with parts suppliers of internationally recognized brands. The machines are equipped with Embraco compressors, Omron sensors and BOE touchscreens. The Embraco compressor adopts frequency conversion technology and optimized design, which is more efficient and energy-saving. The running noise is as low as below 32dB, which ensures that the running noise of the soft serve ice cream vending machine is greatly reduced. And the compressor has a long life with low failure rate, which ensures that the usage time of the ice cream machine is increased and the maintenance cost is reduced. The Omron sensor on the ice cream vending machine adopts micron-level detection technology, and the temperature sensor error is about 0.1℃. And the intelligent sensors are responsive to ensure that the waiting time of customers is reduced. The high-definition picture quality of the large BOE screen on the ice cream vending machine brings customers an excellent viewing experience. The large screen makes ordering smoother. The low power consumption of the touchscreen reduces the running power of the machine.

The appearance of Huaxin ice cream vending machine is more unique. The machine’s stickers, top light box, and real-time cartoon are designed to be innovative and attractive. The machine can support customization. The ice cream vending machine with one milk syrup, three solid toppings and three liquid toppings can make 59 different flavors of ice cream. There will always be one that you like. The ice cream vending machine with high puffing technology gives the ice cream a richer texture that is as soft as a cloud and as silky as silk. The visible window allows you to watch the complete ice cream making process. The 15-seconds making time reduces customers’ queuing anxiety.

In terms of safety, the Huaxin Soft serve Machine is designed to prove that our company always puts the customer first. Infrared sensors are added to the cup picking door to control the lifting of the lid, which can prevent the customer’s hand from being pinched in the process of picking up the cups. Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machines use food-safe materials to ensure customer safety, such as 304 stainless steel and pp-grade tubes. The machine also has the function of pasteurization, high efficiency sterilization to ensure the safety and hygiene of the machine.

When it comes to convenience, Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine is the best choice for your way of doing business. The one-touch cleaning of the machine reduces labor costs, and the twice-weekly cleaning frequency also ensures the hygiene of the inside of the ice cream vending machine. Remote control allows the operator to control the machine anytime and anywhere. The main functions are remote control switch, machine reset, change machine parameters, product price. Through the cell phone can real-time monitor the ice cream vending machine ingredient consumption status. The message about lacking ingredient in the machine’s tank will be sent to you cell phone interface in real time to remind the replenishment of ingredients.

Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine has already gained a firm foothold in the Southeast Asian market. Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine will be more widely appeared in the world, not only monopolize the Asian market, our company’s purpose is to send more high quality ice cream vending machines to the world. Therefore, the company will always put our customers first and customize our ice cream vending machines according to their needs.
