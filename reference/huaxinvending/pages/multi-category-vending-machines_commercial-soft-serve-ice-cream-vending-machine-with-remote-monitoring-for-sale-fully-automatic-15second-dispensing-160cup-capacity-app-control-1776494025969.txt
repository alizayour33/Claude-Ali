The Huaxin B-Series commercial soft serve ice cream vending machine is a fully automated, self-service frozen dessert robot designed for 24/7 operation. With a compact 0.91㎡ footprint, 15-20 second serving time, and 160-180 cups per fill, it eliminates manual labor and captures impulse purchases. Features include remote monitoring via mobile app, 59 flavor combinations (one mix + 3 toppings + 3 syrups), UV and pasteurization sterilization, energy-saving night mode, and one-key self-cleaning. Powered by an Embraco compressor and German condenser fan, this machine delivers reliable performance and rapid ROI for malls, amusement parks, schools, and transit hubs.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How many flavors can it make?
A: 59 flavor combinations using 1 milk base + 3 fruit syrups + 3 dry toppings. Real-time mixing.

Q: How fast does it serve one cup?
A: 15-20 seconds from order to pick-up.

Q: How many cups before refill?
A: 160-180 cups with 20L tank; cup stack holds 160 cups.

Q: Is cleaning difficult?
A: No. One-key self-cleaning program. Recommended weekly 1-2 times.

Q: Can I monitor sales remotely?
A: Yes. Our mobile app shows real-time sales, ingredient levels, machine status, and allows price changes.

Q: What if the machine breaks down?
A: 95% of errors can be diagnosed and fixed remotely via the app. For hardware issues, modular parts are easy to replace with video guidance.

Q: What payment methods are supported?
A: Coins, banknotes, credit cards, and QR codes (WeChat, Alipay, etc.). Customizable for your region.

Q: Can I customize the appearance?
A: Yes. Custom stickers, magnetic panels, voltage, language, and payment system are available for OEM/ODM orders.

Q: What is the minimum order quantity?
A: 1 unit for trial. Bulk orders get better pricing.

Q: How is it shipped?
A: 5-layer protection: stretch film → pearl foam → carton → wooden case → wooden pallet. Typically by sea freight.
