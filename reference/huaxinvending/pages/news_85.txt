The power of technological innovation - ice cream vending machines set the trend
Date：2025-06-10 Author：Huaxin

Ice cream vending machines demonstrate a combination of intelligence and efficiency. Guarantee supply, worry-free peak season of selling ice cream. One- flavor ice cream can enhance your brand obviously, adding 6 different topping to rich the flavor. 15 seconds to dispensing. 24 hours online, no day off.

The soft ice cream vending machine is the perfect combination of technology and innovation in the food industry. It has redefined the way we buy and enjoy desserts. As the pace of people's lives increases, convenience is seen as a major element of modern life and ice cream vending machines address this need. Have you ever missed out on the leisure of a cold drink because you were waiting in a queue? With our latest smart technology vending machines, you can get your favourite ice cream in seconds. This convenience is supported by an advanced payment system that allows you to complete your purchase by simply paying with your mobile phone or swiping your card. The payment system of our machine

In addition to convenience, ice cream vending machines demonstrate a combination of intelligence and efficiency. These machines are equipped with the latest temperature control systems to ensure that the ice cream is kept in optimum condition every time it is served. Whether it's a scorching summer day or a cold winter day, you can enjoy your ice cream at the perfect temperature. Through data collection and analysis, our soft ice cream vending machines automatically adjust their stock based on purchasing trends, ensuring you have a wide selection of fresh taste every time. From classic vanilla to modern matcha, the choices are endless. Choosing your favourite flavor of milk syrup, our soft ice cream machines only have one flavor for your choice, but you can add different toppings for matching. The reason why our ice cream vending machine has one milk hopper is that this design can let machine stabler. It also decreases the fault rate and reduce maintenance fee. One- flavor ice cream can enhance your brand obviously.

 

Guarantee Supply, Worry-free Peak Season of Selling Ice Cream  

Our company has a modern factory with 15,000 square meters and two production lines, with an annual output of more than 6,000 units. We strictly adhere to the quality management system. Additionally, we have obtained the CE certification for exporting goods to Europe and the ETL certification for exporting goods to North America, and other types of certification systems such as ETL, RoHS, etc. These patents can ensure that your orders are delivered timely in the peak season, and not miss sales opportunities. Frozen yogurt vending machine produced be Huaxin Co., Ltd is a new type of our vending machine, which be enhanced the speed of serving single-cup ice cream, up to 15 seconds per cup. With ultra-low temperature endurance freezing technology, it can continuously make 800 cups of ice cream. On the core components of the machine, the vending machines used the world’s top-level Embraco compressor and Omron sensing system, which have undergone rigorous testing to ensure the continuous and stable operation of the equipment so that the equipment brings you the maximum benefits.

In an era of increasing environmental awareness, ice cream vending machines are not far behind. Our ice cream vending machines are made from energy-efficient materials and designed to minimise electricity consumption and promote sustainable use. Each ice cream vending machine is equipped with recyclable packaging options to help reduce the use of plastic. In addition, with the digitization of ice cream vending behaviour, logistics and supply chain management have become more efficient. Reducing our carbon footprint is not only an added value when enjoying ice cream, but also a responsibility to promote a greener society. Through the power of technology, we are not only changing the way we buy, we are also doing our part to protect the natural environment.
