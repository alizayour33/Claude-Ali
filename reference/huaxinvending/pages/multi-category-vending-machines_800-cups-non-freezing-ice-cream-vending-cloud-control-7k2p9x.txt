Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) About Ice Cream Vending
1. How does the machine achieve 800 cups of non-freezing ice cream vending?

It relies on our patented "Cold Quantity Micro-Control Technology" and high-performance components: the Embraco compressor adjusts refrigerant flow precisely to avoid cylinder freezing, while the 5.4㎡ large condenser (2x larger than ordinary models) ensures efficient heat dissipation. This combination maintains stable operation even during 12-hour peak sales.

2. What can I do with the cloud control system for ice cream vending management?

The cloud system (Huaxin "Taishan 12th Gen") supports 4 core functions: ① Real-time data tracking (sales, temperature, inventory); ② Remote control (price adjustment, device on/off, thawing); ③ Automatic alerts (low ingredients, empty cups); ④ Sub-account profit sharing (auto-split revenue with mall partners). You can manage 10+ machines with one phone.

3. How long does it take to make one cup, and how many cups can the machine hold?

Each cup is ready in 15 seconds—ideal for high-traffic scenarios like scenic spots (up to 3,000 cups/day). The 20L material cylinder makes 160-180 cups per fill, and the 4-hole cup dispenser holds 240 cups. The machine automatically alerts you when supplies are low via the mini-program.

4. Is the machine easy to maintain, and what about after-sales support?

Maintenance is hassle-free: use the one-touch self-cleaning function (15 minutes, 1-2 times/week). We offer 24/7 remote technical support (98% issues solved remotely) and a 1-year full warranty. Spare parts are modular and easy to replace—video guidance helps you change components in 5 minutes.

5. Can the ice cream vending machine be customized for different regions?

Yes! We support customization for voltage (110V/220V), payment methods (QR code, credit card, cash), languages (20+ options like English, German, French), and appearance (magnetic panels for IP collaborations). It’s compliant with EU (CE) and North American (ETL) standards, making it suitable for global deployment.

6. What’s the energy consumption of this ice cream vending machine?

Thanks to the inverter compressor and "night mode" (auto-activated 9 PM-8 AM), daily energy consumption is only 10-15 kWh—40% lower than traditional machines. Night mode reduces screen brightness and adjusts cooling intensity, cutting monthly electricity costs by $30-$50.
