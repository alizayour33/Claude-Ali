In the modern food-service equipment sector, technological innovation is crucial for enhancing user experience and operational efficiency. As an industry pioneer, Huaxin has consistently advanced in product development and technological innovation. The Huaxin R&D team has not only significantly improved the stability and maintenance cycles of ice cream vending machines but has also solved the industry-wide challenge of continuous high-volume production without freezing cylinder in low-temperature environments, delivering unprecedented convenience to users. The efficient ice cream vending machines have high-quality assurance, which let you rest assured to use. The main advantages of stable machines are reducing maintenance costs and long-term profit. Multi-flavor ice cream vending machines make your favorites in 15 seconds or less, meeting all needs of different customers.
In this era of pursuing ultimate efficiency and experience, Huaxin's intelligent ice cream vending machines have revolutionized the traditional retail model with groundbreaking technological innovations. Our core technology 5-Second Ice Extreme Refrigeration System employs top-tier compression technology, reducing temperatures from indoor temperature to -18°C in just 5 seconds. It is 8 times faster than conventional equipment, and ensures that every cup of ice cream is served in perfect condition. Even more impressive matter is our proprietary Marathon Freeze Force Technology which enables uninterrupted production of 800 cups, with a daily maximum capacity of 1,200 cups. Huaxin ice cream vending machine effortlessly handle peak holiday demand. The product features a modular smart design. Huaxin ice cream vending machine equipped with a 32-inch touchscreen, and support for multiple payment methods including Alipay, WeChat Pay, and UnionPay. From ordering to pick up ice cream, it only takes 15 seconds. Huaxin has innovatively researched the cooling micro-control refrigeration technology, which can precisely control the refrigerant input to the nanoscale during the operation of the equipment. It ensure that the equipment in different operating conditions under the cooling requirements to achieve the important innovation of machine without freezing cylinder. Notably, our Nanoscale level Cooling Technology utilizes a 0.1℃ accuracy temperature control system, combined with AI algorithms to automatically regulate refrigerant flow. This technology completely eliminate the common issue of freezing cylinder and extending the machine’s lifespan to over 10 years.
