Revolutionizing Frozen Retail: The Future of Automated Ice Cream

The Huaxin B83 Series eliminates the "Efficiency Trap" of traditional ice cream shops. By replacing manual labor with high-precision robotics, we provide a turnkey solution that delivers premium soft-serve in 15 seconds, 24/7, with zero supervision.

Technical Excellence & Problem Solving
1. Industrial Refrigeration Mastery

Equipped with high-performance Embraco compressors and a 5.4m² oversized copper condenser (2x the industry average). Achieves 5-second rapid cooling and maintains a consistent -8°C texture even in high-temperature environments.

2. Master OS Smart Ecosystem

A cloud-based management system allows for remote inventory tracking, price adjustment, and 95% remote fault resolution. Includes an automated one-click cleaning cycle and energy-saving night mode to minimize operational costs.

3. Hygiene & Safety Protocol

Integrated UV + Pasteurization system ensures the liquid hopper (10L-20L) remains germ-free. The closed dispensing window prevents external contamination, meeting the strictest global food safety standards.
