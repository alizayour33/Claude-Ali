Additionally, it comes equipped with a real-time monitoring and feedback system to promptly collect user feedback and continuously improve service. Replenishment and cleaning maintenance are equally efficient and convenient. The device has an intelligent material management system that can detect the remaining amount of ice cream in real time, alerting staff to restock as needed. The modular design makes components easy to disassemble and clean, reducing maintenance costs and time.

A robust after-sales and service support system is a major highlight. A professional customer service team stands ready, providing quick responses and solutions through various channels such as phone calls, emails, and online customer service. The ice cream vending machine comes with built-in fault detection capabilities to monitor its operational status in real time, ensuring the equipment remains in optimal condition. From the analysis of profitability and return on investment, ice cream vending machines have the characteristics of low cost and high returns.
