Product Description.

HUAXIN’s automatic ice cream vending machine with elevator is a CE/ETL certified commercial solution. It offers 15-second fast serving, 59 customizable flavors, and space-saving design (0.91㎡). With remote control, automatic cleaning, and low energy use, it cuts labor costs, fits crowded areas, and delivers high profits. Trusted by 33+ countries, it’s ideal for malls, stations, and scenic spots.

This automatic ice cream vending machine with elevator is a one-stop commercial solution for hassle-free ice cream sales. It handles the entire process from flavor selection, payment to production automatically—no manual intervention needed.
Customers can choose from 59 customized flavors (1 base + 3 toppings + 3 jams) and get their ice cream in just 15-20 seconds.
It operates 24/7, fitting perfectly in high-traffic areas like malls, stations, scenic spots, and schools.
With a space-saving design (0.91㎡), it can be placed on bars or small corners without wasting valuable floor space.
