Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many flavors can the machine produce?

A: The modular design allows for 1 base cream, 3 jams, and 3 dry toppings, enabling customers to choose from up to 59 different flavor combinations.

Q: What is the capacity per refill?

A: Max models hold up to 20L of mix and 160 cups, allowing for approximately 160-180 servings before requiring a refill.

Q: Is the machine easy to move?

A: Yes. Despite its robust 320KG build, the machine is equipped with four adjustable Fuma wheels, allowing a single person to move and reposition the unit easily.
