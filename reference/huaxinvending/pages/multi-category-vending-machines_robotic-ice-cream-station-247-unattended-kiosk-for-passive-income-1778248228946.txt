Solving High-Traffic Retail Pain Points with Robotics
1. Eliminate Rising Labor Costs

Traditional soft-serve retail loses up to 40% of net profit to labor management and training. The Huaxin B83 is a 100% Unattended Robotic Station. It manages payments, inventory alerts, and hygiene cycles autonomously, allowing one operator to manage 20+ units via a mobile App through Cloud Telemetry.

2. Premium Texture via 43% Overrun Mastery

Quality is what drives repeat business. Our proprietary Air-Pump Tech achieves a 43% overrun, infusing air to create a silky, "DQ-style" mouthfeel. Commercially, this also means you get 43% more product volume from the same liquid mix, directly increasing your profit per serving.

3. Solving the Hygiene & Sanitation Challenge

Hygiene is the foundation of consumer trust. Huaxin uses an Integrated UV + Pasteurization Matrix. The machine keeps the mix at a safe 2-6°C and self-sanitizes internal tubes, reducing manual deep-cleaning frequency from daily to just once a week, ensuring total food safety compliance.

Technical Infrastructure for Scalable Growth
Industrial-Grade Cooling

Features Embraco Inverter Technology and a 5.4m² oversized condenser. This system allows the machine to maintain "Marathon output" (continuous peak-hour dispensing) without cylinder freeze-ups, even in high ambient temperatures up to 40°C.

Precision Motion Control

Our RK Industrial Controller integrates Omron photoelectric sensors to coordinate cup placement and dispensing arms with 0.1mm accuracy, effectively eliminating mechanical jams and waste.
