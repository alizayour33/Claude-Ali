Frequently Asked Questions
What is the capacity of the Max Series ice cream vending machine?

The Max Series is equipped with a 20L mix cylinder and 160-cup storage capacity. After filling, it can produce about 160–180 cups, depending on product settings and serving size.

Is the Max Series suitable for European high-traffic locations?

Yes. The Max Series is suitable for shopping malls, airports, train stations, supermarkets, schools, theme parks, tourist attractions, cinemas, and commercial leisure areas.

How long does it take to make one cup?

The Max Series can make one cup in about 15–20 seconds, depending on machine configuration and product settings.

Does it support contactless payment?

Yes. The machine can support credit card payment, QR payment, coin, bill, and customized payment systems according to local European market requirements.

Can I monitor the Max Series remotely?

Yes. Operators can remotely monitor sales data, temperature, ingredient status, cup status, alerts, revenue reports, and machine operating status.

Can the Max Series be customized for my brand?

Yes. Huaxin supports customization for appearance, logo, stickers, voltage, payment system, language interface, UI, and screen content.

Is the Max Series suitable for distributors?

Yes. It is suitable for European distributors looking for a high-capacity smart vending product, regional dealership opportunity, bulk purchase model, and local operator development.

Huaxin provides video technical support, online remote guidance, spare parts availability, operation training, software support, and one-year machine warranty.
