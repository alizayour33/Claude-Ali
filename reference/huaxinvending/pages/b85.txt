B85 FAQ
How much of the B85 can be customized for my brand?

B85 can support exterior branding, logo application, touchscreen interface, menu presentation, language, payment integration, and software functions within the existing machine platform.

Can the B85 touchscreen experience match my retail concept?

Yes. Customer-facing interface visuals, menu content, language, and supported software functions can be adapted to create a more consistent branded experience.

Can B85 integrate payment methods used in my country?

Payment integration depends on the target market and payment provider. Huaxin can evaluate supported card, NFC, QR code, and other local payment solutions.

Does Huaxin offer ODM or internal structure changes for B85?

No. Huaxin does not provide ODM and does not change the internal mechanical structure or electronic layout. Customization is limited to customer-facing branding, interface, payment integration, and software functions.

Can I manage a network of branded B85 machines remotely?

Yes. Master OS can provide machine status, sales information, inventory reminders, and operational alerts across multiple deployed locations.

Which locations are most suitable for B85?

B85 is positioned for customer-facing retail environments such as shopping malls, lifestyle centers, family entertainment venues, campuses, and tourist attractions where visual identity and self-service convenience both matter.
