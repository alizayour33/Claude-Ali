Profit Plan for Ice Cream Vending Machines
Date：2025-10-02 Author：Huaxin

Ice cream vending machines, a light-asset tool for scenic spots, arcades and supermarkets, tap idle space and fragmented customer flow to create extra income. They fit existing consumption chains (e.g., queuing), have low investment (4-6 month payback), high gross margin (83%) and low operation cost, with stable profits across scenarios, activating idle resources.


 
For operators of scenic spots, arcades, supermarkets and other venues, idle space and fragmented customer flow are often untapped profit opportunities. As a light-asset operation tool, ice cream vending machines can create stable additional income for business owners through scenario integration and cost-controllable features. Its profit logic does not rely on large-scale investment, but accurately matches consumer demand, allowing every penny of venue cost to generate additional value.
Integration of Ice Cream Machines with Scenarios: Encouraging Existing Customers to Spend More
Business owners know best: the customer flow in the venue is a ready-made asset, and the key is to get them to spend more. The advantage of ice cream vending machines lies in their ability to fit into the consumption chain of existing scenarios. Scenic spot operators are well aware that tourists have the strongest willingness to consume while queuing or resting. After placing a machine in the cable car waiting area of Yuntai Mountain Scenic Area in Jiaozuo City, the average daily sales in summer have stabilized at over 120 cups with a unit price of 18 yuan — these consumption did not exist before and are completely additional income. For young people waiting for machines in arcades, 5-10 minutes of fragmented time is just enough to buy an ice cream. Tests show that when the machine is placed next to claw machines, sales are 58% higher than when placed in corners, without affecting the original game consumption. Supermarket owners better understand the value of "the last meter". Machines in the checkout queue area take advantage of the 3-5 minutes customers wait to promote impulsive consumption. Data from a community supermarket shows that a single machine can bring an additional monthly sales volume of 12,000 yuan on average without occupying core display space.
Why Ice Cream Machines Can Achieve Low Cost and High Returns
For business owners, the biggest fear of new projects is "trouble" and "high investment". The cost structure of ice cream vending machines just meets the needs of "less worry and low risk". The equipment investment is a one-time expenditure. The payback period of mainstream models is usually 4-6 months (based on an average of 50 cups per day), after which it will generate continuous income. A single machine consumes 8 kWh of electricity per day, with a monthly electricity bill of about 150 yuan. The raw material cost is transparent. The total cost of cream, syrup, etc. for each cup is about 2.5 yuan. Based on a selling price of 15 yuan, the gross profit margin reaches 83%. What's more convenient is the operation: real-time sales and raw material surplus can be checked through the mobile phone background. One refill can last 2-3 days, no need for special personnel to take care of it, and the labor cost is almost negligible. Compared with the decoration and labor required for adding a new stall, this model puts much less pressure on cash flow.
Profit Performance of Ice Cream Vending Machines in Different Scenarios
Feedback from operators in various places shows that the profit performance of ice cream vending machines is stable and predictable, especially suitable as a supplement to the main business. Scenic spots have the strongest peak season performance. The equipment in a coastal scenic spot achieved a monthly sales volume of 42,000 yuan in July and August. After deducting 10% of the venue sharing fee, the net profit exceeded 28,000 yuan, equivalent to hiring an "non-stop salesperson". Arcades have stable customer flow. The equipment in a brand arcade has no obvious off-season throughout the year, with an average monthly net profit of 13,000 yuan per machine, which complements the consumption of game coins. Even in community supermarkets, the monthly net profit of a single machine can be maintained at 8,000-10,000 yuan, equivalent to an additional "passive income" every month. Many business owners feedback that the advantage of such equipment is that "it does not take too much energy but can continuously generate money".
 
The value of ice cream vending machines is not just a machine that sells ice cream, but a way of "activating idle resources". It does not need to restructure existing businesses, but can make existing resources such as venues and customer flow generate greater value. In the current context of increasing operating costs, this "small investment, stable output" model may be a profitable supplement worth trying — after all, a project that can really make money without too much trouble is a good project for business owners.
