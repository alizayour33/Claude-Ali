Product Description.

Smart Yogurt Robot: The Future of Automated Frozen Yogurt Retail

Revolutionize Your Business with Hands-Free Vending

Tired of the high costs and operational hassles of traditional frozen yogurt shops? The Smart Yogurt Robot is here to transform your business model. As a fully automatic robot yogurt vending machine, it eliminates the need for staff, reduces your footprint to less than 1 square meter, and serves delicious, consistent frozen yogurt 24 hours a day. Designed for entrepreneurs, mall operators, and facility managers, this machine turns high-traffic areas into high-profit centers with minimal effort.

Core Technology & Unmatched Reliability

Built with industrial-grade components for non-stop operation, the Smart Yogurt Robot ensures every cup meets the highest standards.

High-Performance Cooling System: Equipped with a commercial-grade Embraco compressor featuring Full-Drive Inverter Technology. It achieves rapid cooling in 5 seconds, maintains a consistent “Marathon Freezing” power for over 12 hours of continuous operation, and uses 40% less energy than conventional systems.

Precision Robotics & Dispensing: The automated arm and lifting mechanism, guided by Omron photoelectric sensors, achieve a positioning accuracy of <0.1mm. Combined with a built-in gear pump (43% overrun), it delivers perfectly textured, ice-crystal-free yogurt every time.

Advanced Hygiene & Safety: A multi-stage UV + Pasteurization sterilization system works in tandem with the hopper’s low-temperature antibacterial function (2-6°C). All food contact parts, including the peristaltic pumps for toppings, are designed for easy removal and cleaning.

Intelligent Self-Maintenance: The integrated Master OS enables one-touch automated cleaning cycles and self-diagnosis for 95% of common faults, which can often be resolved remotely, minimizing downtime.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How many cups can it hold and make?
A: The machine holds approximately 160 cups in its dispenser. The 20L max hopper contains enough yogurt mix to produce 160-180 servings before a refill is needed.

Q: Is it difficult to clean and maintain?
A: Not at all. The machine features a one-touch auto-clean function. Regular cleaning is simple, and most components are modular for easy replacement. Comprehensive guidance is provided.

Q: What payment methods are supported?
A: It supports coins, bills, credit/debit cards, and QR code payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay, etc.). Payment systems can be customized for specific regional needs.

Q: Can I customize the machine's appearance?
A: Yes. We offer full OEM/ODM services. You can customize the exterior wrap, magnetic panels, lighting, and even the user interface language to match your brand identity.

Q: What about installation and power requirements?
A: Installation is plug-and-play. Simply place it on a level floor, connect it to a standard 220V AC power outlet, and it’s ready to go. No complex plumbing or construction is needed.

Q: What is the warranty?
A: The complete machine comes with a 1-year warranty. We provide lifelong maintenance support, with a focus on remote resolution to keep your operations running smoothly.
