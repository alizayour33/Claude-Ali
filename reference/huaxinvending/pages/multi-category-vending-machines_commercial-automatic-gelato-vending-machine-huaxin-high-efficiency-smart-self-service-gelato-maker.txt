HUAXIN Commercial Automatic Gelato Vending Machine - The Ultimate Smart Self-Service SolutionAs a global leading brand in gelato vending machines with 13 years of industry experience, HUAXIN has occupied 70% of the domestic market share and exported to 33 countries worldwide, with CE, ETL, RoHS and CB international certifications. Our commercial automatic gelato vending machine is designed to solve the core pain points of traditional gelato sales, including space anxiety, high labor costs, low impulse consumption conversion and low profit margins, bringing a new retail experience for your business.
Core Advantages Solving Retail Pain Points
Space Revolution - Mini Body & Easy Installation
The machine covers only 0.91㎡ of floor space, which can be directly placed on the bar counter, breaking the space limitation of traditional vending machines. With 0 assembly and 0 transformation, it is plug-and-play, and the 320KG body with 4 adjustable caster wheels can be moved by one person, suitable for various business scenarios.
Labor Liberation - Full Process Automation
From flavor selection, payment to gelato making, the whole process is fully automated, with the fastest dispensing in 15 seconds and an average of 30 seconds for a single cup. The closed discharge port realizes contactless production, avoiding manual participation, saving a lot of labor costs, and the labor management efficiency is upgraded to 10 machines per person.
Smart Black Technology - Master OS Smart System
Equipped with self-developed Master OS smart system, it supports free switching between working mode and night mode to minimize energy consumption, with a daily power consumption of only 10-15 kWh. The mobile app realizes remote control, including real-time viewing of equipment parameters such as temperature and raw material surplus, modifying sales prices, and viewing revenue reports. 95% of faults can be solved remotely with one-click fault inspection, and the one-click self-cleaning function makes equipment maintenance simple and efficient, eliminating the trouble of manual disassembly and cleaning.
Impulse Consumption Converter - Visual Temptation & Fast Dispensing
The panoramic visual window made of tempered glass displays the whole process of gelato making, creating a strong visual temptation for customers. 95% of users share their purchase experience on social platforms, and a single content drives an average of 3-5 secondary consumption, greatly improving the conversion rate of impulse consumption.
Profit Multiplication Module - Diverse Flavors & High Profit Margins
With a single material cylinder matching 3 kinds of jam and 3 kinds of top materials, it can be combined into 59 different flavors, meeting the diverse taste needs of customers. The exclusive cheese flavor gelato has the same quality as DQ but the price is 1/3 lower, with a 30% increase in repurchase rate; the milk tea-style topping combination increases the repurchase rate by 25%. The built-in puffing gear pump with a puffing rate of 43% makes gelato smooth without ice residue. The single cup cost is only 1.5-1.8 USD, and the selling price is 6-10 USD, with a gross profit margin of more than 65%. High-quality locations can sell 200 cups per day, with a monthly net profit of 12,000 USD and a return on investment in 3-4 months.
High-Quality Core Components - Stable & Durable
The core components of the machine are all from industry leading brands, ensuring stable operation and long service life. The Embraco compressor with full-drive frequency conversion technology realizes 5s rapid cooling, 45% faster cooling speed and 40% lower energy consumption than traditional compressors. The condenser with a heat dissipation area of 5.4 ㎡ and German imported exhaust fan with IP54 protection level achieve efficient heat dissipation, with the fan noise below 40dB. Omron photoelectric sensors are widely used in cup dropper, transmission shaft and other parts, ensuring accurate positioning and stable operation. The 32-inch BOE touch screen supports 24-hour stable operation and adapts to high temperature and high humidity environments.
Sterilization & Sanitation - Food Safety Guaranteed
Adhering to the food safety first principle, the machine is equipped with ultraviolet + pasteurization system, and the material cylinder has a low-temperature bacteriostasis function of 2-6℃, which effectively inhibits the growth of bacteria and ensures the safety and health of gelato. The jam motor adopts a peristaltic pump to ensure that all food circulates in the food channel, avoiding secondary pollution.
Customization Service - Tailored For Your Business
We support professional customization of the machine, including appearance customization such as film pasting, magnetic panel and light box, voltage customization, payment method customization and language system customization. The 32-inch large screen supports dual content push, with the upper part as the advertising space and the lower part as the ordering interface, which can realize advertising monetization and cross-format drainage through the coupon function. The machine also supports sub-account settlement function, realizing automatic proportional settlement with shopping malls and partners.
Strong Production & After-Sales Service
HUAXIN has a 15,000 ㎡ modern factory with 2 production lines and an annual output of more than 10,000 units. The product has undergone 11 iterations and upgrades, with strict quality control including part testing, high temperature testing and whole machine comprehensive testing. The after-sales team with more than 20 people provides 24/7 cloud operation and maintenance, free system upgrade, training and Q&A. 98% of the problems can be solved remotely through video guidance and modular plug-and-replace, and the global after-sales team provides 24-hour online guidance with lifelong maintenance service and 1-year whole machine warranty.
