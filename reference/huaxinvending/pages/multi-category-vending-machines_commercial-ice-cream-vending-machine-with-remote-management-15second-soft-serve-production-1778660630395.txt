Product Description.

Commercial Ice Cream Vending Machine for High-Profit Unmanned Retail

The HUAXIN automatic ice cream vending machine is engineered for modern self-service retail businesses that need fast production, stable cooling performance, low maintenance, and intelligent remote management. Designed for shopping malls, airports, schools, amusement parks, commercial streets, and entertainment venues, this smart vending kiosk transforms traditional ice cream sales into a fully automated 24/7 business model.

With ultra-fast 15-second dispensing, 59 customizable flavor combinations, low daily power consumption, and remote app monitoring, operators can maximize revenue while minimizing labor and operational costs.

Why Traditional Ice Cream Shops Struggle

Traditional ice cream operations face several major challenges:

High labor dependency and rising employee costs.
Long waiting times causing impulse purchase loss.
Large equipment footprint limiting installation locations.
Daily manual cleaning consuming operational time.
Low revenue per square meter in expensive retail spaces.
Limited operating hours reducing sales opportunities.

HUAXIN solves these problems through a fully automated, intelligent, self-service vending solution.

Frequently Asked Questions
How many flavors can the machine produce?

The machine supports up to 59 flavor combinations through syrup and topping customization.

How long does it take to make one ice cream?

Production time is approximately 15 to 20 seconds per cup.

How often does the machine need cleaning?

The machine includes a one-click automatic cleaning system. Weekly cleaning 1-2 times is generally recommended.

Can the machine work without internet?

Yes. The machine can operate offline normally. Internet connectivity enables remote monitoring and advanced management features.

Can the machine be customized?

Yes. Custom branding, language systems, payment systems, voltage configurations, and exterior designs are available.

What locations are suitable for deployment?

The machine is ideal for malls, airports, schools, tourist attractions, cinemas, commercial streets, and other high-traffic locations.
