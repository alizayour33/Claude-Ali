Huaxin Invites You to SIGEP WORLD——Unlock New Business Opportunities for Intelligent Ice Cream Machines


SIGEP WORLD, the annual grand event in the global dessert and food service industry, is set to kick off grandly in Rimini, Italy, from January 16th to 20th, 2026! As a pioneer in the field of intelligent ice cream machines, Huaxin sincerely invites global partners and industry peers to attend the exhibition, jointly explore new trends in intelligent retail, and jointly expand the new blueprint of the international market.
At this exhibition, Huaxin will showcase its core products such as intelligent ice cream machines and frozen yogurt vending machines at Booth No. 046, Hall A3. It will fully demonstrate the innovative advantages of its products in intelligent control, efficient operation, quality assurance and other aspects, and provide customized intelligent retail solutions for global customers.
Exhibition Address: Via Emilia, 155, 47922 Rimini RN, Italy
We look forward to communicating with you face-to-face in Rimini, Italy, and discussing cooperation opportunities together! For advance appointment and negotiation or to learn more about the exhibition products, you can contact us through the following methods:
Official Website: https://www.huaxinvending.com/
Email: huaxinchina@huaxinvending.com
Social Media: Follow our Facebook and TikTok accounts to get real-time updates of the exhibition!


References / Sources

Author's Introduction: Huaxin Co., Ltd. has manufactured commercial automated ice cream vending machines in Dezhou, China, since 2013, with machines shipped to more than 50 countries and regions. Each unit completes a 72-hour stress test before shipment, and certifications such as CE, IECEE CB, NSF/ANSI 6 and ETL vary by model.
