Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much space does the B85 need?

A: The machine measures 1950 (H) × 1080 (L) × 940 (W) mm, a footprint of about 0.91 m². Plan extra room for door opening, ventilation and service access, plus a power supply suitable for the 1800 W rated power.

Q: How is hygiene managed?

A: The B85 runs a one-touch cleaning program and is equipped with a UV germicidal lamp. Pasteurization is optional on compatible configurations. Daily cleaning and local food-safety procedures are still required.

Q: How much can I earn with one machine?

A: It depends on your location, selling price, daily volume and operating costs. Tell us your market and location type, and we can help you estimate it with your own figures.

Q: Can I customize the machine?

A: Exterior branding (logo, colors, wrap) and the interface language can be customized. Payment setup and selected software functions are evaluated per project. Standard customization does not include redesigning the machine's internal mechanical or electrical systems.

Buying Multiple Ice Cream Vending Machines? Bulk Configuration, Delivery, Spare Parts and Remote Management

Planning 5 machines for a mall chain, or 10 for a franchise rollout? The Huaxin B85 fully automatic ice cream vending machine can be planned as a fleet rather than one unit at a time. Settle these four points before you order, and every machine goes out ready for its location.

Bulk configuration: one setup across all machines

Choose voltage (220 V standard, 110 V optional) and frequency (50 Hz standard, 60 Hz optional) once for the whole order. Confirm them with your sales manager before ordering.

Interface language, payment setup and exterior branding (logo, colors, wrap) can be specified for the whole order. MDB-compatible payment integration is available, and compatibility depends on the provider and terminal.

Optional pasteurization is available on compatible configurations.

Customization is platform-based. It covers branding, interface, payment and selected software functions, and does not include redesigning the machine's internal mechanical or electrical systems.

Delivery: built and inspected in one factory

Huaxin machines are produced in a 15,000 m² factory with in-house machining, sheet metal, powder-coating and refrigeration lines, and an annual production capacity of 6,000 units. Each refrigeration system goes through a 48-hour pressure-hold test. Machines also go through high-temperature testing and final inspection before shipment. Building in one place helps keep the appearance and quality of a multi-unit order consistent. Production and shipping schedules are confirmed per project, together with the electrical and import requirements of your target market.

Spare parts: plan them with your order

Spare parts support is available for applicable models. Key mechanical parts, such as agitator shafts, lifting rods, nozzles, freezing cylinders and mix tanks, are machined in-house, which helps keep replacement-part supply consistent. Ask your sales manager which parts to stock for your fleet size.

Remote management: monitor every machine from one place

Network-enabled machines support remote monitoring of operating status and selected business data. Status and fault alerts help you spot low stock or problems earlier, and selected settings and basic operations can be managed remotely. Real-time remote functions require an internet connection. Remote management does not replace routine cleaning, restocking or on-site checks. See the Huaxin remote management system for details.

Multi-unit FAQ

Q: Can I order several machines with the same configuration?

A: Yes. Voltage, frequency, language, payment setup and branding can be specified once for the order, subject to the available options.

Q: Are spare parts available?

A: Spare parts support is available for applicable models. Tell us your model and the part you need, and we will confirm.

Q: Do you provide on-site service?

A: On-site service availability depends on the market and project arrangement. Remote diagnosis and video guidance can also be provided.

Q: How do I get a multi-unit quotation?

A: Send your target market, quantity and location type through the inquiry form.
