With the rise of consumer demand and advancements in unmanned retail technology, the desktop ice cream vending machine has emerged as a game-changer in the food and beverage industry. Its high- definition touchscreen and customized brand appearance also make it a natural public offline hotspots, perfect for social media sharing and worldwide marketing.
 
Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine with Ultra-space Design Maximizes Space Efficiency
In space-constrained commercial settings, Huaxin desktop ice cream vending machine breaks the floor area with a 0.46㎡, which is just like the size of an A2 sheet. The design of desktop ice cream vending machine fits in with the demand of tea shops, convenience store counters, and office lounges. It requires no renovations, just plug and play it. Our designer adopt to vertical rectangular design and 13.3-inch touchscreen to make this machine. It is no jarring to put it alongside coffee machines. But the biggest surprise is that the machine even supports dual-sided embedded installation, fitting into walls or counters to utilize every inch of space. Field tests in Singapore showed that a single unmanned unit can sell over 200 cups daily, boosting revenue per square meter by 5 times compared to traditional ice cream machine.   Huaxin Intelligent Desktop Ice Cream Vending Machine Redefine Efficiency In the fast-paced modern business environment, the smart desktop ice cream vending machine is becoming a new favorite in the food and beverage retail industry thanks to its innovative technology. It not only meets consumers’ demand for convenience and flavor, but also provides operators with an efficient, low-cost solution.
Multi-Mode Switching-Smart Energy Savings Huaxin intelligent desktop ice cream vending machine supports one-touch switching between working mode and night mode. Full efficacy operation during the daytime ensures 15 seconds of quick cups. At night, it automatically switches to low energy state, reducing power consumption by 50% while keeping the ingredients at low temperature to preserve freshness. This design allows for a significant reduction in operating costs and is especially suitable for 24-hour convenience stores and shopping malls.
 
App Remote Control Manage Anytime, Anywhere
Through the mobile app, operators can view the machine's operating parameters in real time, including the remaining amount of raw materials, temperature status, etc., and adjust the selling price at any time to adapt to promotional activities. The system automatically generates revenue reports and accurately analyzes sales peaks and popular flavors to help refine operations.
One-Click Fault Detect without Downtime
Huaxin ice cream vending machine has a intelligent diagnostic system. When a fault occurs, the one-click overhaul function can be activated through a small program. The system automatically recognizes the problem and provides a solution. 80% of common faults can be repaired remotely, significantly, which could reduce downtime.
One-Click Cleaning- Hygiene Made Effortless
Say goodbye to the traditional cumbersome disassembly and cleaning, the desktop ice cream vending machine parts fully automatic cleaning program. Just one button start, warm water circulation and food-grade disinfectant work in concert within 8 minutes to complete the deep cleaning of the pipe and the material tank, which can ensure the hygiene and safety of each cup of ice cream.
