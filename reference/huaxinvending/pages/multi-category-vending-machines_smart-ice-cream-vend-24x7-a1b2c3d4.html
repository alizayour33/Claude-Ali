Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How much does an ice cream vending machine cost?

A: The price depends on configuration, quantity, and customization (payment systems, branding). Contact us for a detailed, competitive quote tailored to your needs.

Q: How long does it take to make one cup?

A: It's incredibly fast! The entire process from payment to receiving a finished cup takes approximately 15 seconds.

Q: How often do I need to restock?

A: The machine holds ~160 cups. It sends a smartphone alert when ingredients are low (at 10-20 cups remaining), allowing for easy proactive restocking.

Q: Is the machine hygienic?

A: Absolutely. The process is fully enclosed. It also features a built-in UV + Ozone sterilization system and a self-cleaning function for ultimate hygiene.

Q: What about maintenance and warranty?

A: We offer a comprehensive 12-month warranty. Most issues can be diagnosed and resolved remotely via our support team. Maintenance is simple with automated cleaning prompts.

Q: How profitable is it?

A: Very profitable. With low per-cup costs and high selling prices, margins are strong. Many owners report a full ROI in just 3-4 months in a good location.
