Product Description.


Why Choose Our Ice Cream Vending Machine?

Our smart unmanned commercial ice cream vending machine is engineered to redefine unmanned retail, with 13+ years of R&D expertise and compliance with international quality standards. It addresses all pain points of traditional ice cream sales and delivers a high-ROI business solution for entrepreneurs and retailers.

Core Product Highlights

• 24/7 Unattended Operation: No labor cost, supports round-the-clock sales to maximize revenue potential in high-traffic areas.

• Ultra-Fast Production: 5-second rapid cooling and 15-second cup dispensing, 12-hour continuous output of up to 1373 cups without downtime.

• Space-Saving Design: Only 0.91㎡ floor space, lightweight 320KG with movable casters, easy to deploy in any commercial location.

• Rich Flavor Options: 1 base milk + 3 jams + 3 toppings create 59 unique flavors, 43% expansion rate with no ice residue for premium taste.

• Intelligent Management: APP remote monitoring for sales data, inventory, and device status; one-key self-cleaning reduces maintenance effort.

• Hygienic & Safe: UV sterilization + pasteurization, 2-6℃ antibacterial storage, contact-free production meets food safety standards.

Pain Points We Solve

- High labor costs of traditional ice cream stands: 100% unmanned operation cuts staffing expenses to zero.

- Limited business hours: 24/7 operation captures night-time and off-peak sales opportunities.

- Slow production speed: 15s fast dispensing improves customer experience and throughput.

- Complex cleaning process: One-key self-cleaning reduces maintenance time by 80%.

- Space constraints: Compact design fits small commercial areas like convenience stores and kiosks.

Application Scenarios & ROI Model

Best For: Shopping malls, tourist attractions, universities, airports, train stations, commercial streets, and convenience stores.

Profit Analysis: Cost per cup (raw materials) ≈ $0.21-$0.25; selling price ≈ $0.83-$1.38; gross profit margin >65%.

Payback Period: 200 cups sold daily in prime locations → monthly net profit ≈ $1,650 → full investment recovered in 3-4 months.

Additional Revenue: In-screen advertising and branded cup sponsorships boost extra income by 15-20%.
