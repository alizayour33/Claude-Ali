Huaxin Intelligent Ice Cream Vending Machine Operation Video
Date：2025-09-02 Author：Huaxin

The Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine features a 32-inch touchscreen supporting 14+ languages, allowing users to create 59 flavor combinations with 3 toppings and 3 ingredients. It produces up to 160 cups per full ingredient tank and holds 240 cups in stock. Equipped with a mini-program for remote management, sales tracking, and alerts for low supplies, it supports multi-machine management and self-cleaning. Simply add water and press "Clean" for automated maintenance. Ideal for efficient, high-profit automated ice cream sales.

Thank you for inquiring about the Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine. This guide will provide you with a detailed explanation of how to use the machine, helping you achieve efficient and standardized daily operations and maximize profits with our ice cream vending machine. Step up to the 32-inch ordering screen, where you can quickly switch languages. In the settings, you can select from more than 14 languages. Click the "START" button to begin the production process. Our machine supports 3 types of toppings and 3 types of ingredients, allowing for 59 DIY flavor combinations. We also offer popular classic flavor pairings for your selection. Next, you can choose the number of ice cream cups you want to make. Finally, select your preferred payment method, and you can watch the entire production process through the visual window. (Show the ice cream making process) A fully filled ingredient tank can produce 160 ice cream cups, and the cup dispenser can store up to 240 cups. We provide a mini-program that enables you to remotely control the machine and check sales revenue. You will receive alerts when ingredients or cups are running low. You can adjust the prices of ice cream and various toppings on both the large screen and the mini-program. Additionally, you can bind multiple machines to manage them centrally. The Huaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine supports self-cleaning. Simply pour a bucket of water into the ingredient tank and click the "Clean" button to complete the cleaning process.
