Product Description.

The Future of Automated Soft Serve Retail

The HuaXin B83 and B83 Max series represent the pinnacle of automated dessert technology. As an industry pioneer with 13 years of experience, HuaXin has developed a robotic kiosk that bridges the gap between traditional ice cream parlors and high-efficiency vending. This system is designed for high-traffic environments such as shopping malls, airports, and tourist attractions, providing a premium consumer experience without the need for manual labor.

Solving Traditional Retail Challenges
Eliminate Labor Costs: Traditional ice cream service requires constant staffing. The B83 operates 24/7 with zero human intervention, saving on training, wages, and management.
Optimize Space Productivity: With a tiny footprint of only 0.91m², this machine fits into "gold-mine" locations where traditional kiosks are too large to operate.
Automated Hygiene: Unlike traditional machines requiring daily manual teardowns, our system features one-touch self-cleaning and a Pasteurization system that keeps the product fresh and safe for longer periods.
Core Product Advantages
Master OS Intelligent System

Equipped with a 32-inch BOE touchscreen, the Master OS allows users to track sales, monitor raw material levels, and adjust prices remotely via a mobile app. 95% of technical issues can be diagnosed and resolved through our cloud-based system.

Featuring a high-performance Embraco compressor and a German-imported condenser fan, the machine reaches the ideal freezing temperature in just 5 seconds, ensuring a smooth, ice-free texture with a 43% expansion rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many flavors can the machine produce?

A: By combining one base milk liquid with 3 types of jams and 3 types of toppings, you can offer up to 59 different flavor combinations to your customers.

Q: How often does the machine need cleaning?

A: The machine features a one-touch self-cleaning function. Thanks to the low-temperature preservation system (2-6°C) and Pasteurization, we recommend a full clean once or twice per week.

Q: Can the machine support local payment systems?

A: Yes. We support customization for various payment methods including credit cards, QR codes, coins, and banknotes for over 33 countries.
