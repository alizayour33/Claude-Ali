dHuaxin Ice Cream Vending Machine at Asia Parks and Attractions Expo
Date：2025-05-12 Author：Huaxin

The hot-selling ice cream vending machine has efficient operation to help merchants maximize revenue. With the function of saving energy. the ice cream vending machine reduce the cost of maintenance. Huaxin ice cream vending machines obtained the high reputation at AAA Expo.And the tea-flavor ice cream made by our vending machine is very popular in the exhibition.

On May 10th, the intelligent ice cream manufacturer Huaxin Co., Ltd. landed strongly at the 2025 Asia Amusement & Attractions Expo with its core product B83Max smart ice cream vending machine. As Asia’s largest amusement and attractions industry event, this exhibition brings together the world’s leading companies and innovative technologies. However, Huaxin Technology, with its core products and technological innovation, became the focus of the scene, attracting the attention of industry experts, investors and partners from around the world.

  2025 Asia Amusement & Attractions Expo is one of the largest professional exhibitions in Asia for the cultural and tourism industry, which is jointly organized by China Culture & Entertainment Industry Association and China Tourist Attractions Association, and undertaken by Guangdong Hongwei International Exhibition. This exhibition focuses on “technology-enabled culture and tourism”, covering indoor parks, theme parks, scenic spots, cultural tourism, digital sports, VR/AR technology and other fields. The exhibition aims to promote the integration of the culture and tourism industry with cutting-edge technology, and to help enterprises grasp the global trend of intelligent and digital upgrading. Intelligent soft serve ice cream vending machines made by Huaxin completely comply with the theme of the exhibition.

 

Receive Wide Attention for Their Technical StrengthsHuaxin Ice Cream Vending Machines  

  In this exhibition, Huaxin ice cream vending machine responded to the theme of the exhibition, “Intelligent Terminal Equipment Drives New Scene of Culture and Tourism”. The ice cream vending machine is suitable for high-traffic cultural tourism scenes and has the characteristics of intelligent devices.

  First of all, in terms of efficient operation, the ice cream vending machine realizes all-weather unmanned sales, and through the precise refrigeration and production system. It takes only fifteen seconds on average to complete the production and delivery of a single ice cream, which greatly improves the sales efficiency. At the same time, the machine can monitor inventory and sales in real time, which can facilitate timely replenishment and operational decision-making and help merchants maximize revenue. Secondly, with the help of LOT, the ice cream vending machine supports remote management and data synchronization, and the operator can remotely monitor the running status of the equipment through the backstage platform. It also carry out troubleshooting and push software updates to reduce the cost of manual maintenance and improve the level of management intelligence. In terms of energy saving and environmental protection, the ice cream machine adopts an energy-efficient compressor refrigeration system, and is equipped with automatic dormancy and timing switch functions, which can effectively reduce energy consumption. In addition, the use of environmentally friendly refrigerant and recyclable materials, complying with the concept of green environmental protection. The ice cream machine can help sustainable development. Finally, the multi-language interactive system supports Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean and other languages, with a friendly user interface and easy operation, making it easy for global tourists and users of different languages to make purchases. This condition enhances user satisfaction and the brand’s international image. The ice cream vending machine supports 12 languages to meet the needs of internationalization scenarios.

 

  New product tea-flavored  ice cream vending machine successfully launched
  Oritea Gather tea-flavored ice cream vending machine is an innovative product that combines traditional oriental tea culture with modern intelligent technology. This machine has attracted widespread attention for its unique flavor experience and convenient operation. The vending machine is capable of making two classic Chinese tea-flavored ice creams, Dahongpao Oolong Tea and Xihu Longjing Tea, perfectly blending the mellow aroma of tea with the silky smoothness of the ice cream, bringing a refreshing taste enjoyment.

  The tea-flavored ice cream vending machine made a big splash at AAA Expo, making and serving more than 30,000 cups of ice cream for free in three days, making it one of the most popular exhibits at the venue. After tasting the ice cream, many visitors commented that the tea-flavored ice cream is unique and different from ordinary desserts on the market, and represents a cultural experience. The long queues and enthusiastic response at the site confirmed the great attraction of traditional tea culture through the carrier of modern technology.

  Huaxin brand is committed to promoting Chinese tea culture “going out”, and plans to put tea-flavored ice cream vending machines in Chinatown, Asian-style neighborhoods and popular tourist cities around the world. With this novel, pro-people way, let more overseas friends understand and fall in love with Chinese tea culture, truly realize the “flavor is culture” concept of communication. In addition, the multi-language operation interface of the ice cream vending machine supports multiple languages, which can easily meet the needs of diversified people.

  Tea flavor ice cream vending machine is not only a product, but also a carrier of cultural dissemination. It uses ice cream as a medium, links the world with the sense of taste, makes a cup of tea refreshing, and builds a sweet bridge of cultural exchange on a global scale. In the future, the combination of tea culture and intelligent technology will continue to expand the boundaries and inspire more cross-cultural inspiration and possibilities.
