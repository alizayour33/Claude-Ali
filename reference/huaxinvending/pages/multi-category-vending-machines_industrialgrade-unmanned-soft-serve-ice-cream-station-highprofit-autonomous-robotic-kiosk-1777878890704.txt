Product Description.

The Pinnacle of Unmanned Frozen Retail Engineering

The Huaxin B86max is not just a vending machine; it is an industrial-grade robotic employee. Backed by 13 years of technical evolution, it solves the most critical barriers in the soft-serve industry: consistency, sanitation, and labor costs.

RK Industrial Logic

Utilizes the proprietary RK System industrial controller. Unlike consumer-grade boards, this ensures 24/7 stability and handles complex Omron sensor inputs with zero-latency for precision cup-to-nozzle alignment.

Air-Pump Overrun Mastery

Integrated gear pumps achieve a precise 43% overrun. This increases serving volume by nearly half while creating a silky, no-ice-crystal texture that rivals top-tier ice cream franchises.

5.4sqm Cooling Matrix

Oversized copper condenser and German-imported fans provide double the cooling area of competitors, allowing for "Marathon Frost" capability—continuous output even in 40 degree Celsius ambient heat.

Franchise-Scale Operational Solutions
Eliminating Labor Overhead

Traditional soft-serve retail requires 2-3 staff members per shift. The B86max automates cup dispensing, flavor mixing (59 variations), and payment, reducing labor costs by 100%. One manager can remotely oversee 10-20 units via the Master OS App.

Automated Hygiene Maintenance

Cleaning is the industry's biggest pain point. Our system features a dual UV + Pasteurization cycle. Ingredients stay fresh in the refrigerated hopper (2-6 Celsius) for extended periods, and the self-cleaning mode is triggered with a single tap on the mobile App.

Structured FAQ for Global Operators

Q: How does the machine handle varied payment integrations?

A: Huaxin B86max supports seamless MDB/Executive protocols, integrating with Nayax, Ingenico, and regional QR gateways. We support Credit Cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and localized bill/coin acceptors across 33+ countries.

Q: What is the maintenance frequency for the 43% overrun system?

A: The air-pump system is self-maintained through the automated "One-Click Self-Cleaning" cycle. We recommend a full manual inspection only once every quarter to ensure gasket integrity.

Q: Does the 32-inch screen support third-party advertising?

A: Yes. The Master OS includes a dedicated CMS for content scheduling. You can upload videos and images remotely to run ads, creating a secondary revenue stream for your business.

Q: Is the machine compatible with non-dairy and plant-based mixes?

A: Absolutely. The frequency conversion system allows you to adjust cylinder temperature and overrun ratios to perfectly suit vegan, keto, or traditional dairy recipes.
